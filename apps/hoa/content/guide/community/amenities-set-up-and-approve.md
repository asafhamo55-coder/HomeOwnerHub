Amenities are the shared places residents can book, like the clubhouse, the pool pavilion or a tennis court. You set the hours and the rules once. Residents then pick a day and a time, and two bookings can never overlap.

## Before you start
- Board members and admins can set up amenities.
- Residents need a home linked to their account to book. See [invite owners](guide:members-invite-owners).

## Steps
### Add a space
1. Open **Amenities** in the sidebar and select [[Add an amenity]]. A form opens on its own page. If you manage more than one community, pick **Which community is this for?** under **The basics**.
2. Under **The basics**, type the **Name**, for example "Clubhouse".
3. Under **When it's open**, choose **Same hours every day** and set **Opens at** and **Closes at**. For different hours on some days, choose **Different hours on different days** and set each day.
4. Under **Booking rules**, choose **The board approves each booking** to answer each request yourself, or **Confirmed right away**. Then set **Longest booking, in hours** and **Book how many days ahead**.
5. To change the other limits, select [[Show more rules]]. They are **Bookings per home**, **People at once**, **Bookings at the same time**, **Minimum notice, in hours** and **Cancel cutoff, in hours**. The usual numbers are already filled in.
6. Optional: under **Closed dates**, select [[Add a closed date]] for days the space can't be booked, like winter for a pool. Under **Rules for residents**, write cleanup, guests, noise and anything else you require. Residents must tick **I read the house rules** before they can book.
7. Select [[Add amenity]]. You return to **Amenities** and the space shows under **Your amenities** with a short line of its rules.

### Answer a request or hold a time
1. A request shows under **Waiting for your answer** and rings your bell. You can also answer it in [Approvals](guide:approvals-queue) with [[Approve booking]].
2. Select [[Approve]] to confirm it. Select [[Turn down]], write why in **Why is it not approved?** and send. The resident is emailed your answer.
3. To block a time for repairs or a board meeting, select [[Hold a time]]. Pick the space, **Day**, **From**, **Until** and **What is it for?**, then select [[Hold this time]]. Residents see it as taken.
4. To cancel a confirmed booking, find it under **Coming up** and select [[Cancel booking]]. Give a reason. To drop a hold, select [[Remove hold]].

## What happens next
- A booking that needs no approval is confirmed at once and never reaches the queue.
- The calendar on the **Amenities** page shows booked days in green, requests waiting for you in yellow and closed days in grey. Tap a day to see who booked.
- Residents are emailed when you approve, turn down or cancel, unless they switched email off.

## If something goes wrong
- Approving says the time is taken: someone else got it first. Nothing changed.
- You changed the hours and bookings are affected: the app tells you how many. Choose **Keep them as they are** or **Cancel them and tell each resident**, then select [[Save changes]] again.
- The **Amenities** page says it is almost ready: it has not been switched on for your account yet.

## Good to know
- To pause new bookings for a while, select [[Edit]] next to the space, then untick **Residents can book this now** under **Open for booking**, and select [[Save changes]]. Existing bookings stay.
- To remove a space for good, select [[Retire]]. You choose whether to cancel its upcoming bookings and tell each resident. Its history is kept.
- Residents can cancel on their own until the number of hours you set. After that they have to ask you.
