The community board is where residents talk with each other. Only the residents and the board of your community can read it. Board members and admins can post too, and they can hide posts and close topics.

## Before you start
- Residents reach the board from **Community**, then **Talk with neighbors** on their phone.
- Board posts are marked "(board)" after the name. Residents are shown by first name and last initial.

## Steps
### Check for reported posts
1. Open **Community board** in the sidebar. A **Reported posts** box at the top lists each report with what the resident wrote.
2. Read the report. Select [[Hide the post and close the report]] if the post does not belong.
3. Or select [[Keep the post]] if it is fine. The report is closed.

### Hide a post or close a topic
1. Open **Community board** and select the topic.
2. Under a post, select [[Hide this post]]. Residents no longer see it. You still see it, marked "Hidden from residents". Select [[Show this post again]] to undo.
3. At the top of the topic, select [[Close this topic]] to stop new replies. Residents can still read it. Select [[Open this topic again]] to allow replies.

## What happens next
- A hidden post is gone from the neighbors' view right away.
- A closed topic tells residents "The board closed this topic. You can still read it."

## If something goes wrong
- "We could not find this topic. It may have been removed.": go back to **Community board** and pick it again.
- A button shows an error toast: nothing changed. Try again.

## Good to know
- Residents can't delete or edit their posts. Hiding is how you remove one.
- If you manage more than one community, pick the community at the top of the page.
- Use the board for a friendly tone. For a notice the whole community must see, send an [announcement](guide:communications-community-announcement).
