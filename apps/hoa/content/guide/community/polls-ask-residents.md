A poll is a quick question for the homes in your community, like "Should the pool open on Saturdays?" Each home answers once and you see how many homes picked each answer. A poll is informal. It is not an official vote and it does not bind the board.

## Before you start
- Board members and admins can make polls.
- Residents are not emailed or texted when a poll opens. Tell them with a [community announcement](guide:communications-community-announcement).

## Steps
### Write and open a poll
1. Open **Polls** in the sidebar and select [[New poll]].
2. Under **Your question**, write the question. Add **More detail (optional)** if it helps.
3. Under **Answers, one on each line**, type each answer on its own line. Use between 2 and 20 answers.
4. Choose whether a home can pick **One answer** or **As many as they like**.
5. Choose when residents see the results: **After the poll closes** or **Right away, as homes answer**. You can always see them.
6. Optional: pick a **Last day to answer (optional)**. The poll closes by itself on that day.
7. Select [[Save as draft]]. Nothing is sent yet. Read it over, then select [[Open for answers]].

### Close a poll and read the results
1. Open **Polls** and select the poll.
2. Read the results. Each answer shows how many homes picked it and the percent.
3. When you have heard enough, select [[Close the poll]] and confirm. Residents can no longer answer or change an answer.

## What happens next
- Residents find the poll under **Community**, then **Polls**.
- Answers are counted by home. The board never sees who in the home answered.
- If you chose **After the poll closes**, residents see the results once the poll is closed. A poll with a last day closes on its own.

## If something goes wrong
- The form asks for more answers: use between 2 and 20, one on each line.
- You made a mistake in a draft: select [[Delete draft]], confirm, and write it again.

## Good to know
- You can't edit a poll after you save it, and a closed poll can't be opened again. Start a new poll instead.
- Only a draft can be deleted. An open or closed poll stays on the list.
- Every poll page says it is informal. For a real board election, see [plan an election](guide:election-planner).
