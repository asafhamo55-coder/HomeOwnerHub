When an owner fixes a violation before its fine hearing, cancel the hearing and record why. In Florida and California the law says no fine once the violation is cured in time.

## Before you start
- The violation has a hearing that is **Requested by the owner** or **Scheduled**.
- Only board members and admins can cancel a hearing.

## Steps
1. Open **Violations** and select the violation.
2. Change its status to **Resolved**.
3. On the **Fine hearing** card, find **Cured before the hearing**. It shows what your state’s law says.
4. Under **Why the hearing is cancelled**, pick the reason, for example **The owner fixed the violation before the hearing**.
5. Select [[Cancel the hearing]].

## What happens next
- The hearing shows **Cancelled**.
- The activity log records **Cancelled a fine hearing after a cure**, with the reason as a code. It has no names or notes.
- A cancelled hearing doesn’t count for the fine check. In Florida and California, fining that violation later needs a new hearing.
- Nothing is sent to the owner. To let them know, send a message from **Messages**.

## If something goes wrong
- **Cured before the hearing** doesn’t appear: the violation isn’t **Resolved**, the hearing is already **Held** or **Cancelled**, or the violation was resolved after the hearing date.
- "That reason doesn’t apply in your state.": pick one of the reasons listed on the card.

## Good to know
- **Florida** (Fla. Stat. § 720.305(2)(e)): no fine or suspension if the violation is cured before the hearing, or cured the way the written notice asked.
- **California** (Civ. Code § 5855(c)): the owner may cure before the meeting. The board can’t impose discipline if the owner cured before the meeting, or, when the fix takes longer than the notice period, gave a financial commitment to cure.
- **Other states:** HomeownerHub has no loaded statute text on this. Check your governing documents, and ask your association’s attorney.
- General information from the statute text, not legal advice.
