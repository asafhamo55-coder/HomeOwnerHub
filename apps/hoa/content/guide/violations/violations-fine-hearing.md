Before the board fines an owner, many states require a hearing. Record it on the violation. In Florida and California, the violation can’t move to **Fined** until the hearing on record meets the statute.

## Steps
1. Open **Violations** and select the violation.
2. Find **Fine hearing**. If the owner asked for a hearing, their request and statement are at the top.
3. Pick the **Date**, the **Time** and the **Place, or how to join**.
4. Under **Hearing committee** (Florida) or **Hearing panel**, select [[Add a person]] for each person who will hear the case. Mark each one **Owner (not on the board)** or **Board**.
5. Select [[Draft hearing notice]]. The notice opens in **Messages**, addressed to that home. Fill in anything in brackets, then send it as usual.
6. Back on the violation, enter **Notice sent on** and select [[Record hearing]].
7. After the hearing, set **Hearing status** to **Held**. Pick the **Outcome**, and enter the day you sent the written decision and the day the fine is due. Select [[Save hearing]].

## What happens next
- The checklist on the card shows each step your state requires, what is done, and what is late.
- When you move the violation to **Fined**, the state law check reads the hearing. In Florida and California the fine is blocked until the record shows the notice sent at least 14 days (Florida) or 10 days (California) before the hearing, and the hearing **Held** with the **Outcome** recorded.
- In Florida the record must also show a committee of at least three people, none marked **Board** and none matching a board member or admin on your member list, and the hearing within 90 days of the notice.
- A hearing that did not approve the fine blocks it too.
- To go ahead anyway, the only reason you can pick is **The association’s attorney reviewed this and advised that it is permitted.** It is saved in the activity log as a code, with no names or notes.
- Raising a fine on a violation that is already fined is held to the same check. Lowering it is not.
- The morning email to the board lists hearing requests waiting more than 7 days to be scheduled, and hearing notices and written decisions due within 3 days or already late. It shows counts only.

## Good to know
- **Florida** (§ 720.305(2)): at least 14 days’ written notice; a committee of at least three people who are not officers, directors or employees, or their relatives; the hearing within 90 days of the notice; written findings within 7 days; the fine due at least 30 days after the findings.
- **California** (§ 5855): at least 10 days’ written notice of the board meeting; the owner may attend and address the board, or ask for executive session; the written decision within 14 days.
- **The board roster check** (Florida) compares each committee member’s name with the names of your board members and admins in **Settings**. It can’t see family ties, so check yourself that no committee member is the spouse, parent, child, brother or sister of an officer, director or employee. It also can’t see officers or employees who don’t have a board or admin login.
- The 7-day reminder for an owner’s hearing request is HomeownerHub’s own. The statute doesn’t set a day to schedule a request.
- **Cured before the hearing.** Florida (§ 720.305(2)(e)): no fine if the violation is cured before the hearing, or cured the way the notice asked. California (§ 5855(c)): the owner may cure before the meeting, and the board can't impose discipline once they have, or once they've made a financial commitment to cure something that takes longer.
- To cancel a hearing because the owner fixed the violation first, see **Cancel a hearing when the owner cures first** below, under related articles.
- A cancelled hearing doesn't count for the fine check. In Florida and California, fining that violation later needs a new hearing.
- Only board members and admins can cancel a hearing. The activity log keeps the reason as a code, with no names or notes.
- Other states: HomeownerHub has no loaded statute text for hearings, and no hearing blocks the fine. For a cure before the hearing too, check your governing documents.
- Nothing is sent to the owner from the hearing card. The notice goes out only when you send it from Messages.
- General information from the statute text, not legal advice.
