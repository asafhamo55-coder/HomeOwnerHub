Inspection mode is for a board member walking or driving the community with a phone. Each home you save becomes a draft violation letter. Nothing is filed or sent until the board approves it.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can inspect. Residents can't open this page.
- Covenant Brain finds the matching CC&R section only if your CC&Rs are uploaded. See [Upload your governing documents](guide:documents-governing-and-ask).

## Steps
1. Open **Violations** and select [[Inspect homes]], or go to `/inspect` on your phone.
2. Under **Home**, type the street or house number and pick the home.
3. Select [[Take photo]]. Your phone opens the camera. Add up to 10 photos for the home.
4. Under **What's wrong?**, pick the kind of issue, such as Lawn / landscaping or Parking / vehicles.
5. Add a short **Note** if it helps, such as "Boat trailer in the driveway".
6. Select [[Save for review]]. The form clears so you can go on to the next home.

## What happens next
Each saved home uploads its photos, and Covenant Brain drafts the letter. The draft goes to the [[AI approval queue]] tab under Violations and to the **Violation letters** tab in Approvals. When the board approves a draft, it's filed as an open violation with its photos. Nothing is sent to the owner.

## If something goes wrong
- No signal: keep going. Saved homes wait on your phone and upload when you're back online. The top of the page shows how many are waiting to upload. Select [[Retry now]] to try again straight away.
- "Needs attention": the upload was refused, for example a photo over 25 MB, or you were signed out. Read the reason, then select [[Retry]], or [[Remove]] to delete it from your phone.
- "Keep this page open": your browser can't store photos (a private window, for example). Stay on the page until everything has uploaded.
- A home isn't listed: it has no unit record yet. Ask an admin to check it under **Properties**.

## Good to know
- Photos must be JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC, under 25 MB each.
- Homes can't be sorted by distance from you. HomeownerHub doesn't store map locations for homes, so search by address instead.
- Waiting homes stay on your phone until they upload, even if you close the page. Open **Inspect homes** again to finish uploading them.
