When an owner asks for the membership roster, you usually have to provide it, but not everything the board keeps. The **records-request copy** is the version for handing over. It has owner names, property address, lot and mailing address. It never has phone numbers, ownership shares or balances.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can make the copy. Residents can't open the page.
- Record sales with [[Record a sale]] first, so the right owners are on the date you pick.
- In California, gather the written opt-outs your association has received. HomeownerHub doesn't record them yet.

## Steps
1. Open **Properties**, select the **⋯** menu next to [[Add property]], then [[Owner roster & voter list]].
2. Under **What to produce**, choose **Copy for an owner’s records request**.
3. Set **As of** to the day the copy should reflect, usually today.
4. Select [[Show roster]].
5. Read the notes above the table. In California, remove every member who opted out before you release the copy.
6. Select [[Download CSV]] for a spreadsheet, or [[Print / Save as PDF]] for paper or a PDF.

## What happens next
- Each home shows its owners on that date, the property address and lot, and the mailing address on file. When no other mailing address is on file, the property address is shown and labelled.
- Email addresses are left out in Florida, Texas, Georgia and every other state. Florida keeps owners' email addresses and phone numbers out of the records members may see. For Texas and Georgia, HomeownerHub doesn't hold the verified statute text yet, so it leaves email out to be safe.
- In California, the membership list can include email, but not for members who opted out. HomeownerHub doesn't store opt-outs yet, so no email is included, and the page reminds you to check your opt-outs.
- The guidance notes above the table don't print. The table and the law notes do.
- Each copy you show or download is logged with counts only: never a name, address or email.

## If something goes wrong
- "Couldn't record this export": the log couldn't be written, so nothing was shown. Try again.
- "No owner on record on this date": no ownership covers that day. Open the home and add the owner, or record the sale.
- An email column that says "Not shared": an email is on file, but your state's rules or a missing opt-out record keep it off this copy.

## Good to know
- The board's own [[Owner roster & voter list]] still has emails and phone numbers. Don't hand that version to an owner.
- The page quotes statutes as text. It isn't legal advice: check your governing documents and ask your association's attorney before releasing owner information.
