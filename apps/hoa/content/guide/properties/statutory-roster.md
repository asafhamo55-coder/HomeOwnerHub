The roster page builds two board documents from your homes and owners: the **Owner roster** (who owns each home, their shares and mailing address) and the **Voter list** (one vote per home, with the owners eligible on the date). Pick any date, then download a CSV or print the page.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can make a roster. Residents can't open the page.
- The roster reads the owners recorded on each home. Record sales with [[Record a sale]] so owners change on the right date.
- The owner roster holds owners' email addresses and phone numbers. It's for the board. It must not be shared with residents unless state law requires it. To answer an owner's records request, make the records-request copy instead (see "Give an owner a copy of the member roster").

## Steps
1. Open **Properties**, select the **⋯** menu next to [[Add property]], then [[Owner roster & voter list]]. Florida boards can also open it from the records check under **State law**.
2. Under **What to produce**, choose **Owner roster** or **Voter list**.
3. Set **As of**: the day, in your association's time zone. For a meeting, use the record date your bylaws set.
4. Select [[Show roster]].
5. Select [[Download CSV]] for a spreadsheet, or [[Print / Save as PDF]] for paper or a PDF.

## What happens next
- Owners are the ones on record on that date. On a settlement day the buyer is the owner, not the seller.
- Each home shows its owners with their share. A sole owner with no share recorded shows 100%.
- The mailing address is the one on file from an import or a sale. When there isn't one, the property address is shown and labelled as such.
- The voter list gives each home one vote. HomeownerHub doesn't store a voting rule, so if your governing documents allocate votes differently, use them instead.
- In Florida, a home more than 90 days past due on the date is flagged **May be suspended**. Nothing is suspended: a flagged home votes unless the board voted the suspension at a properly noticed meeting.
- Each roster you show or download is logged with counts only.

## If something goes wrong
- "That date does not exist. Showing today instead.": pick a real date and select [[Show roster]] again.
- "No owner on record on this date": no ownership covers that day. Open the home and add the owner, or record the sale.
- "Couldn't record this export": the log couldn't be written, so nothing was shown. Try again.

## Good to know
- A home whose owner was typed in before ownership history existed shows "From the home's record". Its dates aren't known, so it's today's owner whatever date you pick.
- California voter lists flag no homes. California's election rules don't provide for suspending a vote over unpaid dues the way Florida's do.
- The page quotes statutes as text. It isn't legal advice: check your governing documents and ask your association's attorney.
