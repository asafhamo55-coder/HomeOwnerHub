Owners often assume the association’s master policy covers their home. Send them a short notice about what it covers and what they should insure themselves. It’s optional.

## Before you start
- Record the **Master / property** policy on **Insurance**, with its limit and deductible.
- Have the declarations page to hand, so you can say what the policy covers.

## Steps
1. Open **Insurance** in the sidebar.
2. Find **Tell owners what they need to insure** and select [[Draft HO-6 notice]].
3. The notice opens in **Messages**, addressed to owners.
4. Fill in what the master policy covers, in brackets, from the declarations page.
5. Check the list of what it usually doesn’t cover against the policy.
6. Send it as usual, or close it.

## What happens next
Nothing is sent until you press **Send** in **Messages**. Owners who get it can also see the association’s policies in their portal under **Documents**, then **Association insurance**.

## Good to know
- The notice lists the insurance company, the limit, the deductible and the end date of the master policy in force. It never shows the premium, the policy number or your notes.
- With no master policy on record, those lines are left in brackets for you to fill in.
- It suggests owners ask their agent about an HO-6 policy (or HO-3 for a single-family home) and loss assessment coverage.
- Your governing documents decide what the association insures and what each owner insures. The notice says so, and quotes no statute.
- General information, not insurance or legal advice.
