Use this to see what your association pays for HomeownerHub, and to start or manage the subscription. This is your association's bill to us, not residents' dues.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can see the plan. Only admins can subscribe, change the plan or update the card. Board members see "Only admins can change the plan or payment method."
- The price is per door (home), with a minimum. **Doors on file** shows how many homes your account is billed for.

## Steps
1. In the sidebar, open [[Billing]].
2. Under **Current plan**, check your plan, **Doors on file**, and the **Monthly** and **Annual** totals.
3. Under **Choose a plan**, pick [[Monthly]] or [[Annual]]. Annual saves 10%.
4. Read the **First-year commitment** note.
5. Select the **Subscribe** button. It shows the price.
6. Enter your payment details on the secure Stripe checkout page. When you finish, you come back to **Billing**.

## What happens next
Your plan shows as current. An admin can then select [[Manage subscription]] to update the card or download invoices in Stripe.

## If something goes wrong
- **Couldn’t start checkout**: checkout didn't open, and you weren't charged. Try again. If it keeps happening, contact support.
- "Billing isn’t available right now": checkout is paused on our side, so you can’t start or change a subscription. Your current plan isn’t affected. Try again later, or contact support.
- **Doors on file** is wrong: contact support. You can't change it on this page.

## Good to know
- The first 12 months can't be cancelled. From month 13, you can cancel any time under **Manage subscription**.
- The free plan is temporary. It's for a small group of early partner associations and will end after that phase.
- Small associations pay the minimum. The page shows "(minimum)" when it applies.
- You pay HomeownerHub through Stripe's checkout page, not by check or Zelle.
- To keep a copy of your records, see [Download a full copy of your association's data](guide:settings-data-export).
