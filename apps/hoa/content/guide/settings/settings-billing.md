Use this to see what your association pays for HomeownerHub, and to start or manage the subscription. This is your association's bill to us, not residents' dues.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can see the plan. Only admins can subscribe, change the plan or update the card. Board members see "Only admins can change the plan or payment method."
- There is one plan, HomeownerHub, and everything is included: accounting, the shared mailbox, vendors and RFPs, leases, and the rest. There is nothing to upgrade to.
- The price depends on how many homes you have, in size bands. **Homes on file** shows how many homes your account is billed for. Add your homes under Properties first.

## Steps
1. In the sidebar, open [[Billing]].
2. Under **Current plan**, check your plan, **Homes on file**, and the **Monthly** and **Annual** totals.
3. Under **Choose a plan**, pick [[Monthly]] or [[Annual]]. Annual gives you 2 months free.
4. Select the button on the plan card. A new association sees "Start 30-day free trial". It needs no card to start. After 30 days, add a card to keep going.
5. Enter your details on the secure Stripe checkout page. When you finish, you come back to **Billing**.

## What happens next
Your plan shows as current. An admin can then select [[Manage subscription]] to update the card, download invoices or cancel in Stripe.

## If something goes wrong
- **Couldn’t start checkout**: checkout didn't open, and you weren't charged. Try again. If it keeps happening, contact support.
- "Billing isn’t available right now": checkout is paused on our side, so you can’t start or change a subscription. Your current plan isn’t affected. Try again later, or contact support.
- **Homes on file** is wrong: fix it under Properties. The price follows the homes you have.

## Good to know
- Billing is month to month. You can cancel any time under **Manage subscription**.
- If your home count moves into a different size band, the new price starts at your next renewal, with no partial charges.
- Associations that subscribed before the current pricing keep their price until their term ends, then move to the plan above at your size. We email the board 30 days before.
- You pay HomeownerHub through Stripe's checkout page, not by check or Zelle.
- To keep a copy of your records, see [Download a full copy of your association's data](guide:settings-data-export).
