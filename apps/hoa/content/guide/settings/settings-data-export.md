Use this when you want your own copy of everything, for example for a backup, an audit, a new board, or moving to other software. Your records belong to your association.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can download the data. Residents can't.
- The file holds owners' and residents' names, emails and phone numbers. Save it somewhere private.

## Steps
1. In the sidebar, open [[Your data]]. It's also a tab in **Settings**.
2. Read **What’s included** to see each area and its file name.
3. Select [[Download all data]]. The button shows "Preparing download…" while it works.
4. Your browser saves a zip file named **association-data-** plus today's date.
5. Open the zip. Open any CSV file in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

## What happens next
The zip has one CSV per area: units, properties list, owners, residents, assessments (dues charges), payments, journal entries, journal lines, violations, ARC requests, tickets, documents index, governing docs, vendors and meetings. It also has a README.txt. Each download is recorded in your association's audit log.

## If something goes wrong
- "The export could not be started. Check your connection and try again.": the request didn't reach the server. Check your internet and try again.
- "The download stopped before it finished, so no file was saved. Try again.": nothing partial was saved. Try again.
- If **Download failed** shows another message, the export was refused or failed before any data was sent. Try again, and contact support if it keeps happening.

## Good to know
- It's always the full set. You can't pick certain areas or a date range.
- Not included: the uploaded files themselves (the documents index lists them), bank and payment connections, sign-in details, and vendor tax ids.
- Nothing is stored on our side. The zip is built when you select the button.
- You can download as often as you like.
