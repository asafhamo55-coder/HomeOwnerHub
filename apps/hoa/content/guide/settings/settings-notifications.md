Use this to stop, or start again, the monthly board report email that comes to you.

## Steps
1. Open **Settings** from the sidebar. Board members can open **Members** instead.
2. Select the [[Notifications]] tab.
3. Under **Board emails**, flip the **Monthly board report** switch on or off.

## What happens next
Your choice saves right away. You see "Monthly board report turned off." or "You’ll get the monthly board report." The report comes on the 1st of each month. It covers last month's cash, collections, overdue dues, budget, violations and what's waiting on the board.

## If something goes wrong
- **Not saved**: the switch flips back. Try again. "You’re signed out. Sign in and try again." means your sign-in ran out.
- "Couldn’t load your email settings": refresh the page.

## Good to know
- The monthly board report is the only email you can switch off here. It is on unless you turn it off.
- Your choice applies to every association you're on the board of. It does not change anyone else's emails.
- Alerts that need action, like a disconnected mailbox, always come through.
- The **General** tab shows your profile and your association. Admins can change their name there and select [[Save name]]. Your sign-in email can't be changed there.
