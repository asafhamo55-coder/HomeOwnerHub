Use this to stop, or start again, the monthly board report email that comes to you. Board members can also choose whether residents are told when the board decides or answers their requests.

## Steps
1. Open **Settings** from the sidebar. Board members can open **Members** instead.
2. Select the [[Notifications]] tab.
3. Under **Board emails**, flip the **Monthly board report** switch on or off.
4. Under **Resident emails**, flip **Tell residents about decisions and replies** on or off.
5. Under **Resident portal**, flip **Resident assistant** on or off.

## What happens next
Each switch saves right away. You see "Monthly board report turned off." or "You’ll get the monthly board report." For residents, you see "Residents will be told when the board decides or replies." or "Resident decision and reply notices turned off." The report comes on the 1st of each month. It covers last month's cash, collections, overdue dues, budget, violations and what's waiting on the board.

With **Tell residents about decisions and replies** on, the resident who sent an ARC application gets an email when the board approves it, denies it or uses [[Request info]]. The email carries your letter or message. A resident who opened a support request (a ticket) gets an email each time the board replies to it, so they know you answered without logging in. Residents who opted in to texts also get a short text with a link to the portal. Each decision or reply is sent once.

With **Resident assistant** on, residents chat on their **Ask** tab. The assistant answers about the resident's own balance, requests, announcements and meetings, and about your rules with the sections cited. It never creates anything. It offers [[Ask the board]] and [[Start an ARC application]] buttons that open a filled-in form the resident sends themselves. Off, residents get Ask the Docs.

## If something goes wrong
- **Not saved**: the switch flips back. Try again. "You’re signed out. Sign in and try again." means your sign-in ran out.
- "Couldn’t load your email settings" or "Couldn’t load your resident email settings": refresh the page.
- "Resident notices are paused": the setting was changed outside this screen, so nothing sends. Turn it on again here.
- "Resident assistant is off": the setting was changed outside this screen, so residents see Ask the Docs. Turn it on again here.
- "Email isn’t set up on this server": you can turn the switch on, but no email goes out until email delivery is configured.

## Good to know
- **Monthly board report** is your own choice. It applies to every association you're on the board of and does not change anyone else's emails. It is on unless you turn it off.
- **Tell residents about decisions and replies** is one setting for the whole association, shared by every board member. It is off until a board turns it on.
- **Resident assistant** is one setting for the whole association. It is on unless a board turns it off.
- Turning it on doesn't send anything for decisions and replies made before. Only new ones are sent.
- If an email or text can't be sent, the board's decision or reply is still saved.
- Internal notes are never sent to residents. Residents who turned off email or texts in their preferences aren't contacted that way.
- Alerts that need action, like a disconnected mailbox, always come through.
- The **General** tab shows your profile and your association. Admins can change their name there and select [[Save name]]. Your sign-in email can't be changed there.
