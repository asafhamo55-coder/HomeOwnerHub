Keep every policy the association carries in one list, so a renewal never slips by and the board can answer "are we covered?" quickly.

## Before you start
- Have the declarations page of each policy to hand. It shows the insurance company, the policy number, the limit, the deductible and the dates.
- To link the policy itself, upload the PDF under **Documents** first.

## Steps
1. Open **Insurance** in the sidebar.
2. Select [[Add a policy]].
3. Pick the kind of policy, for example **Master / property**, **General liability**, **Directors & officers (D&O)** or **Fidelity / crime bond**.
4. Enter the insurance company and the date the policy ends. Add the policy number, limit, deductible, premium and start date if you have them.
5. Under **Policy document**, pick the PDF you uploaded, if any.
6. Select **Save policy**.

## What happens next
- The board gets a reminder 90, 60 and 30 days before a policy ends, on the dashboard and in the morning email. An expired policy stays on the reminder until you record the renewal.
- When you renew, add the new policy with its new dates. The reminder for the old one stops once the new one starts by the day after the old one ends.

## Good to know
- The **Coverage check** shows what your state's law, as loaded in HomeownerHub, says about the policies you recorded. It quotes the statute word for word and never blocks anything.
- **Florida:** Fla. Stat. § 720.3033(5) requires insurance or a fidelity bond for everyone who controls or spends the association's money, covering the most money held at any one time. The members can vote each year to waive it.
- **California:** Civ. Code § 5300(b)(9) asks the annual budget report to summarize the property, general liability, earthquake, flood and fidelity policies, with the insurer, the limit and the deductible. The check tells you which of these are missing. HomeownerHub doesn't have the text of § 5806 (the fidelity minimum), so for that, check your governing documents.
- **Other states:** the coverage check says to check your governing documents and ask your insurance agent.
- Only board members and admins see this page. Owners don't.
- Use **Notes** for exclusions or the broker's contact.
- This is general information from the statute text, not legal advice.
