Use this when someone leaves the board, for example after an election or a resignation. The checklist keeps the HOA's accounts and records with the association, not with one person.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can open the handover page. Residents can't.
- Have the successor's email ready, so you can invite them on **Members**.

## Steps
1. In the sidebar, open [[Board handover]]. It's also a tab in **Settings**.
2. Under **Who has access**, tick [[Leaving]] next to each member who is stepping down. This only filters the page. It doesn't remove anyone.
3. Read the **Handover checklist** at the top. Each line marked **To do** links to the screen that fixes it.
4. Under **Connections tied to one person**, select **Reconnect** or **Transfer** for each one set up by a leaving member.
5. Under **Open work**, reassign their inbox threads, action items and tickets.
6. Select [[Print handover summary]] to print the summary or save it as a PDF.
7. Download a full copy of the records from **Your data**, then invite the successor and remove the leaving member on **Members**.

## What happens next
The summary lists board members' names and roles, the status of each connection, open work counts, and the checklist. It has no emails, passwords or account numbers. Once the leaving member is removed, anything still set up under their login shows **Owner no longer on the board**.

## If something goes wrong
- A section says it couldn't be loaded: refresh the page. The checklist marks that section **Unknown** instead of **Done**.
- "Only an admin can reconnect this.": the HOA mailbox is managed by admins. Ask an admin to reconnect it.
- **Last sign-in** shows "Not recorded": that member has never signed in, or the date couldn't be read.

## Good to know
- Ticks under **Leaving** aren't saved. Reloading the page clears them.
- The Zelle details and the subscription billing email don't record who set them up. When anyone leaves, check they belong to the HOA, not a person.
- Residents are counted, not listed.
