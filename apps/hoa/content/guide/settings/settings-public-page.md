The public page is a small web page for your community that anyone can open, even without signing in. Nothing shows on it until you add it, and the page stays private until you turn it on.

## Before you start
- Board members and admins can build the page.
- Once the page is on, anyone on the internet can read it, and search engines can find it. Only add what you are happy to show everyone.
- Documents you can add are files already shared with owners on the owners' portal.

## Steps
1. Open **Settings**, then the **Public page** tab.
2. Under **1. About your community**, check the **Page title**. Pick a short **Web address**. Write a few sentences under **About**. Add a contact email or phone only if you want it shown to everyone. Select [[Save]].
3. Under **2. Documents**, select [[Add]] beside each file visitors may open.
4. Under **3. Notices**, select [[Use this]] beside a message you already sent, or write a new **Notice title** and **Notice text**. Read it and take out anything private. Select [[Add notice to the page]].
5. Look at **On the page now**. Select [[Remove]] beside anything you don't want there.
6. Under **4. Turn it on**, select [[Turn the page on]]. The link is shown there. Share it.

## What happens next
- The page shows your title, the about text, your notices, your documents as links, and the contact details you typed. Nothing else.
- To hide it again, select [[Turn the page off]]. Nobody can see it after that.

## If something goes wrong
- A notice is refused because it has an email address, a phone number, a dollar amount or a street address: take it out of the text and add it again.
- "That web address is taken. Pick another.": change the **Web address**. Use 3 to 40 letters, numbers or dashes, like "oak-hollow".
- "The public page isn't available yet.": it is not switched on for your account yet.

## Good to know
- Never put names, home addresses, balances or private documents on this page.
- Notices are a copy of the text. Changing the original message later does not change the page.
- The page never shows the resident portal, dues, violations, requests or who lives in which home.
