Use this to set the insurance coverage your association requires from vendors and on every RFP, and whether the board is notified before a vendor's certificate runs out.

## Before you start
- Board members and admins can change this.
- Until someone saves this page, no minimums apply and board notifications are on.

## Steps
1. Open **Vendor settings** in the Account section of the sidebar, or the **Vendors** tab in Settings.
2. Under **Insurance minimums**, enter the least coverage you accept for **Per occurrence** and **Aggregate** general liability, **Auto liability** and **Umbrella**, in dollars, without cents. For a $1 million minimum, type 1,000,000. Leave an amount blank for no minimum.
3. Tick **Require workers’ compensation** if every vendor must carry it.
4. Under **Insurance expiry reminders**, leave **Notify the board before vendor insurance expires** ticked, or untick it if your manager tracks renewals another way.
5. Select [[Save vendor settings]].

## What happens next
The compliance check marks a vendor **Non-compliant** when their certificate shows less than a minimum you set, and lists the gap with both amounts. New RFP drafts and every RFP invitation list your minimums, so vendors know them before they bid.

With reminders on, every board member and admin gets a notification in the bell 30 days before a vendor's certificate of insurance expires, again 7 days before, and on the day it expires. Each notification opens the vendor's page.

Nothing is emailed to a vendor automatically. To ask a vendor for a renewed certificate, open the vendor and select [[Request updated certificate]]. You review the email and send it yourself. See [Request an updated insurance certificate](guide:vendors-request-certificate).

## If something goes wrong
- "Check the highlighted fields.": an amount has cents or isn't a number, or the aggregate is less than the per-occurrence amount. Fix the field shown in red and save again.
- **Your saved vendor settings aren’t in use**: they were changed outside this screen, so the defaults apply and board reminders stay on. Check the page and save again.
- "Couldn’t save the vendor settings. Try again.": nothing changed. Save again.

## Good to know
- Turning reminders off stops the bell notifications only. Expiring and expired certificates still show on the dashboard and on each vendor's page.
- Notifications cover active and prospect vendors whose certificate date is entered on their compliance check. Enter a renewed certificate's new date and the notifications start again for that date.
- Minimums don't change a vendor's grade by themselves. A vendor is graded against them the next time their compliance is reviewed.
- An RFP's page shows the minimums from when it was drafted. Invitation emails always list your current minimums.
