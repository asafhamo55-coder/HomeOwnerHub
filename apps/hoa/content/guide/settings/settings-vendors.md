Use this to set the insurance coverage your association requires from vendors, and whether vendors are reminded to send a renewed certificate before theirs runs out.

## Before you start
- Board members and admins can change this.
- Until someone saves this page, no minimums apply and insurance reminders are on.

## Steps
1. Open **Vendor settings** in the Account section of the sidebar, or the **Vendors** tab in Settings.
2. Under **Insurance minimums**, enter the least coverage you accept for **Per occurrence** and **Aggregate** general liability and for **Auto liability**, in dollars, without cents. Leave an amount blank for no minimum.
3. Tick **Require workers’ compensation** if every vendor must carry it.
4. Under **Insurance expiry reminders**, leave **Email vendors before their insurance expires** ticked, or untick it if your manager collects renewals another way.
5. Select [[Save vendor settings]].

## What happens next
The compliance check marks a vendor **Non-compliant** when their certificate shows less than a minimum you set, and lists the gap with both amounts. RFP drafts and RFP invitations from then on list your minimums, so vendors know them before they bid.

With reminders on, a vendor gets an email 30 days before their certificate of insurance expires, again 7 days before, and on the day it expires. Each one asks them to reply with a renewed certificate, and lists your minimums. Each vendor gets each email once.

## If something goes wrong
- "Check the highlighted fields.": an amount has cents or isn't a number, or the aggregate is less than the per-occurrence amount. Fix the field shown in red and save again.
- **Your saved vendor settings aren’t in use**: they were changed outside this screen, so the defaults apply and reminders stay on. Check the page and save again.
- "Couldn’t save the vendor settings. Try again.": nothing changed. Save again.

## Good to know
- Reminders go only to the vendor's primary email. Residents and owners never get them.
- The board gets a notification in the bell for each reminder, even when reminders to vendors are off or a vendor has no email on file.
- A reminder goes only to active and prospect vendors whose certificate is entered on their compliance check. Enter a renewed certificate's new date and the reminders start again for that date.
- Minimums don't change a vendor's grade by themselves. A vendor is graded against them the next time their compliance is reviewed.
