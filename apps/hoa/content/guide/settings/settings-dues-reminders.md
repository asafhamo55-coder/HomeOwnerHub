Use this so owners who owe dues get a friendly email on the days you choose, without anyone sending it by hand.

## Before you start
- Board members and admins can change this.
- Reminders are off until someone turns them on and saves.

## Steps
1. Open **Dues reminders** in the Account section of the sidebar.
2. Tick **Send automatic dues reminders**.
3. Under **When to send**, set each reminder's days and pick before due, on the due date or after due. Select [[Add a reminder]] for another one.
4. Add a **Message to owners** if you like, such as how to pay or who to call.
5. Select [[Preview next run]] to see who would get a reminder next time. Nothing is sent.
6. Select [[Approve and save]].

## What happens next
The page shows **On — sending daily at 9am Eastern** and when it was approved. Each day, owners with a balance whose reminder day has come get one email. It shows their own charges and balance. **What owners receive** shows a sample.

## If something goes wrong
- **Automatic reminders are paused**: the saved settings were changed outside this screen, so nothing will send. Check them and save again.
- **Email isn’t set up on this server**: reminders are saved, but none go out until email delivery is set up. Contact support.
- "Check the highlighted fields.": fix the reminder days or message shown in red, then save.
- "Couldn’t save the reminder settings. Try again.": nothing changed. Save again.

## Good to know
- You can have up to 5 reminders, from 30 days before to 90 days after the due date. The message can be up to 500 characters.
- Saving approves the schedule and message in your name. Any later change is a new approval.
- Owners on an active payment plan, and accounts with the association's attorney, never get an automatic reminder. Owners with no email on file are skipped too.
- Reminders are email only. They are not sent by text.
- To stop them, untick **Send automatic dues reminders** and select [[Save]].
