An owner who pays more than they owe is not turned away. The whole payment is recorded, the charges are paid, and the extra stays on their account as credit until you apply it.

## Before you start
- Your books must be open. If they are not, HomeownerHub tells you.
- Credit is shown as its own line. It never reduces a balance by itself.

## Steps
1. On **Dues**, select [[Record payment…]] for the home.
2. Enter the amount received, how it was paid, the day you received it, and an optional note such as a check number.
3. Select **Show the split**. The charges it pays are listed, and anything beyond what is due is shown as credit.
4. Select **Record**. Every part is saved together, or none is.
5. To use the credit later, open the home and select **Apply credit…**. Choose the charge and the amount.

## What happens next
- The credit appears on the home's page and on the owner's ledger as **Unapplied credit**.
- Estoppel certificates and collection letters show the total due before credit, the credit, and the total due after credit.
- Applying credit moves it from Prepaid Assessments to Accounts Receivable. It never goes beyond what the credit or the charge has left.

## If something goes wrong
- You get "The home's charges changed since you previewed the split": nothing was saved. Preview again.
- You get "That is more than the credit or the charge has left": lower the amount.

## Good to know
- The date received is the association's day. Leave it blank for today.
- Notes stay in the books. They are not copied into the activity log.
