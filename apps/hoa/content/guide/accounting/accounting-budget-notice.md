Once the board adopts next year's budget, owners need a copy. HomeownerHub writes the notice from the adopted budget. You fill in what the app doesn't know, check it, and then print it or send it as an announcement.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can prepare the notice. Residents can't open the page.
- The budget must be approved. A draft budget doesn't offer the notice.
- Bill the year's dues first if you can. The per-home amount then comes from what each home is actually billed.

## Steps
1. Open **Accounting**, then **Budgets**, and open the approved budget.
2. Select [[Prepare owner budget notice]].
3. Under **Fill in the blanks**, enter each item the app doesn't hold. Every notice asks for the reserve accounts and this year's contributions. Florida also asks for the estimated surplus or deficit at the end of the current year and the fees the association pays for recreational amenities. California also asks for the board statements in § 5300(b)(4), (5), (6) and (8).
4. Read the notice to the bottom. Anything still missing shows as a highlighted **BLANK**.
5. Tick [[I’ve checked these figures]].
6. To print, select [[Print / Save as PDF]]. Choose your printer, or **Save as PDF** to send the file yourself.
7. To send it, select [[Create announcement draft]]. **New message** opens with the notice as the subject and message, addressed to owners. Check the audience, read the message and send it the usual way.

## What happens next
- The notice lists every budget category under **Income** and **Expenses**, with total income, total expenses and income less expenses.
- The per-home assessment comes from the dues billed for the budget year when every home is billed the same amount. Before dues are billed, it's the budgeted assessment income (account 4000) shared equally across the homes you bill. If neither works, it's a BLANK for you to fill in.
- In Florida, the notice adds the lines § 720.303(6)(a) asks for and says a copy of the budget is available to any member on request at no charge.
- In California, the notice is labelled **Partial — §5300 requires items not generated here**. It covers the pro forma operating budget and the board statements, and lists what else the annual budget report must include. That includes the reserve summary, the insurance summary and the § 5570 disclosure form. Send those with it. The page also shows the § 5300(a) window: 30 to 90 days before the fiscal year ends.
- In other states, the notice makes no statutory claims.
- Nothing is sent from the notice page. An announcement draft goes out only when you send it from **New message**, with the same checks as any other message.

## If something goes wrong
- [[Print / Save as PDF]] and [[Create announcement draft]] are greyed out: a BLANK is still empty, or [[I’ve checked these figures]] isn't ticked. The message under the buttons says which.
- "This budget isn’t adopted yet": approve the budget first, then come back.
- The per-home assessment is a BLANK: homes are billed different amounts, only some homes are billed so far, or the billed amount doesn't match the budget. The hint under the field says which. Enter the amount from the board's assessment resolution.
- "The budget notice didn’t come through" in **New message**: the browser didn't keep the draft, usually in a private window or a new tab. Go back to the notice and select [[Create announcement draft]] again in the same tab.

## Good to know
- HomeownerHub has no reserve module, so reserve figures are always yours to fill in.
- In Florida, assessments are shared as your governing documents say (§ 720.308(1)(a)). An equal share is only a starting point, so check it against your declaration.
- In California, § 5320 requires individual delivery of the report or its summary. If **New message** offers [[This is an official notice]], tick it.
- The page quotes statutes as text. It isn't legal advice: check your governing documents and ask your association's attorney.
