Each draft or approved budget has an **Adopt this budget** checklist. It walks the board from scheduling the meeting to billing the new dues, and shows your state's deadlines along the way. Nothing on the checklist is sent to anyone.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members see the checklist. Residents don't.
- The budget needs a fiscal period, so the app knows when the budget year starts.
- The deadlines come from Florida and California statute text. In other states, check your governing documents.

## Steps
1. Open **Accounting**, then **Budgets**, and open the budget. The checklist is at the top.
2. Select [[Schedule the adoption meeting]]. The agenda is filled in to say the budget and its assessments will be considered. Pick the date and select [[Schedule meeting]]. The meeting is linked to the budget. If the meeting is already scheduled, pick it from the list and select [[Link meeting]].
3. Select [[Write the meeting notice]]. **New message** opens with the meeting notice filled in from the agenda. Check it and send it the usual way. In Florida, tick **Special assessments will be considered at this meeting** if they will be.
4. After the board votes, select **Approve** on the budget.
5. Under **Record the adoption**, enter the date the board adopted it and select [[Record adoption]].
6. Select [[Prepare owner budget notice]] to send owners the budget. When it has gone out, enter the date and select [[Mark as sent]].
7. Select [[Bill dues]] to bill next year's dues at the new amount.

## What happens next
- Each step shows **Done**, **To do**, **Check** or **Waiting**. **Check** means something doesn't match the rule, and the step says what.
- The checklist shows one date to watch, with a countdown.
- In California, that's when the § 5300(a) window opens or closes. The annual budget report goes out 30 to 90 days before the fiscal year ends. For a calendar year, that's October 2 to December 1.
- In Florida, once a meeting is linked, it's the last day to send the meeting notice: 7 days before the meeting if mailed or delivered. If special assessments will be considered, it's 14 days, and the notice must also be posted on the property.
- Otherwise, it's the day the budget year starts.
- During budget season, the dashboard shows a **Budget season** card for each budget still being adopted, with its next step and date. The season runs from 120 days before the budget year until 30 days into it.

## If something goes wrong
- "The meeting's agenda doesn't say assessments will be considered": in Florida, an assessment can't be levied unless the meeting notice says assessments will be considered and what they are. Edit the meeting's agenda before you write the notice.
- "Notice went out … later than …": the notice was sent after the deadline. In Florida, a notice sent at least 48 hours ahead still counts if it was also posted in a conspicuous place in the community. That doesn't apply when special assessments are considered.
- "Saved progress couldn't be verified": the saved checklist wasn't written by the checklist itself, so the app ignores it. Save each step again.
- **Record adoption** says "Approve the budget first": approve the budget, then record the date.

## Good to know
- The meeting notice comes from the meeting's agenda. Edit the agenda to change what the notice says.
- California's board-meeting notice period isn't loaded in HomeownerHub, so the checklist doesn't count it. Check your governing documents.
- Until dues rates can be saved, you enter the amount each time you bill dues.
- The checklist quotes statutes as text. It isn't legal advice. Check your governing documents and ask your association's attorney.
