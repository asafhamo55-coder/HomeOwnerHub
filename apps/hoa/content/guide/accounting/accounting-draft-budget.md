Use this when the treasurer or board starts next year's budget. Instead of a blank sheet, every line starts from what was actually collected or spent, with the reason written next to it.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can draft and save a budget.
- The draft is for one fund at a time. Start with the operating fund.
- To save it, next year must be open in the books as a fiscal period. Until it is, you can review the draft but not save it. An HOA admin can open it in one step (below).

## Steps
1. Open **Accounting**, then **Budgets**, and select [[Draft next year’s budget]].
2. If the page says next year "isn't open in the books yet", an HOA admin selects [[Open next fiscal year]], then confirms. You can also do this from **Accounting**, then **Fiscal periods**. Your current year stays the current year until next year starts, so dues, bills and recurring entries keep posting where they do today. Board members see who to ask instead.
3. Check the **Fund** and the **Budget year**. The page shows which budget it started from and the dates of the actuals it used.
4. Set the **Inflation adjustment**. It's 3% unless you change it, and it applies to every line.
5. Read each line. Under the account name is the reason for its suggestion, for example "Last year budget $24,000, actual $26,100, +3%". Type a different amount wherever the board wants one. To go back to the suggestion, select [[Use suggestion]].
6. Optional: under **Main changes vs last year**, select [[Write a summary with AI]] for a short paragraph to put in the board packet.
7. Select [[Save as draft budget]], check the totals, then select [[Save draft]].

## What happens next
The budget opens as a **Draft**, and each line keeps its reason as a note. Nothing is approved or posted. The board can keep editing it on the budget page and approves it there when it's adopted.

## If something goes wrong
- "isn't open in the books yet": next year has no fiscal period, so there's nowhere to save the draft. An HOA admin selects [[Open next fiscal year]] (step 2), and Save turns on. Selecting it again does nothing; it says the year is already open.
- "Your fiscal periods aren't monthly, quarterly or yearly": the app can't tell how to add next year to your books. Contact support to add it.
- "Nothing to start from yet": there's no earlier budget for this fund and no posted activity in the last 12 months, so every line starts at $0. Enter the amounts yourself; it still saves as a draft.
- "A budget for that period and fund already exists": that year already has a budget for this fund. Open it from **Budgets** instead, or choose a different year.
- "The draft budget was created, but its lines didn't save": select [[Save as draft budget]] again. It finishes the same draft rather than creating a second one.

## Good to know
- Each suggestion uses the actual activity for the last 12 months when there was any. If there was none, it uses last year's budget. Then it adds the inflation adjustment and rounds to whole dollars.
- If your books cover less than a year, the actuals are scaled up to a full year, and the reason says how many months they covered.
- A reserve contribution is never suggested below last year's budget. The line is marked **Reserve floor**.
- Every number on the page is calculated by the app. The AI summary only describes those numbers. If it uses a figure that isn't on the page, the summary is thrown away and only the list of changes is shown.
- Lines left at $0 aren't saved.
- Opening next fiscal year follows your books: a yearly period if your periods are yearly, monthly periods if they're monthly, starting in the same month your fiscal year does. On **Fiscal periods** the new year shows "Starts" with its first day and can't be closed until it has started.
