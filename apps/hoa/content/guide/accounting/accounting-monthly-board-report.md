The app emails every board member a short report on the 1st of each month. It covers last month's cash, reserves, collections, overdue dues, income and expenses, budget, violations, architectural requests, what's waiting on the board and the next meeting. You don't have to do anything for it to go out.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can preview the report. Only board admins can send it early.
- The report is built from your association's books, so the association needs to be set up.

## Steps
1. Open **Accounting**, then the [[Reports]] tab. The **Monthly board report** card shows when the last one went out and which month is next.
2. Select [[Preview]] to see exactly the email the board will get for last month, including its subject line.
3. To send last month's report now instead of waiting for the 1st, an admin selects [[Send now]] on the card.
4. To stop getting the email yourself, select [[Your email settings]] on the card (or open **Settings**, then [[Notifications]]) and turn off **Monthly board report**. It saves right away.

## What happens next
- On the 1st, at 8:00 AM Eastern, the report for the month that just ended goes to every board member and admin who hasn't turned it off.
- After **Send now**, board members have it in their inbox in a few minutes. The card then shows that month as done, and **Send now** turns off until the next month.

## If something goes wrong
- "The [month] report has already been sent.": each month's report goes out only once, whether it was sent on the 1st or with **Send now**.
- "Couldn’t queue the report. Try again in a minute.": nothing was sent. Wait a minute and select [[Send now]] again.
- **Not delivered** next to the last report: nobody received it. That month can't be sent again from this card. Contact support.
- "You don't have permission to perform this action.": only board admins can use **Send now**. Board members can still preview the report.
- "Couldn’t save your choice. Try again.": your email setting didn't change. Try the switch again.

## Good to know
- The report always covers the last full month. You can't pick a different month, change what's in it, or add your own notes.
- The preview builds the email live, so it shows today's numbers for last month. Nothing is sent from the preview.
- If part of the report can't load, that section says so in the email rather than showing a wrong number.
- Turning the email off only affects you. It applies to every association you're on the board of, and other board members still get it.
- If every board member turns it off, no report is sent that month.
- The **Reserves** section shows the reserve balance at month end, contributions so far this year against the budget, and draws. If the association has no reserve fund, it says so and links to [[Set up a reserve fund]] instead of showing zeros. See [how reserves are counted](guide:accounting-reserves).
- Residents never get this email.
