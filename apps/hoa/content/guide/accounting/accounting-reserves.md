Use this to see whether the association is putting enough into reserves before a big repair turns into a special assessment. The figures come straight from the books each time you open the page.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can see reserves. Residents can't.
- The association needs a fund of the reserve type. Opening the books adds one called **Reserve Fund**.
- To compare against a plan, budget the year's contribution, either in a reserve fund budget or as a transfer-to-reserves line in the operating budget.

## Steps
1. Open the **Dashboard**. The **Reserves** card shows the reserve balance today, what was **Contributed this year** against what the budget planned by today, and what was **Drawn this year**.
2. If the card says **Behind budget**, contributions are more than 10% short of the budget so far this year.
3. Select [[Open the balance sheet]] to see the reserve cash and fund balance behind the number.
4. If the card says no reserve fund is set up, select [[Set up a reserve fund]]. It opens the **Funds** list on the chart of accounts.
5. An admin selects [[Open the books]] there to add the Reserve fund and its accounts. A board member is pointed to the admins instead.

## What happens next
- The card updates on its own as reserve entries are posted.
- On the 1st, [the monthly board report](guide:accounting-monthly-board-report) carries the same figures as of the last day of the month.

## If something goes wrong
- "Couldn’t load the reserve fund": only the card failed. Refresh the page. It never shows $0 in place of a failed number.
- **Set up a reserve fund** still shows after opening the books: the association may already have a fund coded RESERVE that is inactive, or not of the reserve type. Contact support.

## Good to know
- Reserves count every active fund of the reserve type. The balance is what those funds hold less what they owe.
- Contributions are reserve-fund income other than interest, plus money moved straight into reserve fund balance. Interest is shown on its own in the board report.
- Draws are reserve-fund spending, plus money moved back out of reserve fund balance.
- Only the reserve side of a transfer counts, so a transfer from operating is never counted twice.
- An opening balance posted to reserve fund balance this year counts as a contribution.
- The budget figure is the year's planned contribution spread evenly over the fiscal year, up to today.
- In Florida and California the card also quotes the state law on reserves (Florida Statutes 720.303(6)(h), California Civil Code 5300(b)(2)). It is a reminder, not legal advice.
- This is not a reserve study. It shows what the books hold, not what the association should hold.
