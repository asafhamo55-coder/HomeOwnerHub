Use this when you want to know where the association's money stands, or you need numbers for a board meeting. Every report is worked out live from the books each time you open it.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can see every report. Residents can't.
- The reports need your books to be open (a fiscal period and a chart of accounts). If they aren't, the report says so and an admin can open them from there.

## Steps
1. Open **Accounting** from the sidebar. The **Overview** shows **Operating cash**, **Reserve cash**, **Owed by owners** and **Net income** for the current period. Select any of them to go to the page behind it.
2. Check **Needs attention** for work waiting on the board, like bank transactions to match, past-due bills or draft entries. Select a line to go straight to it.
3. Select the [[Reports]] tab, then pick a report: [[Balance sheet]], [[Income statement]], [[Cash flow]] or [[Budget vs. actual]].
4. Choose what the report covers, then select [[Apply]]. The balance sheet uses an **As of** date. The income statement and cash flow use a **Period**. Budget vs. actual uses a **Budget**. Select [[Reset]] to go back to the default.
5. Read the totals at the top, then the lines below them, grouped by account.
6. To see the entries behind a number, select the [[Ledger]] tab. Filter by **Period**, **Source** or **Status**, and select an entry to see its lines.
7. For a quick check that the books add up, select the [[Trial balance]] tab. It shows **Balanced** when debits equal credits.

## What happens next
Nothing changes when you read a report. Numbers update on their own as dues are charged, owners pay, bills are entered and bank deposits are matched.

## If something goes wrong
- "No fiscal period yet": the association has no fiscal period, so there is nothing for the report to cover. An admin can open the books from that message. A board member is pointed to the admins.
- "No budget to compare against": budget vs. actual needs a budget first. Select [[New budget]], or see [how to draft next year's budget](guide:accounting-draft-budget).
- "This budget has no lines yet": the budget you picked is empty. Select [[Edit budget]] to add lines.
- **Out of balance** on the balance sheet or trial balance: the numbers don't add up, which should never happen. Don't rely on the figures. Contact support.
- A tile on the overview says it couldn't load: only that figure failed. Refresh the page. It never shows $0 in place of a failed number.

## Good to know
- Reports count **posted** entries only. Draft entries don't show up until they are posted.
- The balance sheet shows today unless you pick another date. **Total equity** includes net income so far this period.
- The income statement has three ways to count: **Accrual** (income and expenses when billed), **Cash** (only entries that moved cash) and **Modified accrual** (income when collected, expenses when billed). Accrual is the default.
- In budget vs. actual, a positive variance is good news: income above plan, or spending below it.
- Cash flow covers operating and reserve cash, grouped by where each movement came from, from opening to closing balance.
- You can't download these reports from their pages. For a PDF board packet with the balance sheet, income statement and cash flow, open the **Periods** tab. For a monthly email to the board, see [the monthly board report](guide:accounting-monthly-board-report).
- A posted entry can't be edited or deleted. On the entry's page, [[Reverse]] posts an equal and opposite entry dated today, and the original stays on the books marked reversed.
- For the reserve balance, contributions against the budget and draws in one place, see [check your reserves](guide:accounting-reserves).
- The [[Accounts]] tab lists your chart of accounts. You can read it, but you can't add or change accounts there.
