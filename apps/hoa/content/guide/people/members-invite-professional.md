Your CPA and your attorney can work inside HomeownerHub without becoming part of the board. They see only what their job needs, for a time you choose, and nothing they send goes out until a board member approves it.

## Before you start
- Board members and admins can invite a professional. Only an admin can set officer titles.
- Use the professional's own email address. HomeownerHub emails them a link to sign in.
- A CPA reads the accounting records and reports. An attorney sees only the collections cases, violations and records requests you assign.

## Steps
1. Open **Settings**, then **Professionals**.
2. Under **Invite a professional**, choose **CPA** or **Attorney**, enter their email, and check the **Access ends on** date. A CPA starts at one year and an attorney at six months. A grant can run for at most a year, and you can renew it.
3. For an attorney, tick the matters they should see. Tick **General counsel** if they also need the governing documents and the compliance record.
4. Select [[Invite a professional]].
5. To set officer titles, an admin opens **Officers** on the same page and picks a title for each board member: President, Vice-President, Secretary, Treasurer or Director.
6. Under **Dual control**, enter the amount above which a money approval needs two different board members.

## What happens next
- The CPA lands on **Accounting & Reports** and the attorney on **My matters**. Neither sees the inbox, resident messages or the residents list.
- A CPA can mark a fiscal year as reviewed with a note, and propose an adjusting entry. An attorney can add notes, upload documents and draft letters.
- All of it arrives in **Approvals** under **From your professionals**. Approving a CPA's entry posts it to the ledger. A note, upload or draft letter is accepted onto the record, and nothing is sent.
- With a Treasurer on the board, money approvals go to the Treasurer first. That means scanned bills and a CPA's proposed entries. An admin can stand in.
- When the end date passes, their access stops by itself. They see a page saying so.

## If something goes wrong
- You get "Money approvals go to the Treasurer first": ask the Treasurer or an admin to sign it off.
- You get "Your sign-off is recorded": the amount is above your dual-control limit. A second board member must approve it too.
- Everyone you invite gets the same thing: an email with a link. They must sign in with that same email address and select **Accept invitation** before they get any access. The link works once and expires in 7 days. You can cancel it under **Invited**.
- You can't set a title: only an admin can, and each office (other than Director) has one holder.

## Good to know
- To extend access, pick a new date under **Renew until** on their row and select **Renew**. To end access now, select **Remove access**. To give an attorney another matter later, use **Assign a matter** on their row.
- Every grant, renewal, removal and view of a matter or the books is written to the activity log with ids only.
- A CPA sees owners' names on receivables, not their email, phone or mailing address. An attorney sees the owner's name and the home's address on an assigned matter, nothing more.
