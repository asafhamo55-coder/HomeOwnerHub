Some notices must reach owners in a way the law accepts. In California, the association may send official notices by email only to owners who agreed to it (Civil Code §4040). HomeownerHub keeps a record of each owner's choice.

## Before you start
- An owner needs a portal account to hold consent.
- Board members and admins see everyone's status. An owner sees only their own.

## Steps
### An owner chooses in the portal
1. The owner opens **Account** in their portal.
2. Under **Notices**, they turn **Electronic notices** on to get official notices by email, or off to get them by mail.

### The board records a paper or email consent
1. Open **Settings**, then **Members**. Or open a property and go to **People**.
2. Next to the owner, select [[Record written consent]].
3. Choose whether the owner agreed or withdrew, and whether they told you on a signed form or by email.
4. Select [[Save consent]].

## What happens next
The owner's status shows next to their name: **E-consent on file**, **E-consent withdrawn** or **No e-consent**, with the date and how it was given.

## Good to know
- Every choice is kept as a record. Nothing can be edited or deleted. A change of mind adds a new record.
- An owner with no record has not agreed. Treat them as getting official notices by mail.
- When a board member views the portal as a resident, the switch is read-only. Only the owner can change it there.
