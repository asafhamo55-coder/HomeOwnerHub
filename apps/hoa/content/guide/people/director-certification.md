Florida law requires every new director of a homeowners' association to complete state-approved director education and submit the certificate within 90 days. The **Florida director certification** list on **Members** counts down to that deadline for each board member.

## Before you start
- This shows only for associations in Florida. Nothing appears for any other state.
- Only board members and admins see it. Nothing about it goes to residents.
- The rule is Fla. Stat. § 720.3033(1). Within 90 days after being elected or appointed to the board, each director must submit a certificate of completing the education from a department-approved provider.
- A director who doesn't file the certificate on time is suspended from the board until they do. The board may fill the seat temporarily during the suspension.
- The certificate is valid for up to 4 years. A director must complete the new-director education again at least every 4 years.
- The association keeps each director's certificate for members to inspect for 5 years after the director's election.

## Steps
1. Open **Settings**, then **Members**, and scroll to **Florida director certification**.
2. Next to a board member, select [[Add election date]].
3. Enter the date they were elected or appointed. If they already have a certificate, enter its date too. Optionally, add a link to the certificate in **Documents**.
4. Select [[Save]].
5. When the director brings in their certificate, select [[Mark certified]]. This records today as the certificate date.

## What happens next
- Each board member shows a status, such as "Certificate due in 30 days (Nov 20, 2026)", "Certificate overdue since Sep 29, 2026 — suspended until filed", or "Certified Sep 15, 2026 · valid to Sep 15, 2030".
- When a certificate is due within 30 days, is overdue, or is within 30 days of its 4-year limit, a **Director certification** card shows on your dashboard. The board's morning email also gets a line for that director.
- The same status shows next to each person under **Who has access** on **Board handover**.
- Every save is recorded in the activity log.

## If something goes wrong
- "This can’t be saved on this server yet. Contact support.": nothing was recorded. Contact support.
- "Record the date they were elected or appointed first.": select [[Add election date]] before [[Mark certified]].
- "Use an https:// link or a link to a page under Documents.": the certificate link must be a secure web link or a link to a document in the app.
- "Couldn’t load director certification": a loading problem, not "nothing due". Refresh the page.

## Good to know
- Informational, not legal advice. Confirm the details with your association's attorney.
- The law no longer accepts a signed statement instead of the education certificate. Only the certificate counts.
- Directors also need yearly continuing education: at least 4 hours for associations with fewer than 2,500 parcels, or 8 hours for 2,500 or more. This list doesn't track those hours.
- Not having a certificate on file doesn't make a board decision invalid.
