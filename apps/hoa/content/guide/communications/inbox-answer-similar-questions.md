Use this when several open conversations in the Inbox ask the same thing, like when the pool reopens. You write one reply, and each resident gets their own copy.

## Before you start
- An HOA admin must connect the HOA mailbox first. See [connect the HOA mailbox](guide:inbox-connect-mailbox).
- The conversations must be open and waiting for your reply.

## Steps
1. Open **Messages**. On the **Inbox** tab, when open conversations on the list ask the same thing, a line like **3 similar questions** appears above the list.
2. Select [[Answer all]] on that line.
3. Untick any conversation that doesn't belong.
4. Write the message, or pick one under **Start from a saved reply** and edit it. {{first_name}} becomes each resident's own first name.
5. Select [[Review and send]]. Check the count and the message.
6. Select **Send to … residents**.

## What happens next
Each resident gets their own email, addressed only to them. Nobody sees who else got it. Each reply waits 30 seconds before it sends. Select [[Undo all]] in that time to stop them all. The conversations close, and reopen if a resident writes back.

## If something goes wrong
- [[Review and send]] is greyed out: tick at least two conversations, and remove every [[BLANK]] and any {{balance}} from the message.
- "A reply to many residents can’t include a balance.": one message goes to everyone, so it can't hold one home's balance or account details. Answer those one by one.
- **Not sent** after sending: that conversation was closed, already had a reply on its way, or had no address to reply to. A reply that couldn't be queued stays as a draft in its conversation.
- "The number of conversations changed.": check the list and confirm again. Nothing was sent.

## Good to know
- Similar questions are spotted by matching words in each subject and the start of each email. No AI is used. Check the list before you send.
- A batch reply goes to at most 25 conversations.
- Saved replies that show a balance aren't offered here.
- For an announcement to many homes, use [send an announcement](guide:communications-send-announcement) instead.
