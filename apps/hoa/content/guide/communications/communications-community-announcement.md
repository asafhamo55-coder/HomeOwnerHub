Use **New announcement** for the friendly notes you send to the whole community: a party, a holiday greeting, a reminder about parking or pets. You pick what it is about, check a ready-made draft and press send. For a custom message with your own audience rules, see [send an announcement](guide:communications-send-announcement).

## Before you start
- Board members and admins can send announcements.
- You don't need to write anything. The draft already has your HOA's name in it.

## Steps
1. On the dashboard or at the top of **Messages**, select [[New announcement]].
2. Pick the card that fits, like **Halloween party**, **Happy holidays**, **Pet waste and leash rules** or **Severe weather**. Or, under **Or describe it in one sentence**, type something like "Block party Saturday June 14 at 4pm at the pool" and select [[Write it for me]]. We pick out the date, time and place for you.
3. Check the draft. Fill in the **Date**, **Time** and **Where** boxes if the card asks for them, and the draft updates. Change any words you like in **Subject** and **Message**.
4. To also send it in Spanish, tick **Also send it in Spanish**. Read the Spanish and fix anything before you go on.
5. Select [[Looks good, review it]].
6. Under **Who gets it**, leave **All residents** ticked, or pick **Only one street**. Look at the preview to see the email and the text. Under **When**, choose **Send now** or **Schedule for later**.
7. Select the **Send** button at the bottom. It shows how many homes will get it. Nothing goes out until you select it.

## What happens next
The message goes to everyone in the audience. Residents who turned off emails are skipped, and a text goes only to residents who said yes to texts. A scheduled announcement waits until the time you picked. Open the announcement under **Messages** to see how it went.

The text is one short message of at most 279 characters. The email can be as long as you like.

## If something goes wrong
- "Add a subject and a message first.": the subject or message is empty or too short. Add a sentence and try again.
- "Finish the Spanish version or turn it off.": the Spanish boxes are empty. Fill them in, or untick **Also send it in Spanish**.
- "Audience resolved to zero recipients": nobody is in the audience. Choose **All residents**, or check that your homes have contacts on file.
- "Pick a time in the future, or choose Send now.": the day and time you picked has already passed.
- A **We couldn't confirm the send** message: the app lost the connection. Open **Messages** to see whether it went out before you try again.
- The AI could not polish a sentence you typed: you get the simple draft instead. Edit it as usual.

## Good to know
- Within a week of an occasion like Halloween or Thanksgiving, the dashboard shows a card, like **Halloween is Saturday**, with [[Write it]]. It opens the draft with the date filled in. Select [[Not now]] to hide the suggestion for your HOA. It comes back next year.
- Nothing is ever sent for you. Every announcement needs you to select the **Send** button.
- The ready-made drafts use warm wording that suits every household. They don't assume anyone's holidays, family or plans.
- Texts only go if texting is set up for your HOA. A **Severe weather** announcement starts with the text option ticked. For the rest you can tick **Also send a short text to residents who opted in**.
- Spanish for the ready-made cards is written in advance. For a sentence you typed yourself, AI translates it and you read it first.
- Select [[Back to the draft]] on the last screen to change the words without losing your work.
- For a message to one resident, see [reply to a resident](guide:inbox-reply-to-resident). For an emergency like a water shutoff, see [send an urgent notice](guide:communications-urgent-notice).
