Use this when residents need to hear about something soon, like a water shutoff, a storm or a gas leak. An urgent message goes by text as well as email and the portal, so people see it even if they don't check their email.

## Before you start
- Texts only go out if texting is set up for your HOA. If it isn't, an urgent message still goes by email and the portal.

## Steps
1. Open **Messages**, select [[New message]], then [[To many homes]].
2. In **What do you want to tell residents?**, say it in your own words, like "Water off Thursday 9 to 2 on Elm St", and select [[Draft it for me]]. Or pick the **Water shutoff** or **Severe weather** template.
3. Check the **Audience**. To reach only the homes affected, pick **A street** and choose the street. Select [[Continue]].
4. Check the **Subject** and **Body**. Put what, where and when in the first sentence. Select [[Continue]].
5. Under **How should it go out?**, tick **This is urgent**. **Going by** changes to text, email and portal, and a line below says how many recipients get a text.
6. In **What residents get**, open the **Text** tab. It shows the text as it will go out and how many of its 279 characters it uses.
7. Select [[Send now]].

## What happens next
Residents who agreed to get texts get a text. Everyone gets the email and the portal copy, unless they turned off email. The message's page shows, per home, who was reached by text or email. To chase the homes that haven't opened it, see [follow up with residents who didn't open](guide:communications-follow-up).

## If something goes wrong
- "Texting isn't set up yet.": your HOA can't send texts yet, so **Text** can't be ticked. The message goes by email and the portal.
- The **Review** step says "Text: … will be skipped (opted out, or never opted in to texts).": those residents never agreed to texts. They still get the email and the portal copy.
- The text runs past 279 characters: it is shortened and ends with a link to the full message in the portal. Shorten the subject and first sentence if the cut hides something important.
- A text shows **Failed** with "SMS not configured": texting wasn't set up when it was sent, so no text went out.

## Good to know
- Only residents who agreed to get texts are texted. Everyone else gets the email and portal copy. Board members are never texted.
- A text holds up to 279 characters, subject included. Each person gets one text, never a chain of texts. Email has no length limit.
- The **Water shutoff** and **Severe weather** templates tick **This is urgent** for you. [[Draft it for me]] may tick it too. Untick it for a routine message.
- If you tick **Email** or **Text** yourself, the app keeps your choice when you change **This is urgent** afterwards.
- With a Spanish version, a text carries both languages only if both fit in 279 characters. Otherwise the text goes out in English only. The email and portal carry both.
