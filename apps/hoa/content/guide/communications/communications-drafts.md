Use this when you start a message to many homes and can't finish it now. You don't need to press a save button: the composer saves a draft for you.

## Steps
1. Start a message: open **Messages**, select [[New message]], then [[To many homes]].
2. Type a subject or some of the message. A moment after you stop typing, the bottom of the composer says **Draft saved**.
3. Leave the page whenever you like.
4. Later, open **Messages** and select the [[Drafts]] tab. Your draft is marked **Message to many homes**, with its subject or "Untitled draft".
5. Select [[Resume]]. The composer opens where you left off, with your audience, message and channels.
6. Finish it and select [[Send now]], or select [[Delete draft]] to throw it away.

## What happens next
When you send a draft, it leaves Drafts and becomes a sent message under **Sent to residents**. A deleted draft is gone, and nothing was sent.

## If something goes wrong
- "The draft couldn't be saved. Try again.": your changes are still on the page. Keep going and it tries again, or check your connection.
- "This draft was sent or deleted. Changes here aren't saved.": someone sent or deleted it, maybe you in another tab. Start a new message if you still need one.
- "This draft is too large to save.": the message is very long. Shorten it or send it now.
- No draft appears: a draft is only saved once you type a subject, some message text or a template answer.

## Good to know
- A draft is never sent, nobody receives it, and it isn't counted in delivery results.
- Every board member and admin sees the HOA's drafts, not only their own.
- A scheduled message isn't a draft. It is under **Sent to residents** until it goes out.
- The [[Drafts]] tab also lists email replies waiting for you in the Inbox, marked "Reply to" and the home, like "Reply to 12 Elm St". Select one to review it in its conversation. The number on the tab counts both kinds.
- Opening a follow-up or a pre-filled notice doesn't make a draft until you change something.
