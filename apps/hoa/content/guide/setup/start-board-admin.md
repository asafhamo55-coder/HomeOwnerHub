HomeownerHub keeps your board's work in one place: homes and residents, dues, violations, architectural requests, meetings and mail. Its AI drafts letters and answers, but nothing is posted or sent until someone on the board approves it. Start with the setup checklist at the top of your dashboard.

## Steps
1. Open **Dashboard** from the sidebar. The card at the top is called **Get your HOA set up**.
2. Pick any step that is not done yet. The whole step is a link that takes you to the right page.
3. Finish the task on that page, then come back to the dashboard.

![[guide-dashboard-setup]]

### The nine steps
Seven steps are required. The last two are marked **(optional)**.
- [[Set up your association and books]]: the association your homes and dues belong to, with its Operating and Reserve funds, chart of accounts and this year's fiscal period. Only an admin can create it. See [Bill this month's dues](guide:dues-generate-monthly).
- [[Add your properties]]: every address in the community. Type them in, or select [[Import a spreadsheet]] to bring them all in at once. See [Add your properties and residents](guide:setup-properties-residents) and [Import your homes and owners from a spreadsheet](guide:setup-import-roster).
- [[Add owners and residents]]: who lives where, so mail and dues match a person.
- [[Invite the board]]: give at least one other board member or admin their own login. Inviting a resident doesn't tick this step. See [Invite the rest of the board](guide:members-invite-board).
- [[Set up dues]]: bill this month's dues so every home has a balance. See [Bill this month's dues](guide:dues-generate-monthly).
- [[Set up resident payments]]: add the HOA's Zelle details so owners can pay. Until you do, residents see "Online payments aren't set up yet." See [Turn on online dues payments with Zelle](guide:settings-resident-payments).
- [[Upload governing documents]]: your CC&Rs and bylaws, so answers cite your own rules. See [Upload your governing documents](guide:documents-governing-and-ask).
- [[Connect the bank]] (optional): link the HOA's bank so deposits match dues. You can reconcile by hand instead. See [Connect your bank, match transactions and reconcile](guide:accounting-bank-reconcile).
- [[Connect the HOA mailbox]] (optional): resident email comes into **Messages**, filed under the right home. Google mailboxes only for now.

## What happens next
The checklist ticks itself off as the data appears, so do the steps in any order. When every required step is done, the card says **Your HOA is set up** and you can hide it with [[Dismiss]], even if the optional steps are still open.

Homes you add before the association exists are attached to it automatically when an admin sets it up, so they're billed with everyone else. If your HOA has more than one association, the dashboard and the Properties page show "homes aren't assigned to an association" until someone on the board picks one and selects [[Assign them]].

## If something goes wrong
- A step shows [[Couldn’t check]]: we couldn't read that part of your data just now. Reload the page. It is not something you did wrong, and the step is not counted as done until we can check it.
- "Finish every required step before hiding the checklist.": the card can only be hidden once all seven required steps are done.

## Good to know
- Hiding the card only hides it in this browser. If a step stops being true later, the card comes back.
- The sign-up setup page shows the same checklist as the dashboard, with the same steps and ticks.
