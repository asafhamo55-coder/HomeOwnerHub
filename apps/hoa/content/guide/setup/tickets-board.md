Residents send support requests from the portal, like a broken gate or a dues question. They land in **Tickets** for the board to answer.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can see and answer tickets.
- The board is notified when a resident opens a new ticket.

## Steps
1. Open **Tickets** from the sidebar. Use the filters [[All]], **Open**, **In progress** or **Closed** to narrow the list.
2. Select a ticket's subject to open it. The resident's request is at the top of **Conversation**.
3. Type in **Reply to resident** and select [[Send reply]]. The resident sees it in their portal.
4. For a note only the board should see, tick [[Internal note]] first, then select [[Add note]].
5. Under **Details**, set **Status** and **Priority**. Each change saves right away.
6. To track work, select [[Add action item]] under **Action items**. Enter a title, and optionally an assignee, due date and priority. Select [[Create action item]].
7. When it's done, set **Status** to **Closed**.

## What happens next
- Closing a ticket records who closed it and when. The resident sees **Closed** and can't reply anymore.
- Internal notes are highlighted for the board and never shown to the resident.

## If something goes wrong
- "Write a reply first." or "Write the note first.": the box is empty. Type your message.
- "Give the action item a title.": action items need a title.
- A status or priority change shows an error under the field: nothing changed. Try again.

## Good to know
- The resident sees your reply in the portal. They get an email with it only if your association turned on **Tell residents about decisions and replies** in [[Settings]] → [[Notifications]]. It is off until a board turns it on. Internal notes are never sent.
- You can't create a ticket for a resident here. Residents open them from the portal.
- To reopen a closed ticket, set **Status** back to **Open** or **In progress**.
- [[Delete]] removes the ticket and its conversation from the board's list and the resident's portal. To finish a request without removing it, close it instead.
- You and the resident can attach files under **Documents** with [[Attach document]].
- Press ⌘ or Ctrl + Enter to send a reply from the keyboard.
