Every home in the community is a property. Owners, residents, violations and mail all hang off a property, so add them first.

## Before you start
- You need the list of addresses. The owner's name, email and phone are optional.
- Board admins and board members can add properties.

## Steps
1. Open **Properties** from the sidebar and select [[Add property]].
2. Fill in the **Address**. Add the **Unit / Lot #** and owner details if you have them.
3. Select [[Add property]] to save. You go back to the Properties list.
4. Open the property, then the **Residents** tab, and select [[Add resident]].
5. Enter the **Full name** and pick a **Role** (Owner, Tenant, Family member or Other). Email and phone are optional.
6. Tick [[Primary contact]] for the person notices should be addressed to, then select [[Add resident]].

## What happens next
The property appears in the Properties list. Its residents get notices and outreach for that home, and the property page shows its balance, violations and mail in one place.

## If something goes wrong
- "Address is required.": the address is the only field you must fill in.
- "Owner email must be a valid email.": check the email, or leave it blank.
- "Enter the resident’s name.": a resident needs a full name.

## Good to know
- **Dues are billed from your community's unit list, not from this form.** A property you add here appears in Properties, but it is not yet a home that dues can be charged to. The unit list is loaded by HomeownerHub support from your spreadsheet. Ask support to import your homes, or to add a new one.
- Have many homes? There is no spreadsheet upload for boards. Ask support to import your spreadsheet.
- To change an owner's details, open the property and select [[Edit]].
- To remove a resident, open the Residents tab and use the remove button on their row. Their record stays in the property's history.
