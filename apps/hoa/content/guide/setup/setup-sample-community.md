If your account has no homes yet, you can fill it with a made-up community to see how the app works. Nothing is sent to anyone, and you can remove all of it in one step.

## Before you start
- Board members and admins can add and remove the sample community.
- Set up your association first (step 1 of the setup checklist). The sample needs it.
- The offer shows only while your account has no homes. Once you add a real home, it goes away for good.

## Steps
### Add the sample
1. Open the dashboard. If your account has no homes, you see "Want to look around first?"
2. Select [[Try it with a sample community]].
3. Wait a moment. A message says how many sample homes were added.

### Remove the sample
1. Open the dashboard. A **Sample data** notice shows while sample homes exist.
2. Select [[Remove sample data]] in that notice.
3. Read the question and confirm with [[Remove sample data]]. The message says "The sample data is gone."

## What happens next
- You get 12 made-up homes, a month of dues, a few violations and requests, a draft announcement and a meeting.
- Nothing is sent. The sample owners have made-up email addresses.
- Removing it deletes the sample homes, dues, violations, requests, announcement and meeting. Anything you added yourself stays.

## If something goes wrong
- "You already have homes in your account, so the sample community is not available.": the offer is only for an empty account.
- "The sample community is already here.": remove it first if you want to start it again.
- "Set up your association first (step 1 of the checklist), then try the sample community.": finish step 1, then try again.

## Good to know
- Remove the sample before you add your own homes, so your records start clean.
- The notice reads "You’re looking at sample data. Remove it when you’re ready to add your own homes."
