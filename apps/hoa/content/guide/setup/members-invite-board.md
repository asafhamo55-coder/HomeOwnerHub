Each board member should have their own login, so every approval and decision shows who made it.

## Before you start
- Only admins can invite admins and board members. Board members can invite residents only.
- The roles, as the page explains them: "Admins can do everything. Board members run day-to-day operations. Residents see their own dues and documents and can submit requests."

## Steps
1. Open **Members** from the Account section of the sidebar.
2. In the [[Invite a member]] card, enter their **Email**. The **Name** is optional.
3. Pick a **Role**: Admin, Board or Resident. The line under it says what the role you picked can do.
4. For a resident, you can also pick a **Property** under [[Link to a property]].
5. Select [[Send invitation]].

![[guide-members-invite]]

## What happens next
They get an email with a sign-in link. If they already have a HomeownerHub login, they are added to your HOA straight away. They appear in the **Members** list below the form.

## If something goes wrong
- "Board members can only invite residents.": ask an admin to invite board members.
- "You can't demote the last admin. Promote someone else first.": every HOA needs at least one admin.

## Good to know
- **Change a role:** in the Members list, pick a new role from the dropdown on their row. It saves as soon as you pick. Only admins can do this. In Georgia, moving someone onto or off the board (Resident to Board or Admin, or back) also adds a row to amend your Secretary of State registration within 30 days. See [Register your Georgia association with the Secretary of State](guide:ga-registration-kit).
- **Remove someone:** use the trash button on their row and confirm. You can't remove yourself or the last admin.
- The setup checklist's [[Invite the board]] step is done once someone besides you is on the Members list as an Admin or Board member. Inviting a resident doesn't count.
- Invitations nobody has accepted yet are listed under **Pending invitations**, where you can resend or revoke them. To invite every owner on your roster at once, see [Invite every owner to the portal at once](guide:members-invite-owners).
- Managers who look after several HOAs switch between them with [[Switch HOA]] at the top of the sidebar, under the HomeownerHub name.
