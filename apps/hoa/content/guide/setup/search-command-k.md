The search box opens a quick menu that works from every board page. Use it to get somewhere fast without the sidebar.

## Steps
1. Open search: press ⌘K on a Mac or Ctrl+K on Windows. You can also press / when you aren't typing in a field, or click **Search or jump to…** in the top bar. On a phone, tap the search icon.
2. Type a few letters: a page name, an owner's name, an address or a word from a record.
3. Look through the groups. **Go to** and **Create** are pages and quick actions. **Help** is guide articles. Below them are your records, such as **Owners**, **Properties**, **Violations**, **ARC requests**, **Tickets**, **Inbox**, **Meetings**, **Documents** and **Vendors**.
4. Use the up and down arrow keys to pick a row, then press Enter. Or click the row.
5. If nothing matches, pick **Ask the guide: …** to ask your question in the help panel.
6. Press Esc to close search without opening anything.

## What happens next
The page, record or help article opens. Search clears its text each time it closes.

## If something goes wrong
- "Couldn’t search records.": record search failed, but pages and help still show. Try again in a moment.
- "… couldn’t be searched, so results may be incomplete.": some record types failed. A missing result isn't proof the record doesn't exist.
- "No records match …": no owner, home or record has that text. Try fewer letters, part of the address, or the owner's last name.

## Good to know
- Record search starts once you type 2 letters, and shows up to 5 matches per group.
- Searching an owner's name or an address also finds the violations, ARC requests, tickets and inbox threads on that home.
- Search only looks in the HOA you're working in right now.
- Meetings are found by their type and summary, documents by their title, and vendors by their name.
- You can also switch to the light or dark theme, or sign out, from search.
- Residents don't have this search. Only board members and admins can search records.
