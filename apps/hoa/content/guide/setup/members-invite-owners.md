Once your roster is in, invite the owners in one go instead of one at a time. Each owner's account is linked to the home they own on file, so they see their own dues and notices when they sign in.

## Before you start
- Owners need an email address on your roster. Owners without one are skipped.
- Board members and admins can both do this.

## Steps
1. Open **Members** from the Account section of the sidebar.
2. In **Invite owners to the portal**, read how many owners have no portal account yet.
3. Select [[Invite all owners without an account]].
4. Check the count in the confirmation, then select the **Send** button to confirm.
5. If the result says some are left for the next batch, select [[Invite all owners without an account]] again.

## What happens next
Each owner gets the same invitation email as a single invite. Owners who already have a HomeownerHub login are added to your HOA without an email. Everyone invited and not yet signed in is listed under **Pending invitations**.

## If something goes wrong
- "The owner list changed since you reviewed it.": someone changed the roster or sent invitations meanwhile. Select the button again and confirm the new count.
- "email paused for now": the email service is limiting how fast invitations go out. Wait a few minutes and send the rest.
- "held back by test mode": sending is switched off for those addresses (a test or sandbox community), so no email went out.

## Good to know
- Invitations go out 25 at a time, a moment apart, so a large roster takes a few clicks.
- **Resend:** under **Pending invitations**, select [[Resend]] on a row to send the email again.
- **Revoke:** select [[Revoke]] and confirm. The invitation no longer gives them access to your HOA, and the owner's name and email stay on your roster, so you can invite them again later.
- Board members can resend or revoke resident invitations. Only admins can do it for board and admin invitations.
