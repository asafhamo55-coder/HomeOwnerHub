Residents apply for exterior changes, like paint, a fence or a roof, from their portal. Applications land in ARC review for the board to decide.

## Steps
1. Open [[ARC review]] from the sidebar. It opens on applications that need a decision.
2. Open an application.
3. Read **Proposed work** and open any files under **Documents**.
4. Ask the resident a question in **Conversation** if you need more, and select [[Send reply]].
5. In the **Decision** card, type a **Board response** if you have conditions or a reason.
6. Select [[Approve]], [[Deny]] or [[Mark in review]].

![[guide-arc-detail]]

## What happens next
The resident sees the decision and your board response in their portal. No email or text is sent, so tell them yourself if it is urgent.

## Good to know
- No applications yet? The page offers [[Tell residents how to apply]], which starts a message to residents.
- Tick [[Internal note]] in Conversation to leave a note only the board sees.
- There is no "request changes" decision. Use [[Mark in review]] and ask in Conversation.
