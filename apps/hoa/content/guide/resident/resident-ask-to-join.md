If you sign in and see "No home linked yet", your account isn't connected to your home. Your account may be new, or it may belong to another HOA. Ask to join your home, and your board approves it.

## Before you start
- Sign in with your own account first.
- You can ask with your street address and ZIP code, or with a join code your board gives you. A code is quickest if the board offers one.

## Steps
1. On "No home linked yet", tap [[Ask to join]]. Without an HOA yet, use the link under **Create your HOA**.
2. Choose [[My address]] or [[A code from my board]].
3. Fill it in and tap [[Send to my board]].

## What happens next
- A board member reviews your request. Once they approve it, you see your home, dues and notices the next time you open the portal.
- If your home still isn't linked after a few days, contact your board. A request they turn down links nothing.

## If something goes wrong
- "That code didn't work": the code was used, cancelled or is over 30 days old. Ask your board for a new one.
- "You can ask 5 times a day": try again tomorrow, or ask your board for a code.
- You asked with your address but nothing happened: the address may not match your home's record exactly. Ask your board for a code.

## Good to know
- After asking with an address you always see the same message, whether or not it matched a home. That keeps the list of homes in your HOA private.
- A code works once, for one home.
