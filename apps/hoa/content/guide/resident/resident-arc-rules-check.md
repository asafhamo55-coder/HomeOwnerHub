The rules check shows you, before you submit, which of your community's rules likely apply to your change. Afterwards, your application page shows where the board is with it.

## Before you start
- Your account must be linked to your home. If it isn't, you see "No home linked yet".

## Steps
1. Tap **Requests**, then [[Architectural request (ARC)]] under **Start something new**.
2. Pick the **Type of change**, write a **One-line summary**, and fill in the **Detailed description** with sizes, materials, colors and location.
3. Tap [[Check my request against the rules]]. Read **Rules that likely apply**: each rule is marked **Looks consistent**, **Possible conflict** or **Needs detail**. Read **The board will probably ask about** too.
4. If a rule may conflict, change your request or explain in the description why it fits. Tap [[Check again]] to re-run it.
5. Tap [[Submit application]]. Your application page opens.
6. Add plans, drawings or photos under **Documents** with [[Attach document]], now or after you submit. Ask the board a question under **Conversation**, then tap [[Send]].
7. To follow up later, tap **Requests**, then **ARC applications** under **Track**. Each application shows its status.

![[guide-resident-arc-new]]

## What happens next
- Your application shows **Submitted**, then **In review** while the board looks at it, then **Approved** or **Denied**.
- The board's written decision appears at the top of the application page, with its date.
- The board can see what the rules check showed you before you submitted.

## If something goes wrong
- "Pick the type of change and describe it first, then check it against the rules.": choose a type and write a longer description, then check again.
- "The rules check isn’t available right now. You can still submit your application.": the check failed. You can still submit.
- "No clear rules found": the check couldn't find a rule that clearly applies. The board still reviews your application.
- "Describe the scope in more detail.": the description needs at least 20 characters.
- "This application can no longer be withdrawn.": the board has already decided it.

## Good to know
- The rules check is optional, and it is never a decision. You can submit whatever it says. Only the board approves or denies.
- Don't start work before the board approves it in writing. Work started without approval may need to be removed at your expense.
- While an application is **Submitted** or **In review**, you can tap [[Withdraw]], then [[Withdraw application]]. To make the change later, you'll need a new application.
- Once an application is decided or withdrawn, the conversation closes. To propose a new change, start a new application.
- Attachments can be a PDF, an image, or a Word or Excel file, up to 10 MB.
