You can book the shared places in your community from your phone: the clubhouse, the pool pavilion, a court. You move through four short steps, and the screen always says which step you are on.

## Steps
1. On **Home**, tap [[Community]], then [[Book a space]].
2. Step 1: tap the space you want. Each one shows its hours, its longest booking and whether the board confirms it.
3. Step 2: pick a day on the calendar. Days with a solid border have free times. Crossed out days are full, closed or not open yet. Tap [[Earlier month]] or [[Later month]] to move around. If you have more than one home, pick it under **Which home is this for?**.
4. Step 3: tap a start time. Times that are taken say "Booked". Then tap **How long?** and pick how many hours.
5. Tap [[Next: check my booking]]. Step 4 shows one plain sentence, like "Clubhouse, Saturday, November 8, 2:00 pm to 4:00 pm".
6. Optional: enter how many people and what it is for. If the space has house rules, read them and tick **I read the house rules**.
7. Tap [[Book it]].

## What happens next
- If the space is confirmed right away, you see "You are booked." and the booking is under **Your bookings** on the same page.
- If the board confirms each booking, you see "Your request is sent." The time is held for you and the booking says **Waiting for the board**. You get an email when the board answers.
- To cancel, find the booking under **Your bookings** and tap [[Cancel booking]]. Then tap [[Yes, cancel it]] to be sure, or [[No, keep it]].

## If something goes wrong
- "Not available yet" or "Nothing to book yet": your board has not set up a space. Ask them.
- "No home on your account yet": ask the board to link you to your home.
- "You already have ... bookings": each home can hold only so many bookings for a space. Cancel one or wait until it has passed.
- "There are no free times ... right now": tap [[Next day with free times]], or ask the board.
- "This starts soon, so you cannot cancel it here. Please ask the board.": the cancel time has passed. Contact the board.

## Good to know
- Tap [[Back]] to go back a step. Nothing is booked until you tap **Book it**.
- A booking can show **Not approved**. The board's note says why.
