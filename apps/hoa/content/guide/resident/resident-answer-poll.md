Your board may ask the homes a quick question. Each home answers once, and anyone in your home can change the answer until the poll closes.

## Steps
1. On **Home**, tap [[Community]], then [[Polls]].
2. Tap the poll you want to answer.
3. If you have more than one home, pick the home under **Answering for**.
4. Tap your answer. Some polls say **Pick every answer that fits**, so you can tap more than one.
5. Tap [[Send my answer]]. You see "Thank you. Your home’s answer is saved."

## What happens next
- To change your answer, open the poll again, pick a new answer and tap [[Change my answer]].
- The board sees how many homes picked each answer, not who answered.
- Results show as "Results so far" when the board lets residents see them right away. Otherwise they show after the poll closes.

## If something goes wrong
- "This poll is closed.": the board closed it or the last day has passed. You can't answer or change an answer now.
- "No polls right now": the board has not asked anything. A poll shows here once the board opens it.
- "Only homes in this association can answer this poll.": your account is not linked to a home here. Ask your board to add you to your home.

## Good to know
- A poll is informal. It is not an official vote and the board does not have to follow it.
- Your home gets one answer, no matter how many people live there.
