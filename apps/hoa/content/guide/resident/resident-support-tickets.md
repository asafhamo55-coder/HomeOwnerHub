Use a support request when you need the board's help: a repair, a noise or parking problem, a gate key, or a question about your dues. Everything you and the board write stays in one place.

## Before you start
- Your account must be linked to your home. If it isn't, you see "No home linked yet". Ask your board to link you.

## Steps
1. Tap **Requests** at the bottom of the screen, then [[New support request]].
2. If you have more than one home, pick the **Home**.
3. Pick a **Category**, for example **Maintenance request** or **Billing / dues question**.
4. Write a short **Subject**, like "Pool gate latch is sticking".
5. In **Description**, say what's going on, where and when. Write at least 20 characters. Optional: tap [[Improve with AI]] for rewrites, then [[Use this]] on the one you like.
6. Tap [[Send request]]. The request opens so you can follow it.
7. To add a photo or file, tap [[Attach document]] under **Documents**.

## What happens next
- The board is notified of your new request.
- Replies show under **Conversation**. To answer, type in the box and tap [[Send]].
- Find your requests later under **Requests**, then **Support requests**. Each shows **Open**, **In progress** or **Closed**.

## If something goes wrong
- "Subject is too short.": use at least 5 characters.
- "Describe your issue in more detail.": the description needs at least 20 characters.
- "Choose a category.": pick a category from the list.
- "You can only submit tickets for units you own.": the home you picked isn't linked to your account. Ask your board.
- "Couldn’t send your request": nothing was sent. Try again.

## Good to know
- Your association can choose to email you when the board replies. If it hasn't, check **Support requests** for new replies.
- Once the board closes a request, you can't reply to it. Open a new request if you need more help.
- You can't edit, close or delete a request after you send it.
- Files can be a PDF, an image, or a Word or Excel file, up to 10 MB.
- To report someone breaking a community rule, use [[Report a concern]] instead. To ask for approval to change your home, use [[Architectural request (ARC)]].
