When the board opens a violation on your home, it is listed on your home screen. Open it to read the notice and tell the board what you've done about it.

## Before you start
- Your account must be linked to your home. You only ever see violations on your own home.

## Steps
1. On your home screen, tap the violation listed with the notices on your home.
2. Read **The notice**: what the board noted, the **Rule cited**, when the notice was sent and the **Fix by** date. Tap [[Read the letter the board sent]] to see the full letter.
3. Look at **Photos from the board** and **History** if you need them.
4. Under **Respond to the board**, write what you've done or plan to do, or why you think the notice is wrong.
5. To show the fix, tap [[Add a photo of the fix]] and pick a photo.
6. Tap [[Send to the board]].

## What happens next
Your response is filed on the violation, and the board is told there is a new request from your home. Their replies appear in the same conversation. You can add more at any time while the violation is open. Nothing is decided automatically: the board reviews what you sent and updates the violation's status.

## If something goes wrong
- "We couldn’t find that notice on your home.": the violation isn't on a home linked to your account. Ask the board to link you.
- "This violation is closed, so it no longer takes responses.": the board has resolved, waived or dismissed it. Send a support request if you still need to reach them.
- "Your response was sent, but the photo didn’t upload": your message reached the board. Attach the photo again under **Your photos & documents**.

## Good to know
- The letter and the board's photos appear once the board has sent the notice. Before that you see what the board noted and can still respond.
- Your response also appears under your support requests, so you can follow it there too.
- Responding doesn't pause the fix-by date. Ask the board in your response if you need more time.
