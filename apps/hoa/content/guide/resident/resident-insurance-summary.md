The board records the association's insurance policies. You can see a short summary of the ones in force, so you know what the association covers and what your own policy may need to.

## Steps
1. Tap **Documents** at the bottom of the screen.
2. Under the list, tap **Association insurance**.
3. Each policy shows its kind, for example **Master / property** or **General liability**, the insurance company, the limit and the deductible.
4. To get a copy of the certificate of insurance, tap [[Request the certificate]]. The records request form opens with **Certificate of insurance** already picked. Say what you need it for, then tap [[Send request]].

## Good to know
- Only policies in force today are listed.
- The summary never shows what the association pays for a policy or the policy number.
- The association's policies usually don't cover the inside of your home or your belongings. Ask your own insurance agent what your policy should cover.
- "No policies shared yet": the board hasn't recorded the insurance here. Tap **Ask the board** to ask them.
- The board replies to a certificate request in the request's conversation. In Georgia, from January 1, 2027, owners have the right to a copy on written demand (O.C.G.A. § 43-17A-7(2)).
