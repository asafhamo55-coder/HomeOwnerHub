Use this to find out when the board meets next, what's on the agenda, and what was decided at past meetings.

## Steps
1. Tap **Account**, then **Meetings and notices**. On **Home**, you can also tap the **Next meeting** card.
2. Under **Upcoming meetings**, each meeting shows its type (board, special, annual or emergency), its date, and the agenda if the board posted one.
3. Under **Past meetings**, tap [[Read]] to open the approved minutes of a meeting.

## What happens next
- Most meetings are posted here at least 14 days ahead. An emergency meeting can be posted later.
- The date is in your association's time zone. A meeting held today stays under **Upcoming meetings** until the day ends.
- Minutes appear only after the board approves them, usually at the next meeting. Draft minutes are never posted.
- The time and place are in the board's notice. If they aren't listed, ask the board.

## If something goes wrong
- "No upcoming meeting posted": the board hasn't scheduled the next meeting here yet.
- "No approved minutes yet": no minutes have been approved in the portal yet.
- "Meeting notices aren't in the portal yet": your association hasn't turned this page on. It can't tell you whether a meeting is scheduled. Tap [[Ask the board]].
- "Couldn't load your association's meetings": the page couldn't read the list. That doesn't mean there are no meetings. Try again in a minute.

## Good to know
- You see only your own association's meetings.
- Only the board's approved minutes are shown. Recordings, transcripts, attendance lists and closed-session notes are not.
- Need older minutes, or another record? Tap [[Ask the board]] at the bottom of the page.
