As an owner you can ask to see the association's records, such as budgets, bank statements, meeting minutes, contracts or the owner list. Send the request here so it has a date and the board can track it.

## Before you start
- Your account must be linked to your home. If it isn't, you see "No home linked yet". Ask your board to link you.

## Steps
1. Tap **Requests** at the bottom of the screen, then [[Request association records]].
2. If you have more than one home, pick the **Home**.
3. Under **Which records?**, pick the kind of records, for example **Meeting minutes**.
4. Under **Which years?**, pick **This fiscal year**, **Earlier years**, or **Both, or not sure**. For **Certificate of insurance** this question isn't asked.
5. Under **Preferred format**, pick **Electronic copies**, **Inspect in person** or **Paper copies**.
6. In **What do you need?**, say exactly which records you need, for example "The 2025 bank statements and the reserve study". Write at least 10 characters.
7. Tap [[Send request]]. The request opens so you can follow it.

## What happens next
- The board is notified of your request.
- The board replies under **Conversation** to say when and how you can see the records.
- Find your request later under **Requests**, then **Support requests**. It shows as **Records request**.

## If something goes wrong
- "Choose which records.", "Choose which years." or "Choose a format.": pick an option from the list.
- "Say which records you need, in a sentence or two.": write at least 10 characters.
- "You can only send a request for a home you own.": the home you picked isn't linked to your account. Ask your board.
- "Couldn’t send your request": nothing was sent. Try again.

## Good to know
- Your state's law may set how long the board has to answer. The board sees that deadline. Your governing documents may add their own rules.
- The board may charge for copies or ask you to inspect records in person, as your state's law and governing documents allow.
- Some details, like other owners' email addresses and phone numbers, may be left out.
- To ask for the association's certificate of insurance, pick **Certificate of insurance**, or tap [[Request the certificate]] on **Association insurance**.
- In Georgia, from January 1, 2027, every owner may inspect and get copies of the association's records on written demand. For accounting records that means the finalized balance sheet, budget, profit and loss statements and bank statements for the past three years. See **Your rights as an owner** under **Documents**.
