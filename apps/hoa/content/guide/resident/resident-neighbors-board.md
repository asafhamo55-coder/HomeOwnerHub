The community board is a message board for the people in your community and its board. Use it for things like a lost cat, a carpool or a question for the neighbors. It is not open to the public.

## Steps
1. On **Home**, tap [[Community]], then [[Talk with neighbors]].
2. To start a new conversation, find **Start a new topic**. Type a short **Title** and then **Your message**.
3. Tap [[Post topic]].
4. To join a conversation, tap its title, type in **Your reply** and tap [[Post reply]].

## What happens next
- Your topic shows at the top of the list with its number of replies.
- Neighbors see your first name and the first letter of your last name. They never see your email, address or unit.
- Board members' posts say "(board)" after the name.

## If something goes wrong
- "Give your topic a short title.": the title needs at least 3 letters.
- "Write your message first." or "Write your reply first.": the box is empty.
- "This topic is closed, so new replies are off.": the board closed it. You can still read it.
- "There is no community board for you yet.": your account is not linked to a home. Ask the board.

## Good to know
- To flag a post that is rude or does not belong, tap [[Report this post]], say what is wrong and tap [[Send report]]. The board looks at it.
- You can't edit or delete a post after you send it. Ask the board to hide it.
- The board can hide a post or close a topic. Hidden posts do not show to residents.
- For a repair or a problem with a neighbor, send the board a [support request](guide:resident-support-tickets) instead.
