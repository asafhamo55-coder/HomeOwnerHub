If you disagree with a violation notice or a proposed fine, you can ask the board for a hearing from the notice itself.

## Before you start
- Your account must be linked to your home. If it isn't, you won't see the notice. Ask your board to link you.

## Steps
1. On **My home**, select the violation.
2. Find **Hearing**.
3. Optional: under **Your statement**, write what you want the hearing to consider.
4. Select [[Request a hearing]].

## What happens next
The board schedules the hearing and sends you a written notice with the date, time and place. Once it has, they show under **Hearing** on the same page. After the hearing, the decision shows there too.

## Good to know
- You can request a hearing once per violation. To add more, use **Respond to the board** on the same page.
- A closed violation no longer takes hearing requests.
- **Request a hearing** appears once the board has sent you the violation notice.
