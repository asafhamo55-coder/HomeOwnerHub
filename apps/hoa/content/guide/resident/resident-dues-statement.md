Use this when you want to see what you were charged and paid, or need a statement for your records, a lender or a sale.

## Before you start
- Your account must be linked to your home. If it isn't, you see "No home linked yet". Ask your board to link you.

## Steps
1. Tap **Dues** at the bottom of the screen. The [[Balance]] tab shows what you owe now and your open charges, past due first.
2. Tap the [[Ledger]] tab to see every charge and payment, newest first. The top shows your total **Charges**, **Paid & credited** and **Balance**.
3. For a statement, tap [[Printable statement]] on the Ledger tab, or [[View statement]] on the Balance tab.
4. Pick [[Last 12 months]] or [[All time]].
5. Tap [[Print or save PDF]]. To keep a file, choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.

## What happens next
The statement shows your association, the statement date, your homes, and a **Balance due** (or **Credit balance**). Activity is listed oldest first. If your board takes Zelle, it ends with **How to pay**, with the memo code for each open charge.

## If something goes wrong
- "Couldn’t load your dues" or "Couldn’t load your ledger": the page couldn't read your account. Your balance is unknown, not zero. Try again in a minute.
- A payment you just sent isn't listed: payments appear after your board approves them, so anything sent in the last few days may not show yet.
- "Showing your most recent history": your account has more entries than the screen shows. Ask your board for a full statement.

## Good to know
- A minus sign means money off your balance. A balance shown as a "credit" is in your favor.
- **Last 12 months** starts with a **Balance forward** line, so older activity still counts in the total.
- A charge your board waived or wrote off shows as a credit, such as "Waived".
- If you have more than one home, each ledger row says which home it's for.
- To pay, tap the **Pay** button on the Balance tab. See [Your resident portal](guide:resident-portal-basics) for the Zelle steps.
- You can't change or dispute a charge here. Send your board a support request from **Requests** instead.
