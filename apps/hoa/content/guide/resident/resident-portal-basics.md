Everything you need is in the tabs at the bottom of the screen: Home, Dues, Requests, Ask and Account.

## Steps
### Pay your dues
1. On **Home** or **Dues**, tap the **Pay** button. It shows the amount.
2. Open Zelle in your bank's app.
3. Send to the Zelle address shown. Use [[Copy]] to copy it.
4. Enter the amount and the memo exactly as shown.

![[guide-resident-home]]

![[guide-resident-pay]]

### Ask to change your home (ARC)
1. Tap **Requests**, then [[Architectural request (ARC)]].
2. Pick the **Type of change** and write what you want to do.
3. Tap [[Submit application]].
4. Add photos or plans afterwards: open the application and tap [[Attach document]].

![[guide-resident-arc-new]]

### Report a problem
1. Tap **Requests**, then [[New support request]] for a repair or question. Tap [[Send request]].
2. Or tap [[Report a concern]] to tell the board about a rule being broken. Tap [[Send report]].

## What happens next
- Your payment is matched to your home by the memo. Your balance updates when it arrives.
- The board answers your requests in the portal. Check **Requests** to see the status.

## Good to know
- To see every charge, open **Dues**, then [[View statement]].
- **Announcements** and **Documents** are in the **Account** tab.
- Have a question about the rules? Tap **Ask** to use Ask the Docs.
- Wait for the board's written approval before you start work on your home.
