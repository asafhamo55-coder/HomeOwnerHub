The **Ask** tab is a chat assistant once your board turns it on. It can see your own account and requests, your community's rules and the announcements sent to you. It answers in a few sentences.

## Steps
1. Tap **Ask** at the bottom of the screen.
2. Tap a suggested question, like **What do I owe?** or **When is the next meeting?**, or type your own.
3. Tap [[Send]]. The answer appears under your question.
4. Ask a follow-up in the same chat. Tap [[New chat]] to start over.

## What happens next
- A rules answer lists the sections of your community's documents it used, under the answer.
- If the documents don't answer your question, the assistant says so and offers [[Ask the board]].
- [[Ask the board]] opens a new support request with your question filled in. [[Start an ARC application]] opens the ARC form with your question filled in. The form says **Suggested by the assistant — review before sending**. Check the form, change anything, then send it yourself.

## Good to know
- The assistant never sends, pays, changes or cancels anything for you. Only you can send a request.
- It is not legal advice. Only the board decides.
- It only sees your own homes, charges and requests, never your neighbours'.
- It can't see payment plan details. Ask the board about your plan.
- The chat stays in this browser tab only. Closing the tab clears it.
- You can send 20 messages an hour and 100 a day.
- The assistant is off until your board turns it on. Until then, or if your home isn't linked to your community yet, the **Ask** tab shows Ask the Docs instead. It answers one rules question at a time.
