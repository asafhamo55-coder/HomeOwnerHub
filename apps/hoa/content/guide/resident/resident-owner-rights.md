If your association is in Georgia, the portal lists the rights Georgia law gives every owner, in the law's own words. Each right links to the place in the portal where you use it.

## Before you start
- The page is for owners in a Georgia association. In any other state it says **Not available for your association**.

## Steps
1. Tap **Documents** at the bottom of the screen.
2. Under the list, tap **Your rights as an owner**.
3. Read each right. The words in quotes are the law, O.C.G.A. § 43-17A-7.
4. Tap the link under a right to use it, for example **Request records** or **Association insurance**.

## Good to know
- The law takes effect on January 1, 2027. Until then each right is marked **takes effect Jan 1, 2027**, and your governing documents and the laws in force today apply.
- Right (1) covers the association's records. For accounting records that means the finalized balance sheet, budget, profit and loss statements and bank statements for the past three years.
- Right (2) is a copy of the association's certificate of insurance. Ask for it from **Association insurance** with **Request the certificate**.
- This is general information, not legal advice. Your governing documents and other Georgia laws also apply.
