Use this when someone asks "when did the board decide that?" You don't have to open each meeting's minutes.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can open the motion log. Residents can't.
- Motions come from the minutes you've recorded. A meeting that hasn't happened yet isn't included, because its text is still an agenda.

## Steps
1. Open **Meetings** and select [[Motion log]].
2. Type a word or a name in **Search**, for example "fence" or a director's last name. You can also pick a **Year** or a **Result**. Then select **Apply**.
3. Select a meeting date to open those minutes.
4. To keep a copy, select [[Export CSV]]. The file has the same filters as the list.

## What happens next
Each row shows the motion, who moved and seconded it when the minutes say, the result and vote count, and the meeting it came from. You can also find motions with the search box (⌘K or Ctrl+K) at the top of every page.

## If something goes wrong
- A motion is missing: check that the meeting's minutes mention it. The best place is under **Decisions Made**, for example "Motion to replace the north fence, moved by Pat Lee, seconded by Sam Ortiz; passed 5-0".
- If **Download failed** shows a message, nothing was saved. Try again.

## Good to know
- Rows marked "From minutes text" were read from the wording of the minutes, not from a recorded vote. Open the minutes to check the exact record.
- "Not recorded" means the minutes don't say whether the motion passed.
- Motions from draft minutes are included and labelled **Draft**. Approve the minutes to make them final.
