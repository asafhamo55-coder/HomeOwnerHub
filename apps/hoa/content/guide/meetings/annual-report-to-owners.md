At the annual meeting the board reports to owners on the year: the money, the reserves, next year's budget and what the board decided. The annual report builds that from your books in one printable page that any owner may see.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can open the report. Residents can't open it; they get the copy you print or send.
- The report reads your accounting, so post the year's entries first. Budgets come from **Accounting**, insurance from **Insurance**, motions from approved minutes and results from the election planner.
- The meeting's type must be **Annual** for the meeting page to show the report.

## Steps
1. Open the annual meeting from **Meetings**.
2. On the **Annual report to owners** card, select [[Open annual report]].
3. Under **From the board**, write a short message: what the board did this year, what is coming, and anything owners should know.
4. Read the report, including the motions. Each motion's text comes from your approved minutes.
5. Select [[Print / Save as PDF]] to print it or save it as a PDF to hand out or attach.
6. To send it, select [[Draft message to owners]]. Messages opens with the subject and a summary filled in, addressed to owners. Fill in anything in brackets, then send it as usual.

## What happens next
Nothing is sent until you send the message from Messages. The board's message isn't saved: it stays on the page until you leave, so print or draft the message first.

## If something goes wrong
- A section says **Couldn't load**: that read failed and the rest of the report is unaffected. Reload the page before you print.
- "Accounting isn't set up": the money sections need a chart of accounts and posted entries.
- "Next year's budget isn't drafted yet": draft it under **Accounting**, then **Budget**. A draft shows as proposed, an approved budget as adopted.
- "The annual report message didn't come through": go back to the report and select [[Draft message to owners]] again in the same browser tab.

## Good to know
- The report is for every owner, so it shows totals only. Collections are a total, a count of homes and an aging split. No home, owner name or balance of any one home is shown.
- Insurance shows the insurer, limit, deductible and end date of each policy in force. It never shows the premium, the policy number or your notes.
- Election results show the seats and the names elected. Other candidates aren't listed.
- Motions are the ones that passed, from approved minutes. The names of who moved and seconded are taken out.
- A bank or routing number typed into an account's name is masked.
- The report covers the fiscal year to date. In the first 60 days of a new fiscal year it covers the whole year just ended instead, and the projection is the budget for the year that follows.
- **Georgia:** the Property Owners' Association Act says that "at the annual meeting, comprehensive reports of the affairs, finances, and budget projections of the association shall be made to the lot owners" (O.C.G.A. § 44-3-230(a)). The report quotes it. It applies to associations whose property is submitted to the Act.
- Other states get the same report without the Georgia wording. Your governing documents say what to report and when.
- The figures come from your books in HomeownerHub. They aren't audited.
