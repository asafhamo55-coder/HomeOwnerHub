Annual meetings fail on quorum far more often than on disagreement. The quorum kit helps you collect proxies before the meeting and count the room on the day.

## Before you start
- Board admins and board members can use the kit. Residents can't see it.
- The kit is for the **Annual** meeting, or a **Special** meeting where owners vote. Set the meeting's type when you schedule or edit it. A special meeting asks you to confirm that owners vote at it.
- Have your bylaws at hand. You enter the quorum percentage from them. HomeownerHub doesn't read it from your documents.

## Steps
1. Open the meeting from **Meetings** and select [[Open quorum kit]].
2. Under **Quorum**, enter the percentage from your bylaws, for example 25, and select [[Save quorum]]. Until you do, the kit can't tell you how many votes you need. In Georgia and Florida, if your bylaws are silent, you can pick the state default with one tap instead.
3. Under **Print**, select [[Sign-in sheet]] and the proxy forms. Use your browser's print dialog, or save them as a PDF. Fill in the meeting time and place on the proxy forms before you hand them out.
4. As proxies come back, find the home in the list and set **Proxy** to General or Limited.
5. Select [[Chase list]] to see the homes with no proxy and no check-in yet. Call or email them yourself. [[Copy reminder text]] copies a short reminder you can paste into a message you send.
6. On meeting day, select **Present** as each owner signs in. The counter at the top shows how many votes are represented and how many you still need.

## What happens next
Each tap is saved straight away, so another board member who opens the kit sees the same count. When the count reaches the number needed, the kit shows **Quorum reached**. Record the result in the minutes as usual.

## If something goes wrong
- The meeting has no **Quorum kit** card: its type is Regular or Emergency. Edit the meeting and set the type to Annual or Special.
- "The saved check-ins couldn't be verified": the saved list wasn't written by a board save, so the kit starts empty. Your next change replaces it.
- If you see **Not saved**, nothing changed. Try again.
- A home is missing: add it on the Properties page. Owners come from the owner roster.

## Good to know
- Each home counts once. A home with a proxy whose owner also signs in counts as present.
- The kit counts one vote per home. If your documents give homes different votes, work the final count from them.
- The printed sheet and forms show names, addresses, lots and ownership shares only. They never show an email address or phone number.
- **Georgia:** unless your declaration or bylaws say otherwise, a quorum is owners holding more than one-third of the votes, present at the start of the meeting (O.C.G.A. § 44-3-228). Your bylaws win if they set a different number. The state default counts exactly "more than one-third" of your homes.
- **Georgia proxies** (§ 44-3-224): a proxy must be dated, and can be revoked only by written notice to the association. An undated proxy is void, and so is one that says it can be revoked without that notice. The Georgia form has a date line and says so. A home owned by a company or trust must vote by proxy. If more than one owner of a home votes, they must agree.
- **Georgia, missed annual meeting:** if none is held by the end of the fiscal year, owners holding 5% of the voting power (or your bylaws' figure, never more than 25%) may call one (§ 44-3-230(b)). The kit page lists these rules and quotes the statute.
- Georgia's Nonprofit Corporation Code also covers proxies, including how long one lasts. HomeownerHub doesn't hold that text, so check your bylaws.
- Florida: § 720.306 sets a 30% quorum unless your bylaws set a lower number, and says what a valid proxy must include. The Florida form follows it. California forms put any voting directions on a separate page the proxy holder can detach. Everywhere else, check your bylaws.
- Nothing in the kit sends a message to residents.
- There's no online voting yet.
