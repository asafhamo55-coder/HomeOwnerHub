**Tasks** puts every action item from your meetings, and every follow-up made from a ticket, on one list. Each one has an owner and a due date, so the work doesn't all land on one person.

## Before you start
- Only board admins and board members can see and change tasks.
- Tasks come from meetings and tickets. To add one, open a meeting and select [[Add item]] under action items.

## Steps
1. Open **Tasks** from the sidebar. Tasks are grouped into **Overdue**, **This week**, **Later** and **Done**.
2. To see one person's tasks, pick them under **Assigned to**. Pick **My tasks** for your own. To see one meeting's tasks, pick it under **Meeting**.
3. To assign a task, pick a board member in the task's assignee box. Pick **Unassigned** to clear it.
4. To set or change the due date, pick a date in the task's date box.
5. To finish a task, select the circle next to it. Select it again to reopen the task.
6. To see where a task came from, select the meeting name under it. It opens that meeting's minutes.

## What happens next
- Your dashboard shows a **My tasks** card with how many of your tasks are overdue and due this week.
- Every morning, the daily email tells each board member how many of their own tasks are overdue or due within 7 days, with a link to their tasks. Members with nothing due get nothing.

## Good to know
- **This week** means today and the next 6 days. A task due today is not overdue yet.
- A task with no due date sits under **Later**.
- **Done** shows the 50 most recently finished tasks. Cancelled tasks show there too.
- Only board members and admins can be assigned from this list. If someone typed a name on the meeting page instead, that name still shows, but that person gets no email and no **My tasks** count.
- Residents never see tasks, and nothing here is sent to residents.
