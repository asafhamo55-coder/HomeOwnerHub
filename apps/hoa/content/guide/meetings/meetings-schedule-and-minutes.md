Schedule the meeting, bring the board packet, then file the minutes when it is over.

## Steps
### Schedule a meeting
1. Open **Meetings** from the sidebar and select [[Schedule meeting]].
2. Pick the **Date** and the **Type** of meeting.
3. Add the **Agenda**: topics to discuss and documents to review.
4. Select [[Schedule meeting]] to save it.

![[guide-meetings-list]]

### Print the board packet
1. Open the meeting and select [[Board packet]].
2. Select [[Print / Save as PDF]].

![[guide-meetings-packet]]

### File the minutes
1. On the Meetings page, select [[Record minutes]].
2. Enter the meeting date, type and attendees, and paste the **Transcript**.
3. Select [[Generate minutes]]. The AI drafts the minutes.
4. Read the draft, fix anything wrong, and select [[Approve & file minutes]].

## What happens next
Upcoming meetings show under **Upcoming** on the Meetings page. Filed minutes show under **Past meetings**. To track follow-ups, open a meeting and select [[Add item]] under action items.

## Good to know
- The board packet holds the agenda, last meeting's action items, the income statement, balance sheet, budget against actual, who is behind on dues, open violations and open ARC applications. It lists delinquent homes, so it is for the board only. It is never sent from the app.
- Scheduling a meeting does not notify anyone. Send a message from Communications.
- Filed minutes are saved as their own meeting record. They are not added to a meeting you scheduled earlier.
- Action items are not pulled from the minutes. Add them yourself.
- Have minutes from before you used HomeownerHub? Use [[Record minutes]] and paste them in.
