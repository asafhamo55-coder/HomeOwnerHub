Tell every owner when and where the meeting is, and what is on the agenda. The notice opens ready to check. Nothing goes out until you select **Send**.

## Steps
1. Open **Meetings** and select the scheduled meeting.
2. Under **Notice to owners**, select [[Send meeting notice]].
3. The message opens on the **Meeting notice** template, addressed to all owners. The type, date and agenda are filled in from the meeting.
4. Read the notice-period box at the top. It says whether a notice sent today gives enough notice.
5. On the Message step, enter the **Start time** and **Where**.
6. Review the message and select **Send**.

## What happens next
- Owners get the notice by email and in the resident portal.
- Under your message, the notice adds a link to download a calendar invite, plus a link to the agenda in the resident portal. Owners sign in to the portal to open it.
- The meeting page shows **Notice sent on** and the date. Select the date to see who it reached.

## Good to know
- **Florida:** a board meeting notice must be posted in the community at least 48 hours ahead (Fla. Stat. § 720.303(2)(c)1). If you mail or deliver it instead of posting it, give 7 days. Special assessments or parcel-use rule changes need 14 days (§ 720.303(2)(c)2). Owners' meetings need 14 days (§ 720.306(5)).
- **California:** for an annual meeting where directors are elected, § 5115 sets the election notice and ballot deadlines. For other meeting notice periods, check your bylaws.
- **Georgia:** owners' meetings need 21 days' notice for the annual meeting and 7 days for any other (O.C.G.A. § 44-3-230(a)). For board meetings, check your bylaws.
- **Georgia condominiums:** the same 21 and 7 days come from the Georgia Condominium Act (§ 44-3-102), and every notice must state the time, place and purpose of the meeting.
- **Texas and other states:** the notice period isn't loaded in HomeownerHub. Check your bylaws.
- The box is general information, not legal advice.
- The calendar invite contains only your association's name, the meeting type, date, time and place. It holds no owner's details.
- Sent it already? The button changes to [[Send another notice]].
