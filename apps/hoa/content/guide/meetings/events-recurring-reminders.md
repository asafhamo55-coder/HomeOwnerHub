Use this for dates that come around every year, like the annual meeting, the insurance renewal, a state filing or a yearly inspection. The app sends a reminder ahead of each one, so nobody has to remember.

## Before you start
- Only board admins and board members can add and change events.
- Have the date of the next time it happens.

## Steps
1. Open **Events** from the sidebar and select [[New event]].
2. Enter a **Title**, and a **Description** if you want to note who is responsible.
3. Pick the **Next occurrence** date. Under **Repeats**, choose **Every year** or **Does not repeat**.
4. Set the **Lead time**: how many days before the date the reminder goes out. It's 7 unless you change it. 0 sends on the day itself.
5. Under **How to notify**, tick **Email**, **SMS** and/or **In-app portal**.
6. Under **Who to notify**, choose **All community**, **Board** or **Specific resident**. For the board, tick the members you want, or use [[Select all]].
7. Select [[Create event]].

## What happens next
The app checks events every morning. When an event's lead time arrives, it sends the reminder to the people and channels you picked. The event page shows **Next reminder** and **Last reminder sent**.

For a yearly event, once the date passes the event moves forward a year, and next year's reminder goes out the same way. A one-time event moves to **Past** after its date.

## If something goes wrong
- "Pick the date of the next occurrence." or "Give the event a title.": fill in the missing field.
- "Use a whole number from 0 to 90.": the lead time must be between 0 and 90 days.
- "Pick at least one notification channel." or "Pick at least one board member, or choose Select all.": tick at least one box.
- "No recipients available to alert. Check the event audience has contact info on file.": the people you picked have no email or phone on file.
- If a reminder fails to send 3 times, the app stops trying for that date and puts a notification in front of every board member and admin.

## Good to know
- To check it works, open the event's **More actions** menu and choose [[Send test alert now]]. This counts as this year's reminder, so the scheduled one won't go out again for this date.
- To stop reminders for a while, select [[Pause reminders]]. Select [[Resume reminders]] to turn them back on.
- To change an event, open it, edit the form below the details and select [[Save changes]].
- SMS is skipped for board members and admins, because no phone number is on file for those roles.
- If the app missed a reminder, it still sends it late rather than skipping it.
- Deleting an event removes the reminder and its history. You can't undo it.
- Events are reminders only. To schedule a board meeting with an agenda and minutes, use **Meetings**.
