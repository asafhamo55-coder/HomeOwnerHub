Florida law sets how far ahead a meeting's notice must be posted: 14 days before an owners' meeting and 48 hours before a board meeting. Record the date here and the compliance check uses it.

## Steps
1. Open **Meetings** and select the meeting.
2. Under **Details**, find **Notice posted**.
3. Posted it just now? Select [[Mark notice posted]].
4. Posted it earlier? Select [[Enter a date]], pick the date and time, and select **Save**.

## What happens next
The Florida records and notices check on **State Law** measures the notice against the meeting date you recorded.

## Good to know
- To fix a wrong date, select [[Change date]]. To remove it, select **Clear**.
- A notice can't be dated in the future.
- Recording the date does not post or send anything. Post the notice the way your association always does.
