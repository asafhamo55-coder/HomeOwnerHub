Ask the Docs and Covenant Brain cite your community's own rules, so they need your documents first.

## Steps
1. Open **Documents** from the sidebar, then the [[Governing documents]] tab.
2. Give the document a **Title** and pick its **Document type**, such as Declaration / CC&Rs or Bylaws.
3. Choose [[Upload file]] for a PDF, text or Markdown file, or [[Paste text]] to paste the words in.
4. Select [[Upload & index]] and wait while it shows **Indexing…**.
5. To ask a question, open [[Ask the Docs]] from the sidebar, type your question and select [[Ask]].

![[guide-documents-governing]]

## What happens next
The document appears under **Loaded documents** with the number of sections found and an **Indexed** badge. Ask the Docs can now answer from it, and Covenant Brain can cite it in violation letters.

![[guide-ask-docs]]

## If something goes wrong
- "We could not extract text from this PDF.": the PDF is probably a scan. Use [[Paste text]] instead.
- "Upload failed": nothing was saved. Try again in a moment.

## Good to know
- Ask the Docs answers from your documents and names the section it used. When your documents don't cover a question, it is told to say so rather than guess.
- Ask the Docs answers what **your rules** say. This Help guide explains **how to use HomeownerHub**.
- Scanned PDFs without text can't be read. There is no text recognition.
- You can't open, edit or delete a document from this page yet.
