The **Georgia registration kit** helps the board register the association with the Georgia Secretary of State, as SB 406 requires from Jan 1, 2027. It also keeps the board's record of each filing.

## Before you start
- Only board members and admins see the kit. It's for Georgia associations.
- Upload your declaration, bylaws and rules under **Documents**, so the checklist can find them.
- Have your corporate records to hand, for the mailing address, the registered agent and the officers' titles.

## Steps
1. Open [[Compliance]] in the sidebar, then select **Open the registration kit** on the registration row.
2. Under **Decide: register, or opt out**, read the statute text, then select [[We will register]] or [[We opted out in writing]].
3. Under **What the filing needs**, check the name, officers and number of units HomeownerHub filled in. Type the mailing address and registered agent, then select [[Save to the sheet]].
4. Tick off the governing documents, and run the balance sheet and income statement from the links.
5. Select [[Print / Save as PDF]] to print the checklist and fill-in sheet. Write in the officers' titles by hand.
6. File with the Secretary of State.
7. Under **Record the filing**, enter the day you filed and the confirmation number, then select [[Record the registration]]. Each year, record the renewal the same way.

## What happens next
- The kit shows **Registered**, with the day and the year the registration covers.
- On **Compliance**, the registration row shows done, and the renewal row shows the next Dec 31.
- After an election, record its results in the election planner. An amendment row appears under **Amendments**. Select [[Record the amended filing]] once you've filed, or [[Officers didn’t change]] if nothing changed. Either marks the row done on **Compliance**.
- Every change goes in the activity log, with no dates, numbers or names.

## If something goes wrong
- "The filing day can’t be in the future.": enter the day you actually filed.
- "Use letters, numbers, spaces and - / . # only": type the confirmation number as it appears on the receipt.
- "The stored record can’t be verified": something changed the record outside HomeownerHub. Record your filings again. Until then the association counts as not registered.
- "Couldn’t load the board roster" or "Couldn’t load your documents": a loading problem. Fill those lines in by hand, or refresh the page.

## Good to know
- **Before Jan 1, 2027**, HomeownerHub warns when the board levies a fine, drafts a collections letter that asks for fines or fees, or records a lien, and the registration isn't recorded.
- **From Jan 1, 2027**, those steps are blocked until a current registration is recorded here, and the nightly late fee is skipped. The board can go ahead only on the attorney's advice, and that override is logged.
- A letter that asks only for dues isn't blocked. The statute names fines and fees.
- Each registration expires on December 31. If no renewal is recorded for the new year, the kit shows the registration as lapsed, and the block returns.
- If the board opted out, HomeownerHub treats fines, fees and accelerated assessments as barred.
- The statute text HomeownerHub holds asks for the name, address and officers. It doesn't mention a registered agent or the number of units. The kit lists them because the Secretary of State's form may ask. Check the form when you file.
- The kit doesn't file anything for you. It isn't legal advice. Check the Secretary of State's guidance and ask your association's attorney.
