Owners always see your governing documents and rules. Anything else stays with the board until you post it.

## Steps
### A document from the Documents page
1. Open **Documents** and select the document.
2. Under **Owners**, turn on [[Visible to owners]].

### A governing document
1. Open **Documents**, then the governing documents tab.
2. In the **Owners** column, turn on **Visible to owners** for the document.

## What happens next
Owners can open and download the document from **Documents** in their portal. Turn the switch off to take it down.

## Good to know
- The Declaration, CC&Rs, Bylaws, Rules, amendments and policies are always visible to owners. They have no switch.
- A document the AI drafted is never shown to owners.
- An older version replaced by a newer one is not shown to owners.
