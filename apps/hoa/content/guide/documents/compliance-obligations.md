**Compliance** lists every state-law obligation HomeownerHub tracks for your association, says where each one stands, and keeps the document that proves the board met it.

## Before you start
- Only board members and admins see the page.
- The list depends on your association's state in its setup. Georgia has the longest list.
- Upload the proof first, for example the Secretary of State receipt, under **Documents**.

## Steps
1. Open [[Compliance]] in the sidebar.
2. Read the red banner at the top, if there is one. It lists what's overdue. Select an item to jump to it.
3. Under each obligation, read its status, the steps, and **What the statute says**. That's the law's own words.
4. For a deadline you've met, pick the proof under **Evidence**, then select [[Mark as done]].
5. To add or change the proof later, pick another document and select [[Save evidence]].

## What happens next
- The obligation shows **Done** with the date. The evidence links to the document.
- Marking it done here also clears it from the dashboard's State law deadlines card and the morning email.
- The monthly board report has a **State law compliance** section: what's overdue, what's coming up and the most pressing item. The board packet lists every obligation with its status.

## If something goes wrong
- "That document isn’t in your association’s documents.": pick a document from your own Documents.
- "Mark it as done first, then attach the evidence.": the deadline isn't done yet. Select [[Mark as done]] instead.
- "Couldn’t load your meetings": a loading problem, not "no meeting". Refresh the page.

## Good to know
- **Georgia:** registering with the Secretary of State, then renewing every year and amending after an officer change. Also the annual members' meeting with 21 days' notice, minutes and financial records, and a statement of amounts due within five business days. The last three come from the Property Owners' Association Act, which covers property submitted to it. If yours isn't, check your governing documents.
- **Texas:** the management certificate. **Florida:** director education certificates (tracked on Members). **California:** the annual budget report window (from the budget's adoption checklist).
- Director election dates aren't tracked yet, and Georgia's condominium rules aren't loaded. The page says "check your governing documents" for those.
- The page quotes statutes word for word. It isn't legal advice. Ask your association's attorney.
