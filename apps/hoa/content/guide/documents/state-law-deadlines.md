The **State law deadlines** card on your dashboard shows fixed legal deadlines that apply to your association because of its state. Each one cites the law it comes from, says what's at stake, and lists what to do.

## Before you start
- Only board members and admins see the card. Nothing about it is sent to residents.
- The card uses your association's state from its setup. It shows nothing for a state with no deadline on the list.
- **Georgia:** register with the Secretary of State. Georgia's 2026 "Property Owners' Bill of Rights Act" (SB 406) adds O.C.G.A. § 43-17A-2, which takes effect **January 1, 2027**. From that day, an association that isn't registered can't collect fines or fees, file or record liens, or start foreclosure.
- **Texas:** keep your management certificate recorded with the county and filed with the Texas Real Estate Commission (Property Code § 209.004).

## Steps
1. Open the **Dashboard**. The **State law deadlines** card shows at the top when a deadline applies.
2. Read the countdown, for example "Takes effect in 73 days (Jan 1, 2027)", and the steps under it.
3. Open the official link on the card for the forms and the filing date. For Georgia it goes to the Secretary of State's Property Owners' Associations Division. For Texas it goes to TREC's management certificate site.
4. Do the steps. For Georgia, the board decides whether to register or to file a written notice opting out. An association that opts out can't charge fines or fees at all.
5. When it's done, select [[Mark as done]] on that deadline.

## What happens next
- The deadline disappears from the card and from the morning email for every board member of that association. The app records who marked it done and when.
- Until then, a Georgia association's morning email has a countdown line from 90 days before the date, and keeps it after the date passes. The Texas certificate has no date, so it stays on the card only and isn't in the email.

## If something goes wrong
- "Not saved" with "This can’t be saved on this server yet. Contact support.": nothing was recorded, and the deadline still shows. Contact support.
- "That deadline doesn’t apply to this association’s state.": the association's state in its setup isn't the deadline's state. Check the state in your association's setup.
- "Couldn’t load your state law deadlines": a loading problem, not "nothing due". Refresh the page.

## Good to know
- Informational, not legal advice. Confirm the details with your association's attorney.
- Georgia's registration is yearly. Each registration expires December 31 and must be renewed by then. A change of name, address or officers needs an amended statement within 30 days. The card covers the first registration only, so put the renewal on your own calendar.
- The Secretary of State says registration opens on or after January 1, 2027 through an online portal. Check its page for the exact date.
- Other states' deadlines, such as Florida's milestone inspections and California's balcony inspections, aren't on the list. Florida's apply to condominium and cooperative buildings, not HOAs under chapter 720, and the California text isn't in our sources yet.
- Every association also sees two federal tax dates, Form 1099-NEC and Form 1120-H, whatever its state. They link to the year-end package. See [Download the year-end package](guide:accounting-year-end-cpa).
- To read your state's HOA law itself, use [State Law](guide:legal-state-law-compliance).
