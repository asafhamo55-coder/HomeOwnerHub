# DECLARATION OF COVENANTS, CONDITIONS, AND RESTRICTIONS
# Creek Valley Community Association, Inc.

**RECORDED:** Cobb County, Georgia — Deed Book 14823, Page 0117
**EFFECTIVE DATE:** March 1, 2014 (as amended through January 15, 2025)
**DECLARANT:** Creek Valley Development, LLC
**ASSOCIATION:** Creek Valley Community Association, Inc., a Georgia nonprofit corporation

---

**RECITALS**

WHEREAS, Declarant is the owner of certain real property located in Cobb County, Georgia, consisting of approximately 47.3 acres subdivided into one hundred thirty-five (135) residential Lots, together with Common Areas including a Clubhouse, swimming pool, two (2) tennis courts, walking trails, and stormwater facilities; and

WHEREAS, Declarant desires to subject said property to the provisions of the Georgia Property Owners' Association Act, O.C.G.A. § 44-3-220 et seq. (the "Act"), and to establish a uniform plan for the development, improvement, and maintenance of the Property;

NOW, THEREFORE, Declarant hereby declares that the Property described herein shall be held, sold, conveyed, and occupied subject to the following covenants, conditions, restrictions, easements, and liens, which shall run with the land and be binding on all parties having any right, title, or interest in the Property.

---

## Article I — Definitions

As used in this Declaration, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below. Capitalized terms not otherwise defined herein shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Act.

**1.1 "Association"** means Creek Valley Community Association, Inc., a Georgia nonprofit corporation, its successors and assigns.

**1.2 "ARC"** means the Architectural Review Committee established pursuant to Article III.

**1.3 "Board"** means the Board of Directors of the Association.

**1.4 "Common Area"** means all real property owned by the Association for the common use and enjoyment of the Owners, including the Clubhouse, pool, tennis courts, entrance monuments, landscaped islands, walking trails, retention ponds, and private streets, if any.

**1.5 "Lot"** means any numbered plot of land shown on the recorded subdivision plat, intended for residential use, excluding Common Area.

**1.6 "Member"** means every Owner of a Lot who is entitled to membership in the Association.

**1.7 "Owner"** means the record holder of fee simple title to any Lot, excluding mortgagees and those holding title merely as security.

**1.8 "Property"** means the real property described in Section 2.1, together with such additions as may hereafter be brought within the jurisdiction of the Association.

**1.9 "Residence"** or "Dwelling" means the single-family detached home constructed upon a Lot.

## Article II — Property Subject to This Declaration

### Section 2.1 — Description of Property

The real property which is, and shall be, held, transferred, sold, conveyed, and occupied subject to this Declaration is located in Land Lots 423 and 424 of the 17th District, 2nd Section, Cobb County, Georgia, and is more particularly described on the plat recorded in Plat Book 287, Pages 14–19, Cobb County records, comprising one hundred thirty-five (135) residential Lots numbered 1 through 135, together with the Common Areas designated thereon. All Lots are restricted exclusively to single-family residential use, and no Lot shall be subdivided, replatted, or recombined without the prior written consent of the Board and compliance with all applicable Cobb County ordinances. Each Owner, by acceptance of a deed to a Lot, becomes a Member of the Association and is bound by this Declaration, the Bylaws, and the Rules and Regulations duly adopted by the Board.

### Section 2.2 — Additions to Property

Additional real property may be annexed to and brought within the jurisdiction of the Association by the Declarant during the Development Period (defined as the period ending December 31, 2024, or upon conveyance of the last Lot owned by Declarant, whichever occurs first) without the consent of Members. After expiration of the Development Period, annexation of additional property shall require the affirmative vote of two-thirds (2/3) of the Members present and voting at a meeting duly called for such purpose, in accordance with O.C.G.A. § 44-3-225. Any annexation shall be effected by the recording of a Supplemental Declaration in the Cobb County records describing the additional property and any modifications to the covenants applicable thereto.

## Article III — Architectural Standards

### Section 3.1 — Architectural Review Committee

The Board shall appoint an Architectural Review Committee ("ARC") consisting of three (3) to five (5) members, who shall serve at the pleasure of the Board. At least a majority of ARC members shall be Owners residing within the Community. The ARC shall be responsible for reviewing all applications for exterior modifications, additions, alterations, and new construction within the Property, and for adopting and publishing Architectural Guidelines consistent with this Declaration. The ARC shall act on complete applications within thirty (30) days of submission; failure to act within such period shall constitute approval of the application as submitted, provided the application complies with the published Architectural Guidelines. ARC decisions may be appealed to the Board within fifteen (15) days of issuance.

### Section 3.2 — Approval Required for Modifications

No building, fence, wall, deck, patio, pool, exterior paint color change, roof replacement, driveway alteration, landscape feature exceeding 24 inches in height, or other structure or exterior modification shall be commenced, erected, placed, or altered on any Lot until plans and specifications have been submitted to and approved in writing by the ARC. Routine maintenance, interior alterations not affecting the exterior appearance, and replacement of existing items with materials of substantially identical color, dimension, and material do not require ARC approval. Commencing work without prior approval constitutes a violation of this Declaration and may result in fines, orders to remove or restore at the Owner's expense, and other enforcement action under Article IX.

### Section 3.3 — Submission Requirements

Applications to the ARC shall be made on the form prescribed by the Committee and shall include, at minimum: (a) a site plan showing the location of the proposed improvement on the Lot, with setbacks dimensioned; (b) elevations and exterior renderings; (c) a complete description of materials, colors, and finishes, with manufacturer samples or color chips where applicable; (d) the name and contact information of the contractor, if any; and (e) the estimated start and completion dates. Incomplete applications shall not start the thirty (30) day review period. The ARC may request reasonable additional information. Approved work must commence within six (6) months and be completed within twelve (12) months of approval, after which the approval shall lapse and re-submission shall be required.

### Section 3.4 — Fences and Walls

Fences shall be permitted on Lots subject to ARC approval. The maximum permitted fence height is six feet (6'-0") measured from finished grade. Approved fencing materials are: (i) cedar or pressure-treated wood in shadowbox, board-on-board, or stockade style; (ii) black or dark-bronze aluminum or wrought-iron picket; and (iii) vinyl-coated chain link only in rear yards screened from street view. Chain-link is prohibited in front yards and on any street-facing side yard. No fence shall be installed forward of the rear corners of the Residence except for decorative split-rail fencing not exceeding 3 feet in height, subject to ARC review. Fences enclosing pools must comply with Cobb County code. Retaining walls exceeding 24 inches require ARC approval and engineered drawings.

### Section 3.5 — Exterior Paint and Materials

All exterior paint and stain colors must be selected from the ARC-approved palette, available from the Association upon request. Owners proposing a color not on the palette must submit a sample for ARC review. Permitted siding materials are brick, stone, fiber-cement (Hardie-style), and stucco; aluminum and vinyl siding are prohibited except for soffits, fascia, and trim. Roofs shall be architectural-grade asphalt shingles in weathered-wood, charcoal, or slate-gray tones; metal roofs are permitted only with ARC approval and only in standing-seam matte finishes. Front doors, shutters, and garage doors may use a wider range of accent colors subject to ARC review. Reflective or mirrored exterior films on windows are prohibited.

### Section 3.6 — Decks, Patios, and Outdoor Structures

Decks, patios, screened porches, pergolas, gazebos, and similar outdoor structures require ARC approval. Decks shall be constructed of pressure-treated lumber, cedar, or composite materials in earth-tone colors complementary to the Residence. Detached structures such as gazebos and pergolas shall not exceed twelve feet (12') in height and shall be located within the rear yard, set back at least ten feet (10') from any property line. Storage sheds are limited to one (1) per Lot, shall not exceed one hundred forty-four (144) square feet, shall be located in the rear yard behind the Residence, and must match the Residence in siding material and roof color. Outdoor playsets and trampolines must be located in rear yards and shall not exceed twelve feet (12') in height.

### Section 3.7 — Mailboxes and Address Numbers

All mailboxes within the Community shall be of uniform design, color, and post style as specified by the ARC (currently the "Madison Black" cast-aluminum design with black 4x4 wood post). Replacement mailboxes shall be purchased from the Association's designated vendor at the Owner's expense. Address numbers shall be displayed on the mailbox in white reflective vinyl, four (4) inches in height, and shall also be displayed on the Residence in a manner consistent with neighboring homes. Decorative flags, signs, or attachments to mailboxes are prohibited except for U.S. flags and seasonal decorations not exceeding 30 days in display per season. Damaged mailboxes must be repaired or replaced within thirty (30) days of damage.

### Section 3.8 — Satellite Dishes and Antennae

In accordance with the Federal Communications Commission's Over-the-Air Reception Devices Rule (47 C.F.R. § 1.4000), Owners may install satellite dishes one meter (39.4 inches) or less in diameter and over-the-air television antennae, subject to reasonable placement preferences enforced by the ARC. Owners are strongly encouraged, but not required, to locate such devices on the rear roof or in a rear-yard location not visible from the street where reception is not impaired. Devices exceeding one meter, large amateur-radio towers, and rooftop commercial antennae are prohibited absent ARC approval. Cabling shall be routed in a manner that is concealed, painted to match the Residence, or otherwise minimized visually to the extent feasible.

## Article IV — Property Maintenance

### Section 4.1 — Lawn and Landscape Standards

Each Owner shall maintain the Lot, including all landscaping, in a neat, healthy, and attractive condition consistent with the overall character of the Community. Lawns shall be mowed at a height not exceeding four (4) inches during the growing season (March 15 through October 31), and edged along driveways, walkways, and curbs. Dead or diseased trees, shrubs, and turf shall be removed or replaced within forty-five (45) days of notice from the Association. Weeds in landscape beds, mulch areas, and along curblines shall be controlled. No artificial turf, gravel front yards, or vegetable gardens in front yards are permitted. Tree removal of any tree exceeding six (6) inches in caliper requires ARC approval except in cases of imminent hazard, in which case notice shall be provided to the ARC within seven (7) days.

### Section 4.2 — Building Exterior Maintenance

Owners shall maintain the exterior of the Residence and all improvements on the Lot in good repair, including but not limited to: roofs, gutters, downspouts, siding, brick, stucco, trim, windows, doors, shutters, decks, fences, driveways, and walkways. Surfaces shall be pressure-washed as necessary to remove mildew, algae, and staining, and repainted or restained when the existing finish becomes faded, peeling, or weathered. Driveways shall be kept free of significant cracks, oil stains, and surface deterioration. Window screens shall be intact. The Association may, after notice and opportunity to cure pursuant to Article IX, perform required maintenance and assess the cost to the Owner as a Specific Assessment which shall constitute a lien on the Lot in accordance with O.C.G.A. § 44-3-232.

### Section 4.3 — Holiday Decorations

Seasonal and holiday decorations are encouraged as expressions of community spirit. However, to maintain neighborhood appearance, decorations shall be subject to the following limitations: (a) winter-holiday decorations may be displayed from the day after Thanksgiving through January 15; (b) Halloween decorations may be displayed from October 1 through November 7; (c) other holiday-specific decorations may be displayed no earlier than 30 days before and no later than 14 days after the holiday; (d) decorations shall be maintained in good condition (no torn inflatables, burned-out bulbs not replaced within 14 days); and (e) decorations shall not produce sound audible beyond the property line after 10:00 p.m. Owners displaying decorations outside these windows shall be subject to enforcement under Article IX.

## Article V — Use Restrictions

### Section 5.1 — Vehicles and Parking

All Lots are intended for the parking of operable, currently registered passenger vehicles. Vehicles shall be parked in garages or on driveways. Overnight on-street parking is prohibited from 11:00 p.m. to 6:00 a.m. except for short-term guest parking not exceeding three (3) consecutive nights. Inoperable, unregistered, or wrecked vehicles; commercial vehicles bearing signage or exceeding three-quarter (¾) ton rating; boats; trailers; campers; recreational vehicles; and tractors shall not be parked or stored on any Lot or street except (i) within a fully enclosed garage or (ii) for loading and unloading not exceeding 48 hours. Repair of vehicles on driveways or streets, other than minor emergency repairs, is prohibited. Persistent violators are subject to towing at the Owner's expense after notice.

### Section 5.2 — Trash and Refuse

Garbage and refuse shall be kept in closed, animal-resistant containers and stored within garages or in screened locations not visible from the street except during the period from sundown the evening before collection until sundown the day of collection. Empty containers shall be returned to their concealed storage location by sundown on collection day. No accumulation of trash, debris, yard waste, construction materials, or junk shall be permitted on any Lot. Yard-waste piles shall be removed within seven (7) days of generation. Bulk items awaiting special pickup shall be placed at the curb no earlier than 24 hours before scheduled pickup. The burning of trash, leaves, or other refuse is prohibited except for recreational fires in approved fire pits.

### Section 5.3 — Pets

No more than three (3) dogs and three (3) cats, or a combination not exceeding four (4) total domestic pets weighing over ten pounds, may be kept on any Lot. No livestock, poultry (including backyard chickens), exotic animals, or animals kept for commercial breeding purposes shall be permitted. Pets shall not create a nuisance through excessive barking, odor, or aggressive behavior. All dogs outside of a fenced area shall be on a leash held by a person able to control the animal. Owners shall promptly remove and dispose of pet waste from Common Areas and other Owners' Lots. Pets confirmed to be dangerous, as determined by the Board after hearing, may be ordered permanently removed from the Community. All pets must be licensed as required by Cobb County.

### Section 5.4 — Signs

No signs of any kind shall be displayed on any Lot or Common Area except: (a) one (1) "For Sale" or "For Rent" sign not exceeding six (6) square feet, placed on the Lot offered for sale or rent; (b) one (1) security-system sign not exceeding one (1) square foot per company; (c) political signs displayed during the period beginning forty-five (45) days before and ending seven (7) days after any election, not exceeding four (4) square feet in size and limited to two (2) signs per Lot, consistent with O.C.G.A. § 44-5-60.1; (d) contractor signs during active work, removed within seven (7) days of completion; and (e) standard address and house-numbering signs. Commercial advertising, garage-sale signs (other than during the annual Community Yard Sale), and signs displayed on Common Areas without Board approval are prohibited.

### Section 5.5 — Short-Term Rentals (Prohibited)

No Lot or Residence shall be leased, rented, or otherwise occupied for a term of less than one hundred eighty (180) consecutive days. The use of any Lot or Residence as a short-term rental, vacation rental, transient lodging, bed-and-breakfast, hotel, or for similar purposes through platforms such as Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, or any successor service is expressly prohibited. All leases must be in writing, must require the tenant to comply with this Declaration and the Rules and Regulations, and must be provided to the Association within fifteen (15) days of execution. The Owner remains jointly and severally liable with the tenant for any violations. Violation of this Section may result in fines of up to $500 per day, in addition to all other remedies available under Article IX and applicable Georgia law.

## Article VI — Nuisance and Quiet Enjoyment

### Section 6.1 — Quiet Hours

Quiet hours within the Community shall be observed from 10:00 p.m. through 7:00 a.m. daily. During quiet hours, no Owner, tenant, guest, or invitee shall produce noise audible beyond the boundaries of the Lot, including but not limited to: amplified music, loud television or stereo, motorized landscape equipment (mowers, blowers, edgers, chainsaws), construction or remodeling activities, recreational vehicles, sustained dog barking, or party noise. Pool and Common Area amenities close to general use at 10:00 p.m. unless an event has been pre-approved by the Board. New Year's Eve and Independence Day are recognized exceptions for which quiet hours shall be observed from midnight to 7:00 a.m.

### Section 6.2 — Nuisance Prohibited

No noxious, offensive, dangerous, or unsightly activity shall be conducted upon any Lot, nor shall anything be done thereon that may become a nuisance or annoyance to the Community. Without limiting the foregoing, the following are deemed nuisances: persistent foul odors; unscreened junk, debris, or inoperable equipment visible from the street; sustained dog barking exceeding fifteen (15) minutes; chronic outdoor cigarette/cigar smoke drifting onto neighboring Lots; outdoor lighting that shines directly into a neighboring Residence; security cameras aimed primarily at a neighbor's interior windows or fully enclosed yard; and use of fireworks (except sparklers and similar non-aerial devices on Independence Day and New Year's Eve, consistent with Georgia law). The Board shall have discretion to determine whether an activity constitutes a nuisance after notice and hearing.

## Article VII — Assessments

### Section 7.1 — Annual Assessments

Each Lot shall be subject to an Annual Assessment in an amount established by the Board prior to the start of each fiscal year, sufficient to fund the operating budget, reserves, and required contributions to the Common Area. The Annual Assessment for the fiscal year beginning January 1, 2026 is set at Three Thousand Five Hundred Eighty-Eight Dollars ($3,588.00) per Lot, payable in monthly installments of $299.00 due on the first of each month. Owners may elect to pay quarterly or annually via the Association's payment portal. The Board may increase the Annual Assessment by up to ten percent (10%) per year without Member approval; increases exceeding 10% require approval of a majority of Members voting at a duly called meeting, pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 44-3-225.

### Section 7.2 — Special Assessments

In addition to Annual Assessments, the Board may levy Special Assessments for: (a) capital improvements; (b) unanticipated repairs to Common Area; (c) shortfalls in operating budget; or (d) other extraordinary expenses. Special Assessments exceeding one-third (1/3) of the Annual Assessment per Lot in any fiscal year require the approval of two-thirds (2/3) of Members present and voting at a meeting duly called for such purpose, with at least 21 days' written notice. Special Assessments shall be allocated equally among all Lots and are payable on the schedule set by the Board, but no less than 30 days after notice. Specific Assessments may also be levied against an individual Lot for costs incurred by the Association as a result of the Owner's violation, pursuant to Section 4.2 and O.C.G.A. § 44-3-232.

### Section 7.3 — Late Fees and Lien Rights

Assessments not paid within ten (10) days of the due date shall be deemed delinquent. A late fee of Twenty-Five Dollars ($25.00) per month shall be charged for each month or portion thereof that an assessment remains unpaid, in addition to interest at the rate of ten percent (10%) per annum on the outstanding balance. All unpaid assessments, late fees, interest, attorney's fees, and costs of collection shall constitute a continuing lien on the Lot from the date such assessment becomes due, in accordance with O.C.G.A. § 44-3-232. The Association may, after providing notice and opportunity to cure as required by law, record a lien in the Cobb County records and may foreclose such lien in the manner provided by law for the foreclosure of mortgages. The Association may also suspend voting rights and amenity privileges of delinquent Owners pursuant to Section 9.4.

## Article VIII — Common Areas

### Section 8.1 — Pool and Clubhouse

The Clubhouse, swimming pool, tennis courts, and walking trails are for the exclusive, non-transferable use of Owners in good standing, members of their household, and accompanied guests, subject to the Rules and Regulations adopted by the Board. The pool is generally open from Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day weekend, daily from 10:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., or as posted. Children under fourteen (14) must be accompanied by a responsible person sixteen (16) or older. Guests are limited to four (4) per household per visit, and the Owner must be present unless a guest pass is issued. The Clubhouse may be reserved for private events by Members in good standing pursuant to the rental policy and fee schedule adopted by the Board. Alcohol is permitted in moderation; glass containers are prohibited in the pool area.

### Section 8.2 — Common Area Use Restrictions

No Owner shall use the Common Area in any manner that interferes with the use and enjoyment of others, that damages the property, or that violates applicable law. The following are prohibited: unauthorized commercial activity; smoking within Clubhouse buildings or within twenty-five (25) feet of pool decking; pets within the pool fence (except service animals); motorized vehicles on walking trails or grass areas; amplified sound systems without Board approval; fishing in retention ponds; and removal or damage to plantings, signage, or equipment. Owners are responsible for any damage caused by themselves, their family members, tenants, or guests, and may be assessed the cost of repair. Hours of access to all Common Areas other than walking trails shall be 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. unless otherwise posted.

## Article IX — Enforcement

### Section 9.1 — Notice and Cure Process

Upon a determination by the Board, Management, or its designee that an Owner has violated this Declaration, the Bylaws, or the Rules and Regulations, the Association shall provide written notice to the Owner at the address on file. The notice shall identify the alleged violation, the section violated, the action required to cure, and the deadline for cure. Standard violations shall be subject to a cure period of fourteen (14) days from the date of notice. Violations of Article III (Architectural Standards) shall be subject to a cure period of thirty (30) days, in recognition of the time typically required for design, permitting, or contractor work. The Board may extend cure periods for good cause shown. Failure to cure within the prescribed period shall result in escalation to hearing and imposition of fines under Section 9.3.

### Section 9.2 — Hearings

Prior to imposing fines, suspending privileges, or taking other adverse action, the Association shall offer the Owner an opportunity for a hearing before the Board or a Covenants Committee appointed by the Board, consistent with O.C.G.A. § 44-3-223. Notice of hearing shall be provided not less than ten (10) days in advance and shall state the time, place, and nature of the alleged violation. The Owner may appear personally, may be represented by counsel at the Owner's expense, may present evidence and witnesses, and may question witnesses presented against them. The hearing body shall issue a written decision within thirty (30) days of the hearing. Decisions of a Covenants Committee may be appealed to the full Board within fifteen (15) days; decisions of the full Board are final at the Association level.

### Section 9.3 — Fines and Penalties

For each violation that remains uncured after the applicable cure period, the Association may levy fines as follows: initial fine of Fifty Dollars ($50.00); continuing violations after second notice, One Hundred Dollars ($100.00) per occurrence or per week (whichever is applicable to the type of violation); aggravated or repeat violations, up to Two Hundred Fifty Dollars ($250.00) per occurrence; and violations of Section 5.5 (Short-Term Rentals), up to Five Hundred Dollars ($500.00) per day. Fines shall be treated as Specific Assessments against the offending Owner's Lot, shall accrue interest and late fees in the same manner as other assessments, and shall be enforceable by lien pursuant to Section 7.3 and O.C.G.A. § 44-3-232. Fines are in addition to, and not in lieu of, the Association's right to seek injunctive or other relief.

### Section 9.4 — Suspension of Privileges

Upon notice and hearing, the Board may suspend the voting rights and the right to use Common Area amenities (including the pool, Clubhouse, and tennis courts) of any Owner who is more than sixty (60) days delinquent in payment of any assessment, fine, or charge, or who is in continuing violation of this Declaration after a final adverse decision. Such suspension shall continue until the delinquency is cured or the violation is remedied. Suspension of privileges shall not apply to access to the Owner's Lot or essential ingress and egress and shall not be construed to deprive any Owner of rights guaranteed by Georgia law. Members of an Owner's household may also have amenity privileges suspended during the period of the Owner's suspension.

## Article X — Amendment

### Section 10.1 — Procedure

This Declaration may be amended at any time after the expiration of the Development Period by an instrument signed by Owners holding not less than two-thirds (2/3) of the total voting power of the Association and recorded in the Cobb County records, in accordance with O.C.G.A. § 44-3-226. Amendments proposed by the Board shall be sent to all Members not less than thirty (30) days before any meeting called to consider the amendment. Written ballots, including ballots submitted by mail, e-mail, or electronic ballot platform, shall be solicited from all Members and shall be valid if returned within forty-five (45) days. No amendment shall be effective which (i) deprives any Owner of access to the Owner's Lot, (ii) materially alters the formula for assessment allocation without unanimous consent, or (iii) violates applicable law. Technical or scrivener corrections may be made by the Board without Member vote.
