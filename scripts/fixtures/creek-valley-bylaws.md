# BYLAWS
# Creek Valley Community Association, Inc.

**A Georgia Nonprofit Corporation**
**Adopted: March 1, 2014**
**Last Amended: January 15, 2025**

---

## Article I — Name, Principal Office, and Definitions

The name of the corporation is **Creek Valley Community Association, Inc.** (the "Association"), a Georgia nonprofit corporation organized under the Georgia Nonprofit Corporation Code (O.C.G.A. § 14-3-101 et seq.) and the Georgia Property Owners' Association Act (O.C.G.A. § 44-3-220 et seq., the "Act"). The principal office of the Association is located at 1200 Creek Valley Parkway, Marietta, Georgia 30062, or at such other location within Cobb County as the Board of Directors may designate. Terms capitalized but not defined in these Bylaws shall have the meaning given them in the Declaration of Covenants, Conditions, and Restrictions for Creek Valley (the "Declaration"). In the event of conflict between these Bylaws and the Declaration, the Declaration shall control. In the event of conflict between these Bylaws and Georgia law, applicable Georgia law shall control.

---

## Article II — Membership and Voting

### Section 2.1 — Membership

Every Owner of a Lot within the Property described in the Declaration is a Member of the Association. Membership is appurtenant to and may not be separated from ownership of the Lot. Transfer of title to a Lot automatically transfers membership to the new Owner.

### Section 2.2 — Voting Rights

Each Lot shall be entitled to one (1) vote on any matter submitted to the Members. When more than one person holds an interest in any Lot, all such persons shall be Members, but the vote for such Lot shall be cast as the co-Owners determine among themselves. No vote shall be cast for any Lot owned by the Association.

### Section 2.3 — Good Standing

A Member is "in good standing" if the Member is current on all assessments, fines, and charges owed to the Association and is not subject to a final adverse decision in a covenant-enforcement matter that has not been cured. Only Members in good standing may vote, run for the Board, or use Common Area amenities.

### Section 2.4 — Suspension

Voting rights and amenity privileges may be suspended for any Member more than sixty (60) days delinquent or otherwise not in good standing, in accordance with Article IX of the Declaration and O.C.G.A. § 44-3-223.

---

## Article III — Meetings of Members

### Section 3.1 — Annual Meeting

The Annual Meeting of the Members shall be held in the month of November each year, on a date and at a time and place designated by the Board, for the purpose of electing Directors, receiving reports of officers, and transacting such other business as may properly come before the meeting.

### Section 3.2 — Special Meetings

Special Meetings of the Members may be called by the President, by a majority of the Board, or upon written petition signed by Members holding not less than twenty-five percent (25%) of the total voting power of the Association. Notice of any Special Meeting shall state the purpose, and no business other than that stated shall be transacted.

### Section 3.3 — Notice

Written notice of every meeting of the Members shall be delivered to each Member not less than twenty-one (21) days nor more than fifty (50) days before the meeting date. Notice may be delivered by U.S. mail to the address on file, by personal delivery, or by electronic means (e-mail) for Members who have affirmatively opted in to electronic notice.

### Section 3.4 — Quorum

A quorum at any meeting of the Members shall consist of the presence, in person or by proxy or by written ballot, of Members holding not less than thirty-five percent (35%) of the total voting power of the Association. If a quorum is not achieved, the meeting may be adjourned, and at any rescheduled meeting the quorum requirement shall be twenty-five percent (25%).

### Section 3.5 — Proxies and Ballots

A Member may vote in person or by proxy executed in writing by the Member or the Member's duly authorized attorney-in-fact. No proxy shall be valid for more than eleven (11) months from its date. The Board may authorize voting by written mail-in ballot or by secure electronic ballot for any matter, including the election of Directors. Mail ballots must be received by the Secretary not later than the close of polls at the meeting.

### Section 3.6 — Conduct of Meetings

The President, or in the President's absence the Vice President, shall preside over meetings of the Members. Meetings shall be conducted in accordance with the most recent edition of *Robert's Rules of Order, Newly Revised*, except where inconsistent with the Declaration, these Bylaws, or applicable law.

---

## Article IV — Board of Directors

### Section 4.1 — Number and Qualifications

The affairs of the Association shall be managed by a Board of Directors consisting of five (5) Directors. Each Director must be a Member of the Association in good standing. No more than one (1) Member from any single Lot may serve on the Board at the same time. Directors must reside within the Community as their primary residence for at least six (6) months prior to taking office.

### Section 4.2 — Term

Directors shall serve staggered terms of two (2) years. At each Annual Meeting, approximately one-half of the seats shall be elected, such that no more than three (3) seats are up for election in any single year. Directors may serve no more than three (3) consecutive two-year terms, after which a one-year hiatus is required.

### Section 4.3 — Election Procedure

Directors shall be elected by plurality vote of the Members at the Annual Meeting. Voting shall be conducted by a combination of (i) mail-in ballot mailed to all Members not less than thirty (30) days before the Annual Meeting and (ii) in-person voting at the Annual Meeting itself. The candidates receiving the highest number of votes for the available seats shall be elected. In the event of a tie for the final seat, the Board shall break the tie by lot.

### Section 4.4 — Nominations

A Nominating Committee of three (3) Members in good standing shall be appointed by the Board not later than ninety (90) days before each Annual Meeting. The Nominating Committee shall solicit candidates and shall close nominations forty-five (45) days before the Annual Meeting. Nominations from the floor shall not be permitted, except in the event of an uncontested seat or a vacancy occurring after the nomination deadline.

### Section 4.5 — Vacancies

Vacancies on the Board occurring between Annual Meetings (by resignation, death, removal, disqualification, or otherwise) shall be filled by majority vote of the remaining Directors. The replacement Director shall serve until the next Annual Meeting, at which time the unexpired term, if any, shall be filled by election.

### Section 4.6 — Removal

Any Director may be removed, with or without cause, by the affirmative vote of Members holding two-thirds (2/3) of the total voting power, at a meeting duly called for such purpose. A Director who fails to attend three (3) consecutive regular Board meetings without good cause may be removed by majority vote of the remaining Directors.

### Section 4.7 — Compensation

Directors shall serve without compensation but may be reimbursed for reasonable expenses incurred in the performance of their duties.

---

## Article V — Meetings of the Board

### Section 5.1 — Regular Meetings

The Board shall hold regular meetings not less than quarterly (four per fiscal year). The schedule of regular meetings shall be set by the Board at its organizational meeting following the Annual Meeting and shall be posted to the Association's website and bulletin board.

### Section 5.2 — Special Meetings

Special meetings of the Board may be called by the President or by any two (2) Directors. Notice of special meetings shall be given to each Director not less than seven (7) days in advance, except in emergencies, in which case notice may be given by any reasonable means.

### Section 5.3 — Quorum

A quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting of the Board shall be three (3) of the five (5) Directors. The act of a majority of Directors present at a meeting at which a quorum is present shall be the act of the Board, except as otherwise required by the Declaration, these Bylaws, or applicable law.

### Section 5.4 — Open Meetings

All regular and special meetings of the Board shall be open to all Members, who may attend and observe. The Board may convene an executive session, closed to non-Director Members, to discuss: (i) personnel matters; (ii) litigation or potential litigation; (iii) contract negotiations; (iv) covenant-enforcement matters involving specific Owners; and (v) other matters required to be confidential by law. Decisions reached in executive session must be ratified in open meeting.

### Section 5.5 — Action Without a Meeting

Any action required or permitted to be taken at a meeting of the Board may be taken without a meeting if all Directors consent in writing (including by e-mail), and such written consents are filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board. Such action shall be reported at the next regular open meeting.

### Section 5.6 — Meeting by Electronic Means

Directors may participate in any meeting by means of telephone, videoconference, or similar communication equipment by which all persons participating can hear each other. Such participation shall constitute presence in person at the meeting.

---

## Article VI — Officers

### Section 6.1 — Officers

The officers of the Association shall be a President, a Vice President, a Treasurer, a Secretary, and a Member at Large. All officers must be Directors and shall be elected by the Board at its organizational meeting following each Annual Meeting, for terms of one (1) year.

### Section 6.2 — President

The President shall be the chief executive officer of the Association, shall preside at all meetings of the Members and of the Board, shall sign contracts and other documents on behalf of the Association, shall act as the primary liaison to the Association's management company and legal counsel, and shall perform such other duties as are customarily performed by the president of a Georgia nonprofit corporation.

### Section 6.3 — Vice President

The Vice President shall perform the duties of the President in the President's absence, inability, or refusal to act, and shall perform such other duties as may be assigned by the President or the Board.

### Section 6.4 — Treasurer

The Treasurer shall have charge of the financial affairs of the Association, shall oversee the collection of assessments, the payment of obligations, the maintenance of financial records, and the preparation of the annual budget and financial statements. The Treasurer shall ensure that an independent CPA review or audit is performed at the intervals required by Article IX, and shall report on the financial condition of the Association at each regular Board meeting.

### Section 6.5 — Secretary

The Secretary shall keep the minutes of all meetings of the Members and of the Board, shall give all required notices, shall maintain the official roster of Members, shall be custodian of the corporate records (other than financial records, which are kept by the Treasurer), and shall attest signatures of the President as required.

### Section 6.6 — Member at Large

The Member at Large shall serve as the fifth Director and officer, with no fixed portfolio, providing an additional voice in Board deliberations and undertaking such special assignments as the President or Board may designate.

### Section 6.7 — Removal of Officers

Any officer may be removed from office, with or without cause, by majority vote of the Board, but such removal shall not affect the officer's status as a Director.

---

## Article VII — Committees

### Section 7.1 — Standing Committees

The Board shall maintain at least the following standing committees: (a) **Architectural Review Committee (ARC)**; (b) **Covenants Committee**, to conduct hearings under Article IX of the Declaration; (c) **Finance Committee**, to assist the Treasurer; and (d) **Social/Community Committee**, to plan Community events.

### Section 7.2 — Special Committees

The Board may from time to time create special committees for specific purposes, with terms and membership defined at the time of creation. All committee actions are advisory unless explicitly delegated authority by the Board.

### Section 7.3 — Composition

Each committee shall consist of not fewer than three (3) Members in good standing, appointed by the Board. The Board shall designate the chair. At least one (1) Director shall serve as a liaison to each standing committee.

---

## Article VIII — Fiduciary Duties and Conflicts of Interest

### Section 8.1 — Fiduciary Duty

Each Director and officer of the Association shall discharge his or her duties in good faith, with the care an ordinarily prudent person in a like position would exercise under similar circumstances, and in a manner the Director reasonably believes to be in the best interests of the Association and its Members.

### Section 8.2 — Conflicts of Interest

A Director or officer who has a direct or indirect material financial interest in any proposed transaction with the Association shall: (a) promptly disclose the conflict to the Board in writing; (b) recuse himself or herself from deliberation and voting on the matter; and (c) not be counted toward the quorum for purposes of the vote on that matter. A record of all disclosed conflicts shall be maintained by the Secretary.

### Section 8.3 — Code of Conduct

Directors and officers shall not use their position for personal gain, shall not accept gifts of substantial value from vendors or contractors, shall maintain confidentiality of executive-session matters, and shall treat all Members and one another with civility and respect.

### Section 8.4 — Self-Dealing Prohibited

No Director, officer, or family member thereof shall be employed by the Association for compensation, nor shall any such person hold a contract for goods or services with the Association, unless the contract is awarded through a competitive process and is approved by unanimous vote of disinterested Directors after full disclosure.

---

## Article IX — Fiscal Affairs

### Section 9.1 — Fiscal Year

The fiscal year of the Association shall begin on January 1 and end on December 31.

### Section 9.2 — Budget

Not later than November 15 of each year, the Treasurer, with the assistance of the Finance Committee and management company, shall prepare a proposed budget for the upcoming fiscal year. The Board shall consider and adopt the budget at its November or December meeting. A copy of the adopted budget and the schedule of assessments shall be delivered to all Members not less than thirty (30) days before the start of the fiscal year.

### Section 9.3 — Reserves

The Board shall maintain a reserve fund for the periodic repair and replacement of Common Area capital components. A reserve study shall be commissioned not less than every five (5) years and updated annually.

### Section 9.4 — Audit and Review Requirements

A CPA-prepared **financial review** shall be performed annually. A full **independent audit** by a licensed Georgia CPA firm shall be performed not less than every three (3) years, or more frequently if requested by Members holding at least twenty percent (20%) of the voting power or required by the Association's lender, lender's underwriting standards, or applicable law. Audit and review reports shall be made available to all Members upon request.

### Section 9.5 — Bonding

The Association shall maintain a fidelity bond or crime-insurance coverage in an amount not less than three (3) months of operating expenses plus the maximum amount of reserves on hand during the year, covering all persons (including management company personnel) with access to Association funds.

---

## Article X — Books, Records, and Inspection Rights

### Section 10.1 — Records

The Association shall maintain, at its principal office or the office of its management company: (a) the Declaration, Bylaws, and Rules and Regulations as currently in effect; (b) the membership roster; (c) minutes of meetings of the Members and Board for the past seven (7) years; (d) financial records, including annual budgets, financial statements, audits, and reviews for the past seven (7) years; (e) contracts and insurance policies currently in effect; and (f) records of architectural approvals and covenant-enforcement actions.

### Section 10.2 — Member Inspection Rights

Any Member in good standing shall have the right, upon written request stating a proper purpose, to inspect and copy the records of the Association during regular business hours at the office where the records are kept, in accordance with O.C.G.A. § 14-3-1602 and § 44-3-231. The Association may charge a reasonable copying fee. Personnel records, attorney-client communications, and records subject to executive-session confidentiality are exempt from inspection.

### Section 10.3 — Privacy

The Association shall not disclose Members' personal contact information to other Members for non-Association purposes, and shall comply with applicable privacy laws regarding Member data.

---

## Article XI — Indemnification

### Section 11.1 — Right to Indemnification

To the fullest extent permitted by O.C.G.A. § 14-3-851 through § 14-3-857, the Association shall indemnify and hold harmless each Director, officer, committee member, and other duly authorized agent acting on behalf of the Association against any judgments, fines, settlements, and reasonable expenses (including attorney's fees) incurred in connection with any threatened, pending, or completed civil, criminal, administrative, or investigative proceeding by reason of such person's service to the Association, provided that such person acted in good faith and in a manner reasonably believed to be in the best interests of the Association.

### Section 11.2 — Insurance

The Association shall maintain Directors and Officers (D&O) liability insurance in a commercially reasonable amount (not less than $1,000,000), as well as general liability, property, workers' compensation (if applicable), and umbrella coverage as recommended by the Association's insurance broker.

### Section 11.3 — Advancement of Expenses

Reasonable expenses incurred by an indemnified person in defending a proceeding may be advanced by the Association upon receipt of a written undertaking to repay such amount if it is ultimately determined that the person was not entitled to indemnification.

---

## Article XII — Amendment of Bylaws

These Bylaws may be amended by the affirmative vote of Members holding a majority of the total voting power of the Association, at a meeting duly called for such purpose with not less than thirty (30) days' written notice setting forth the proposed amendments. The Board may propose amendments by majority vote. No amendment shall conflict with the Declaration or with applicable Georgia law. The Secretary shall maintain a current, consolidated version of these Bylaws and shall make it available to all Members on the Association website and upon request.
