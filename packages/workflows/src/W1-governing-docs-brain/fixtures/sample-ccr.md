## Article IV — Architectural Control

### Section 4.1 Approval Required

No exterior modification, addition, or alteration to any Unit, including but not limited to paint color, roofing material, landscaping above 24 inches in height, fences, satellite dishes, or accessory structures, may be commenced without prior written approval of the Architectural Review Committee (ARC). Submissions must be in writing on the form provided by the Association and must include drawings, dimensions, and material samples where applicable.

### Section 4.2 Standards

Approved exterior paint colors are limited to those on the Association's Approved Color Palette, available from the Managing Agent. Front doors may be painted in any color from the Approved Door Color Palette without ARC review, provided the homeowner notifies the Managing Agent within 30 days of painting.

## Article V — Use Restrictions

### Section 5.3 Pets

No more than two (2) domesticated household pets per Unit are permitted. All pets must be leashed when outside the Unit, and pet waste must be cleaned up immediately. Aggressive breeds as defined by the Association's Rules are prohibited.

### Section 5.7 Vehicles and Parking

No commercial vehicle, recreational vehicle, boat, trailer, or inoperable vehicle may be parked on any street, driveway, or lot for more than 48 hours, except in a fully enclosed garage. Resident vehicles must be parked in the homeowner's driveway or garage; street parking is for guests only and limited to 72 consecutive hours.

## Article VI — Assessments

### Section 6.1 Schedule

Annual assessments are due in equal monthly installments on the first day of each month. Late fees of five percent (5%) of the unpaid balance accrue on the 15th day of each month if the installment remains unpaid.

### Section 6.4 Lien Rights

Pursuant to Georgia statute O.C.G.A. § 44-3-232, the Association may file a lien against any Unit whose assessments remain unpaid for more than 60 days. A demand letter must be sent at least 30 days before recording the lien.
