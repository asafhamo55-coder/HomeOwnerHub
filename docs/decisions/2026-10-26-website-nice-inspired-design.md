# Marketing website: design inspired by nice.com

**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Decided by:** Asaf ("on the website try to replicate the design patterns and colors of www.nice.com").

## Decision

The marketing site (apps/marketing) adopts the visual language of www.nice.com: its color palette and mood, type scale and weights, section patterns, spacing rhythm and component styles (buttons, cards, navigation, hero treatment). This replaces the blue-first palette from the BL-170 redesign on the marketing site.

## Boundaries (so it stays HomeownerHub and stays clean)

- **Inspired by, not copied.** No NICE logo, wordmark, imagery, illustrations, copy, icons, fonts licensed only to them, or source code. No pixel-for-pixel page clones. The site must never look like it is NICE or affiliated with NICE.
- **HomeownerHub identity stays:** the name, the "H with a roof" logo shape (its colors may move to the new palette), our own content and screenshots, and the Georgia-first message.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG AA contrast. Respect reduced motion.
- **The app** (apps/hoa) keeps its current palette for now. Bringing it in line is a separate decision.
- All BL-157/170/171 truth rules and SEO work stay as they are.
