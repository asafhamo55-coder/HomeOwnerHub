## Context

$4.99/door with a 41-door floor bills a 50-door HOA $249.50/mo; PayHOA bills $65 (3.8×). The self-managed market prices in **flat size bands** that fall per door with size (PayHOA $2.16/door at 25 doors, $0.63 at 400), and free or $29 AI-light tools anchor the bottom. Linear per-door pricing (today's, BL-068's or a single lower rate) lands at 2.5–3.2× PayHOA by 300–400 doors. Realistic variable cost is ≈$5–10/org/mo (AI ≈$1–2), so margin isn't the constraint; positioning, simplicity, a flat AI abuse ceiling and onboarding time are.

## Decision — Scenario 2

### 1. Plans and prices

| Doors (band) | Essentials /mo | Complete /mo | Essentials /yr | Complete /yr |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1–50 | $49 | $99 | $490 | $990 |
| 51–100 | $79 | $149 | $790 | $1,490 |
| 101–200 | $119 | $229 | $1,190 | $2,290 |
| 201–400 | $179 | $349 | $1,790 | $3,490 |
| 401+ | $0.45 × doors | $9 + $0.85 × doors | $4.50 × doors | $90 + $8.50 × doors |

- **Essentials:** Core (properties and owners, resident portal, communications, documents and cited state-law Q&A, violations, ARC, meetings, tickets, events), dues with a simple ledger, and the AI assistant at Essentials limits.
- **Complete:** Essentials plus Accounting (GL, bank rec and Plaid, AP and bill scan, budgets, reports, collections, payment plans), Mailbox, Vendors & RFPs, Leases, full AI limits and the larger SMS allowance.
- **Annual = 10 × monthly** (2 months free). Both cadences are **month-to-month / cancel any time**; the 12-month first-year commitment (`COMMIT_MONTHS`) goes for new subscriptions.
- **30-day free trial**, on Essentials limits, no card required to start (card at conversion).
- **Founding customers:** the first 25 paying orgs keep the price they sign up at for 24 months.
- **Invariant:** the price never falls as doors rise (tested from 1 to 2,000 doors).

### 2. Door count — who sets it and when it changes

- Stripe `quantity` = the server-side count of the org's units (properties), never a form field. The checkout form fallback (BL-103) is removed for HOA plans.
- Quantity editing is off in the Stripe customer portal.
- A band change (doors cross 50/100/200/400) takes effect at the **next renewal**, `proration_behavior: 'none'`. A nightly job updates the subscription quantity with no proration; the new band bills on the next invoice.

### 3. AI ceilings per band

Rule: **worst-case AI cost, board-only cap included, ≤ 30% of the band's monthly price.** Today every org gets the same per-org daily ceilings in `apps/hoa/src/lib/api/ai-limits.ts`, worth ≈$115/mo if all are maxed (research §3.1). Each `perOrg.limit` becomes `max(5, round(today's limit × multiplier))`:

| Band | Essentials | Complete |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1–50 (and trial) | 0.10× | 0.20× |
| 51–100 | 0.18× | 0.30× |
| 101–200 | 0.28× | 0.55× |
| 201–400 and 401+ | 0.44× | 0.85× |

Example: a 40-door Essentials org gets 60 resident-assistant messages and 50 doc questions per day. Per-user windows are unchanged.

The board-only AI routes that are unlimited today (violation drafting from the wizard and the /inspect queue, digests, parse/suggest) get a combined per-org daily cap: **100/day on Essentials, 200/day on Complete** (≈$2.70 and ≈$5.40/mo at ~$0.0009 per call). Over the cap, the board sees the same 429 copy pattern and can still write by hand.

Check (1–50 band): Essentials 0.10 × $115 + $2.70 = $14.20 ≤ 30% of $49 ($14.70); Complete 0.20 × $115 + $5.40 = $28.40 ≤ $29.70. These multipliers are estimates on unmeasured token use; re-derive them from `ai_runs` at the revisit.

### 4. SMS allowance

- **Essentials:** 2 segments per door per month (minimum 100).
- **Complete:** 4 segments per door per month (minimum 200).

Over the allowance, texts aren't sent (email still goes) and the board sees why. Metered overage is a later Stripe change. Mailed letters (BL-032) will be pass-through at cost when they ship.

### 5. Existing subscriptions — keep their price until renewal

- **Existing paying subscriptions keep their current price until renewal.** We do not move anyone mid-term.
- **"Renewal"** = the end of the subscription's current term: the 12-month first-year commitment for monthly subscribers, or the current paid year for annual subscribers. At renewal the subscription moves to the matching new plan and band (Complete by default, since it holds all current features). The new price is lower at every size, so this is never an increase. The board gets an email 30 days before.
- A customer who asks to move to the new, lower price earlier is moved at their next invoice, and released from the remaining commitment. We don't do it unasked.
- Old Stripe Prices (`hoa_per_door_monthly`, `hoa_per_door_annual`) stay **active but unlisted** until the last grandfathered subscription renews. Never archive or edit a Price a live subscription uses.
- **Madison Park** (pilot) is grandfathered on whatever it was promised, then gets founding-customer terms.
- **Before cut-over:** list live subscriptions in Stripe (live mode, `price.lookup_key in (hoa_per_door_monthly, hoa_per_door_annual)`) and record org ids and term ends (ids only) in BL-104. `docs/decisions/2026-10-pricing.md` says nobody paid list price yet; confirm it.

### 6. What must change together (BL-104)

Code (`packages/billing`, checkout, webhook, `ai-limits.ts`, module gates, settings/billing), Stripe (four new tiered Prices, old ones kept) and marketing (`hoa-pricing.ts`, its test pins and the pages that read it) ship in **one PR**. The investor deck (`~/HomeownerHub-decks/`) changes from "$4.99/door and BL-068 modules" to this table at the same time.

## Rejected

- **Scenario 1, BL-068 per-module pricing:** six prices and six minimums for a volunteer board to model; linear scaling reaches 2.5–2.9× PayHOA at 300–400 doors; six Stripe items per org.
- **Scenario 3, $1.99/door with a $79 floor:** simplest code, but 3.2× PayHOA at 400 doors and no answer to the $0–29 entry tools.
- **A free tier:** support load without proof of demand; the 30-day trial does the job.
- **Keep $4.99/door:** 3.8× PayHOA at 25–50 doors for a product with no published reviews yet.

## Consequences

- List price falls at every size (50 doors: $249.50 → $49 / $99). No customer's bill goes up.
- Small management companies aren't priced for; they buy Complete per association until a portfolio product exists (unchanged from the 2026-10-15 recommendation).
- If the owner prefers AI out of Essentials, the fallback is an AI add-on at a flat $20/mo (1–100 doors) or $40/mo (above). That changes this record, not the structure.

## Revisit

After **10 paying orgs or 90 days after launch**, whichever comes first. Use measured `ai_runs` token totals (query in `2026-10-pricing.md` §4), SMS segments sent, and onboarding hours per org. Every price is reversible for new signups; changes for existing customers follow §5.

## Amendment — BL-106 (2026-10-20): meeting summary and board Copilot caps

§3 assumed every board-only AI route was in `board_ai`. Two were not: `POST /api/ai/meeting-summary` and `POST /api/ai/ask-community` (the board Copilot) had no limit, and the comment in `ai-limits.ts` saying they drew on `board_ai` was wrong. Each now has its **own bucket**, never band-scaled. The §3 multipliers and the `board_ai` pool (100 / 200 a day) are unchanged.

**Decision (coordinator, 2026-10-20):**

1. **Bound the cost of a Copilot question in code.**
   - Each model step in the Copilot loop sends `max_tokens` 1,500 (`COPILOT_MAX_TOKENS_PER_STEP`). It was first set at 1,000. It went up to 1,500 because gpt-oss counts its reasoning against the limit, and cutting off answers for today's customers costs more than the extra ~$0.0024 a question.
   - A question runs at most 6 docs searches (`COPILOT_TOOL_BUDGET.perTool`). The other limits stay at the default: 8 tool calls per step, 16 per question, 8 model steps.
   - The resident assistant's loop is unchanged.
2. **Per-plan community ceilings.** **PROVISIONAL — to be rebalanced before PRICING_V2 is switched on (BL-108).** The Essentials values (3 and 0) and the Complete overage below wait on the per-band AI budget rebalance:
   - Orgs with no tier keep the base ceilings. That is every org before `PRICING_V2`, and grandfathered or pre-flip orgs after it.
   - Complete keeps the base ceilings.
   - Essentials, which includes trials once `PRICING_V2` is on, gets lower ones. They were lowered until Essentials 1–50 fits the ≤30% rule with every bucket included (table below).

| Bucket | Per person | No tier / Complete per community | Essentials per community | Est. cost per call |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `meeting_summary` | 5 / hour | 10 / day | **3 / day** | $0.0024 |
| `ask_community` | 15 / hour | 25 / day | **0 (not on the plan)** | $0.0234 |

**Per-call estimates** come from the code's budgets, at the gpt-oss-120b list price §3 uses ($0.15/M in, $0.60/M out):

- **Meeting summary:** `summarizeMeeting` sends at most the first 30,000 characters of the transcript (~7.5k tokens) plus ~0.2k of prompt, with `max_tokens` 2,000. That is 7.7k × $0.15/M + 2k × $0.60/M ≈ **$0.0024**.
- **Copilot question:** up to 8 model steps, each re-sending the growing conversation (~9k tokens on average, so ~72k in), at ≤1,500 out per step (≤12k out). That is ≈ $0.018. Add up to 6 docs searches at ~$0.0009 each ($0.0054). Total ≈ **$0.0234** at most. A typical question uses 2–3 steps and one search, and costs about $0.004.

**Worst case per month** (every ceiling hit every day for 30 days):

| Org | §3 buckets | BL-106 buckets | Total | 30% of price | Fits |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| No tier (legacy, pre-flip) | (no band price) | (10 × $0.0024 + 25 × $0.0234) × 30 = $18.27 | n/a | n/a | n/a |
| Essentials 1–50 | $14.22 | 3 × $0.0024 × 30 = $0.22 | **$14.44** | $14.70 | yes |
| Essentials, every band, 1–2,000 doors | | $0.22 | | | yes (tested) |
| Complete 1–50 | $28.44 | $18.27 | **$46.71** | $29.70 | **no** |

**Why Essentials gets no Copilot.** §3 already uses nearly all of the Essentials budget: $0.48 of room at 1–50, $0.26 at 51–100, $0.74 at 101–200 and $0.31 at 201–400. One Copilot question a day costs $0.70 a month. With the 3 summaries a day on top ($0.22), it does not fit at any Essentials size up to 400 doors. The coordinator's starting value of 10 a day ($6.30/mo) is far over. So the per-community ceiling is 0. An Essentials board that asks the Copilot gets a 429 `rate_limited`, reason `plan_limit`, with "Copilot questions aren't included in your community's plan." This applies to trials too, since they run on Essentials limits. Three summaries a day is the most that fits at 51–100, the band with the least room.

**Known gap, Complete.** The coordinator's decision keeps Complete at 25 Copilot questions a day. Complete's §3 room is only $1.09–$4.74 a month, depending on the band, so Complete is over 30% at every band from 1 to 400 doors. At 1–50 it is $46.71 against $29.70. The tests check ≤30% with every bucket for Essentials. For Complete they check §3 alone, and they record the overage. To bring Complete under the rule, choose one:

- Cap Complete Copilot at about 1–2 a day.
- Take room from the §3 multipliers.
- Raise the 30% rule to include a Copilot allowance.

**Normal work today:** nothing changes for current orgs until `PRICING_V2` is switched on. They keep 10 summaries and 25 Copilot questions a day, with 15 an hour per person.

Over a cap, the route returns the standard 429 `rate_limited` with `Retry-After` and the usual message. The meeting wizard still lets the board write minutes by hand. Re-derive both per-call estimates from `ai_runs` at the Revisit, along with the rest of §3.
