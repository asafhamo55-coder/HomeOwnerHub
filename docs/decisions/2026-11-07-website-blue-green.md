# Website recolour to blue and green (2026-11-07)

Status: accepted. Option B chosen by Asaf; supersedes the Royal purple decision
of 2026-11-06. The HOA app (apps/hoa) and the shared preset in
packages/ui/src/tokens.ts do not change.

## Palette

- Primary blue #2563EB (hover #1D4ED8, subtle #EFF6FF) for buttons, links and
  the logo badge.
- Deep blue #1E40AF for the hero band, closing band and night sections, with a
  white "Book a demo" button carrying #1E40AF text.
- Green #047857 for eyebrows, step badges, approved states and the state-law
  band (subtle #ECFDF5, border tint #A7F3D0). Green is never a primary button.
- Mint #6EE7B7 only for the highlight phrase on deep blue bands.
- Ink #0F172A, secondary #334155, muted #526077, canvas #F5F8FC, border
  #E2E8F0, footer #0F172A with #CBD5E1 text.

## Where

apps/marketing globals.css tokens, panels, buttons, footer; the share images;
favicon, apple icon and public/brand files (regenerated by
scripts/build-brand-assets.ts). Contrast ratios are listed in the globals.css
header comment.

The hero, closing band, tile and /og gradients are now flat #1E40AF, and the OG "Hub" accent is mint #A7F3D0.
