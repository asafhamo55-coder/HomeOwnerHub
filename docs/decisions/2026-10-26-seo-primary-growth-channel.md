# SEO is the primary growth channel

**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Decided by:** Asaf ("invest the most in SEO"). Execution plan by Claude.

## Decision

Search (SEO) gets the largest share of growth effort. Other channels (communities, referrals, webinar, paid search) support it and send people to the SEO pages.

## Why it fits

- Self-managed boards search when they hit a problem ("Georgia HOA registration", "HOA violation letter template"). That is high intent and free, and it compounds.
- SB 406 creates a wave of new Georgia searches before and after **2027-01-01**. Pages published in November can rank by then.
- We have an unusual asset: verified, official statute fixtures (GA Articles 3 and 6, SB 406, Title 14; FL, CA, TX). Our pages can quote the law exactly where competitors paraphrase.

## The program (BL-172, ongoing)

1. **Technical base (BL-171):**
   - analytics and conversion events;
   - Search Console with the sitemap;
   - per-page titles and descriptions;
   - JSON-LD (Organization, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, Article);
   - fast Core Web Vitals and internal linking;
   - a canonical domain (www.homeownerledger.online).
2. **Content clusters, Georgia first:**
   - **Georgia law guides:** registration, the attorney's-fee notice, foreclosure threshold, owner rights, late fees, the complaint process, condo vs HOA, annual meetings and quorum, proxies, records requests, nonprofit annual registration.
   - **Free templates and tools,** which carry the highest intent and earn links: a violation letter, a collections notice, a meeting notice, a proxy form, an estoppel request, a budget template, the SB 406 readiness check, a late-fee calculator under the Georgia cap.
   - **"How to run a self-managed HOA" guides:** treasurer duties, reserve studies, the annual meeting checklist, transitioning from a management company.
   - **Comparison pages,** factual and dated, from public information only: HomeownerHub vs PayHOA / Buildium / AppFolio / CINC, and "management company vs self-managed".
   - **Local pages,** only where there is real local substance (county lien-recording office, county court for liens): metro Atlanta counties first (Fulton, Gwinnett, Cobb, DeKalb, Forsyth, Cherokee). No thin doorway pages.
   - **Then FL, CA and TX,** reusing their fixtures.
3. **Cadence:**
   - The loop ships **at least 3 new or improved SEO pages a day**, through a Sonnet SEO agent, and a full-site SEO audit weekly.
   - Each deploy's release note lists the new pages.
4. **Quality bar:**
   - Statute text is quoted only from the official fixtures, with word-for-word tests.
   - Features are described only if they're shipped. Every legal page says it isn't legal advice.
   - Each page ends with "Book a demo" or the relevant free tool.
5. **Links (with Asaf):**
   - free templates people cite;
   - Georgia HOA attorneys and CPAs (BL-168 gives them seats);
   - the CAI Georgia chapter, and local news on SB 406.

## Measuring it

Weekly, from Search Console and Vercel Analytics: impressions, clicks, and the top queries and pages; demo-button clicks and demo submissions per landing page. The first two weeks set the baseline. Targets are set after that, not invented now.
