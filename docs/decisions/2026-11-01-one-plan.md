# One plan with every feature

**Date:** 2026-11-01. Asaf asked to merge into one plan and chose these prices (option B) after asking Claude to validate an earlier, lower proposal. This resolves A5 in the PayHOA parity program and supersedes the two-plan structure and prices in `docs/decisions/2026-10-20-pricing.md`. The band method in that document stays.

## Decision

Sell one plan, named HomeownerHub, with every feature:

| Homes | Monthly | Yearly |
|---|---|---|
| 1-50 | $59 | $590 |
| 51-100 | $109 | $1,090 |
| 101-200 | $169 | $1,690 |
| 201-400 | $249 | $2,490 |
| 401+ | $0.65 per home | $6.50 per home |

The 30-day free trial with no card stays.

## Why

- Two plans ask a volunteer board to choose before they understand the product. One plan removes that step, which suits older board members.
- Accounting was the main thing held back in Complete. Boards compare us with all-in-one tools, so a plan without it reads as incomplete.
- These prices sit roughly level with PayHOA (research dated 2026-10-20 and 2026-10-31). The first proposal, the former Essentials prices of $49 to $179, undercut it by up to 35% without a reason. The old Complete prices would be up to about twice PayHOA at small sizes, which is hard to defend without reviews.
- Cost does not set the price: estimated variable cost is about $2 to $5 per community per month under 150 homes, and under $15 at 400.

## Costs we accept

- New Stripe Prices are needed, created by Asaf. Old Prices stay so any existing subscription keeps working and keeps its price.
- These prices are not proven. There are no paying customers yet, so this is a market comparison and not evidence of what boards will pay.
- AI and SMS limits stay at the former Essentials values for now, since $59 does not support Complete's limits under the 30% AI cost rule. Features that were Complete-only run under those limits. If real usage hits them, raise the limits using measured cost.

## Not decided here

Founding-member discounts and add-ons. Online payment fees are separate: see `docs/decisions/2026-11-01-online-payment-fee-decisions.md`.
