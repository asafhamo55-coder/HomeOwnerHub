# Website positioning: product first, states as supporting benefits

**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Decided by:** Asaf ("the website is too much focus on Georgia… it should be focus in the HOA self service AI based workspace and as side can provide the benefits of GA and other states").

## Decision

The marketing website leads with **the product**: an AI-based, self-service workspace for self-managed HOA boards. Georgia, Florida, California and Texas show up as supporting benefits ("built-in state law support"), not as the headline.

## What changes

- **Home, /hoa, /pricing, /about:** heroes, headlines and the first screens describe the workspace: AI drafting with the board approving, dues and collections, violations, communications and announcements, accounting, documents and AI answers with citations, the resident portal, and compliance. Every claim must be a shipped feature.
- **State support** becomes one section ("State law built in: Georgia, Florida, California, Texas") linking to the state hubs. Georgia's SB 406 content stays available but is not the lead.
- **Navigation:** "Georgia" becomes "State laws" (or similar), with Georgia first in the list.

## What stays

- The /georgia hub, the guides, templates and the readiness check. They are SEO assets (BL-172) and keep ranking for Georgia searches; they simply aren't the homepage message.
- Georgia remains the launch market for sales and for product priority. This decision is about website messaging only.
- The truth rules, SEO metadata, the nice.com-inspired design and the "Book a demo" CTAs.
