## Summary

When an owner hasn't fixed a violation, the board can now draft the next letter right from the violation's page: a second notice, a hearing notice, a fine notice or a decision letter. Every letter waits in Approvals, and nothing is sent to the homeowner. The user guide now also covers this week's new features.

## What's new

### Follow-up letters
- On a violation's page, the new **Follow-up letters** card has **Draft follow-up letter**.
- Pick **Second notice**, **Hearing notice**, **Fine notice** or **Decision letter** (fine or suspension). The AI drafts it from the violation and your state's rules.
- If state law doesn't allow that letter yet, for example a fine before a hearing, or a fine while a California owner's commitment to cure is on file, you'll see why and no letter is drafted.
- Edit the draft, then **Save to approvals**. Approving it in **Approvals → Violation letters** files it on the same violation and adds it to the history. No new violation is opened.

### User guide
- New help for Spanish announcements, resident decision emails, California's commitment to cure, and vendor insurance minimums.
- Ask the guide now finds the right article for all 226 how-to test questions.
