---
title: Safer estoppel certificates and collections letters, and a hardened resident assistant
---

## Summary

A review of this week's new features found several problems. This update fixes them. Estoppel certificates and collections letters can be printed again safely.

## Fixes

### Estoppel certificates and collections letters
- **Up-to-date figures only:** if a payment or charge posts after you open the certificate, printing stops and asks you to reload. The printed figures always match the account.
- **Browser printing (Cmd+P) no longer produces a usable document.** It prints a "DRAFT — NOT VALID" page. Use the **Print** button, which checks blanks, verification and approval first.
- **Fee check:** the Florida $150 delinquent add-on now applies only when money is actually overdue. A payment that hasn't been matched to a charge now counts against the overdue amount.
- **Payment plans:** payment-plan installments are no longer listed as special assessments on certificates.
- **California letters** now ask how many days after the due date your declaration treats an assessment as delinquent (at least 15). Letters approved before this update need to be drafted again.
- **No double records:** printing the same approved letter twice no longer records the collections step twice.

### Resident assistant
- **Off until the board turns it on:** the assistant now starts **off**, and the board switches it on in **Settings → Notifications**, with a "Recommended" hint. Each association has its own switch.
- **Usage limits:** each question can trigger only a small number of lookups, which keeps usage within the limits.
- **Suggested forms:** "Ask the board" and ARC forms suggested by the assistant are filled from the resident's own words, never from document text, and are labelled "Suggested by the assistant".
- **Privacy:** street addresses are now removed from what the assistant's search stores.

## Known issue

- **Payment plans can count the original overdue charges twice.** A fix is in progress. Until then, please don't create new payment plans.
