title: A chat assistant for residents, and a focused HOA-only site
---

## Summary

Residents can now chat with an assistant on the portal's **Ask** tab. It answers questions about their own account, their requests, community announcements and the community's rules, and cites the section of the documents for every rules answer. It never acts for the board.

The website and app now show only the HOA product.

## What's new

### Resident assistant
- **What it can answer:** "What do I owe?", "Where's my ARC request?", "Can I paint my front door?", "When's the next meeting?" Rules answers always cite the document section.
- **What it can see:** only the resident's own information, never other owners' details.
- **What it never does:** create or change anything. When the documents don't answer a question, it offers **Ask the board**, which opens a request already filled in from the chat. For projects it offers **Start an ARC application**. The resident reviews and sends either one.
- **Limits:** up to 20 questions an hour and 100 a day per resident. Every answer shows "Not legal advice — the board decides."
- **Board control:** turn it off in **Settings → Notifications → Resident portal**. While it's off, residents get the earlier Ask the Docs. It is on by default; residents only get answers when they ask.

### HOA only
- The Property Management and Eviction products are hidden everywhere: in the app's hub switcher and in the website's menus, pricing and pages. Old links go to the HOA page.
