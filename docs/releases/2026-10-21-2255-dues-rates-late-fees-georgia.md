## Summary

Boards now set their own late-fee policy, and **no late fee is charged until you save one**. Dues rates, owners asking to join their home, and evidence on the Compliance page are switched on. Georgia boards get the two collections notices the law requires, Georgia quorum and proxy rules, and an annual report for owners.

## Action needed
- **Late fees are off until you set them.** Until now the app added a fixed 5% late fee to every overdue charge, fines included, with no grace period. That has stopped. Open **Settings → Late fees** to choose a flat amount or percent, a grace period, and whether fines are excluded (they are by default), and confirm your declaration provides for a late charge. State caps apply: in Georgia, the greater of $10 or 10%; in Florida, the greater of $25 or 5%; in California, the greater of $10 or 10%, after 15 days.
- **Add your current dues rate:** open **Dues → Dues rates** and add today's rate, so billing starts from it.

## What's new

### Dues
- **Dues rates:** keep a schedule of your dues: the amount per home, how often it's billed, and when it starts. Generate dues and Add a due start from the rate in effect. An upcoming increase can be drafted as a notice to owners.
- **Budget kit:** the last step of the budget adoption checklist opens Dues rates filled in from the approved budget.

### Owners
- **Ask to join:** an owner whose account isn't linked to a home can ask to join it, by address or with a one-time code from the board. Requests wait in **Approvals**; nobody can approve their own. Make codes on **Settings → Members → Join codes**.

### Georgia
- **Collections letters:** the notice before attorney's fees (SB 406, 30 days from receipt) and the notice before foreclosure (§ 44-3-232(c)), with the $2,000 threshold and 10% interest cap checked. Later letters that claim attorney's fees stay blocked until the fee notice has gone out.
- **Quorum and proxies:** the quorum kit now shows Georgia's default (more than one-third, unless your bylaws say otherwise) and a proxy form that meets Georgia's rules.
- **Annual report to owners:** from an annual meeting, print a report of the year's finances, budget, reserves, insurance, meetings and elections for owners, as § 44-3-230(a) asks. It never shows any home's balance or owner names.

### Compliance
- **Evidence:** attach a document, such as a registration receipt, when you mark a deadline done.
- **Director elections:** each planned election now shows on Compliance with its next date. In Georgia, a reminder appears to amend your Secretary of State registration within 30 days if officers changed.

## Good to know
- No late fees were removed from existing bills. Only new nightly fees are affected.
