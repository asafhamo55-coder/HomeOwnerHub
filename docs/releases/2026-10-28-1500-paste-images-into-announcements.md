## Summary

Boards can now paste an image straight into an announcement (a flyer, a photo of the pool, a map of the closure). Residents see the picture inside the email body, where it was pasted. Text messages leave the picture out and stay within the length limit.

## What's new

### Announcements
- Paste an image (PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP, up to 5 MB, up to 6 per announcement) into the English or Spanish message. It uploads in the background and shows in the phone preview and in "send a test to me".
- In the email, the picture sits in place in the body. Some mail apps, Outlook among them, ask the reader to click "show images" first.

## Under the hood

- Pictures are stored privately under the association and served from a link containing a random ID. Only these announcement pictures can be reached that way.

## Needs your action

- Send yourself a test announcement with a pasted picture and check that it shows in Gmail and on your phone.
