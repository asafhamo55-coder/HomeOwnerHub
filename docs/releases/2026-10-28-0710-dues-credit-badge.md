title: See which homes have credit waiting, right on the dues list
---

## Summary

On the board's Dues page, homes that have money waiting as credit now show a small "Credit $X" badge. One click opens the home's page, where you can apply the credit to a charge.

## What's new

- **Credit badge:** a "Credit $X" badge sits under the address on the Dues list for any home with unapplied credit.
- **Apply it in one click:** the badge links to the home's page, which has the existing **Apply credit** button.
- **Nothing extra to look at:** homes without credit show no badge. If the credit can't be loaded, the page works as before, just without badges.

## Under the hood

- **One read per page:** the credits for all homes on the page come from a single database read.
