## Summary

Residents have a new public page with a short video on getting started: signing in the first time, putting HomeownerHub on the phone, and finding dues, requests, booking and help. Boards have a ready-made welcome email to introduce HomeownerHub to their community. House photos are now shown only when the app is sure it has the right house.

## What's new

### Residents
- A public "Getting started" page at /welcome with a short video (captions on screen) and the same steps written out below it.

### Boards
- Communications has a new "Send the welcome email" card. It opens a short, plain welcome email for your community: what to do first, what residents can do, and a link to the video. You review it, send yourself a test, and then send it. Send the invitations first, then this email a few minutes later.
- Association settings has a new "Where the community is" card for the community's city and ZIP code. Homes whose address is only the street use it to be found on the map.

### Properties
- A home whose address has only a street and no city or ZIP now shows the house drawing instead of a guessed photo, so a photo of the wrong house never appears. Fill in "Where the community is" to fix it for every home at once.

## Needs your action

- In Association settings, fill in "Where the community is" with your community's city and ZIP code.
- Before inviting all owners: OA-51, send sign-in emails from HomeownerHub through Resend.
