## Summary

Homes added before your association was set up are now billed correctly. The setup checklist is now one clear list that tells the truth. Georgia and Texas associations get a dashboard reminder about a state filing requirement.

## What's new

### Homes are billed from day one
- **The fix:** homes added before the association was set up were never included in dues. Now, setting up the association picks them up automatically.
- **A warning when it matters:** if any homes still aren't attached to an association, the dashboard, Properties and Dues show "N homes aren't assigned to an association" with a one-click button to fix it.

### One setup checklist
- **The same list everywhere:** the dashboard and the setup page now show the same checklist of nine steps, seven of them required. It covers the association and books, homes, residents, inviting the board, dues, resident payments and governing documents, plus bank and mailbox, which are optional.
- **Each tick is real:** "Invite the board" now ticks only when at least two board members are on the account. Inviting a resident no longer counts.
- **Importing a spreadsheet** is self-serve. You no longer need to contact support.

### State-law deadlines
- **Georgia:** a new law takes effect on January 1, 2027. Every owners' association must register with the Secretary of State, or it can't collect fines or fees, file liens or foreclose. The dashboard counts down, lists what to file (the fee is $100), and links to the official page. The board's morning email reminds you until you mark it done.
- **Texas:** a reminder to record your management certificate with the county and file it with the Texas Real Estate Commission. Until it's filed, owners don't owe attorney's fees or interest on late dues.
- **Not legal advice:** these reminders are informational. Check with your attorney.
