## Summary

HomeownerHub now sells one plan with every feature, including accounting, so boards no longer have to choose between two. This release keeps today's prices for now. Your chosen prices, $59 to $249 by community size, switch on in a follow-up release, once the matching prices exist in Stripe.

## What's new

### Plans
- The app and the website now offer a single plan, priced by the number of homes.
- Accounting, the mailbox, vendors and bids, and leases are included for every association.
- The billing page shows one simple choice: pay monthly or yearly.

## Under the hood

- Existing subscriptions keep their current price and limits. Nobody's bill changes, and nobody loses access.
- AI and text message limits are unchanged for every association.
