## Summary

The website has two more guides and one more free template. Georgia boards get a guide to the five-business-day statement of amounts due at resale. Texas boards get a guide to the resale certificate. Every board gets a printable annual budget and reserve worksheet. The law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-estoppel-letter-resale`: O.C.G.A. § 44-3-232(d). Who can ask for a statement of amounts due, what the request must say, the five-business-day deadline, what a late answer does to the lien against the buyer, the binding effect of the statement, and the $10.00 fee limit. It links to the attorney's-fee notice checklist.
- `/guides/texas-hoa-resale-certificate-chapter-207`: Tex. Prop. Code §§ 207.002 to 207.004. The 10-business-day deadline, what the certificate must contain, the $375 and $75 fee caps, no fee when late, the 180-day update window, and the owner's remedies.
- `/templates/hoa-annual-budget-worksheet`: a printable worksheet with income and expense lines, a reserve contribution line, the dues math, and a fill-in budget summary for owners. It links to the attorney's-fee notice checklist.

The State laws page now links the Texas guide, the treasurer guide links the worksheet and the checklist, and the new pages are in the sitemap.
