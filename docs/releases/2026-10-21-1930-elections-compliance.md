## Summary

Boards can plan a paper board election from nominations to results, with each state's dates worked out. A new Compliance page lists what state law asks of your association, with Georgia's obligations first. A fine hearing can now be closed when the owner fixes the violation before it.

## What's new

### Elections
- **Election planner:** from an annual or special meeting, open "Board election" to set seats and dates, list candidates and inspectors or tellers, and record results. The timeline is worked back from the meeting date for your state and flags any date that breaks a rule. A tie for the last seat is flagged, never broken by the app.
- **Print kit:** ballots that never name the voter, envelope labels, and an inspector record (California) or tellers' tally sheet.
- **Georgia meeting notice:** members' meetings are now checked against § 44-3-230(a): 21 days for an annual meeting, 7 for a special one.

### Compliance
- **New Compliance page:** every state-law obligation for your association in one list, with an overdue banner. For Georgia this includes the Secretary of State registration, the annual meeting notice, records, and the five-business-day statement of amounts due. The monthly board report and the board packet now include a compliance section.

### Violations
- **Cured before the hearing:** when a violation is resolved before its fine hearing, the hearing card offers to close the hearing and records why. Florida and California show the rule that applies. A closed hearing doesn't count toward a fine later.

### Insurance
- **HO-6 notice:** draft a notice telling owners what the association's master policy covers and suggesting their own unit coverage. It opens in the composer; nothing is sent automatically.
- **Handover kit:** now includes insurance, so incoming boards know where the policies are and who the agent is.

## Good to know
- The election planner and the Compliance page are for board members and admins only.
