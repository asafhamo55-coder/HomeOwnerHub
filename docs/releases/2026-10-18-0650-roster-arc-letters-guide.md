## Summary

Boards can now produce the owner roster and voter list that Florida and California law require, as of any date. The AI review of an architectural request now looks at the attached photos, and every decision comes with a ready-to-edit letter. Owners can update their own phone, email and mailing address from the portal; the board approves each change. The built-in user guide now has 42 articles, covering every main page of the app.

## What's new

### Owner roster and voter list
- **Properties → ⋯ → Owner roster & voter list.** Pick a list and an "as of" date, then download a CSV or print it.
- The roster shows each home's owners on that date, their ownership shares, the mailing address on file, and contact details.
- The roster is marked board-only, because it contains personal contact details.
- The voter list gives one vote per home. In Florida, homes more than 90 days behind are flagged "may be suspended, only if the board voted it". Nothing is ever suspended automatically. A 30% quorum figure is shown.
- The relevant Florida and California statutes are cited on the page, with a reminder to check them against your governing documents.

### ARC reviews
- The AI review now reads up to 3 attached photos and lists what they appear to show for each rule. These are observations only, never a reason to deny on their own.
- Approve, Approve with conditions, Deny and Request info each come with a full decision letter you can edit. A letter with an unfilled blank can't be sent.

### Owners update their own contact details
- In the portal, **Account → My contact details** lets an owner or resident send a new phone number, email for notices, or mailing address.
- The board sees each change, old and new, under **Approvals → Contact updates**. Approving updates that person's records everywhere.
- The property page shows "Updated by owner on [date]".

### Help and onboarding
- **25 new guide articles**, covering accounting reports, bank reconciliation, bills, payment plans, vendors and RFPs, leases, events, the HOA mailbox, communications, settings, state law and resident help.
- **New boards** see a "Your first week" card on the dashboard for 14 days. **New residents** see "New here?" on their first visits.

## Fixes

- **Inspect homes:** if your phone loses signal right after saving, retrying no longer creates a second draft.
