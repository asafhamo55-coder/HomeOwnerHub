## Summary

From January 1, 2027, Georgia's SB 406 pauses fines and fees while an owner's complaint to the Secretary of State is open. Boards can now record such a complaint on the home, and the app holds back fines, fee letters and late fees for that home while the pause runs. Georgia owners also get a page listing their twelve new rights, a button to request the association's insurance certificate, and boards get a printable 3-year financial packet.

## What's new

### Collections (Georgia)
- A "Secretary of State complaint" card on each home's collections file (board and admins only). Record the filing date and, later, the hearing officer's conclusions.
- From January 1, 2027, while the pause runs, the app blocks fines and collection letters that list fines or fees for that home, and the nightly job charges it no late fees. Dues letters still go out. Before 2027 it only warns. The only override is "attorney advised", and every override is logged.

### For owners (Georgia)
- "Your rights as an owner": the twelve rights from the new law, quoted word for word, each linked to where you use it in the app. They take effect January 1, 2027, and the page says so until then.
- Request the association's insurance certificate from the insurance page.

### Records (boards)
- A printable 3-year financial packet (balance sheets, income statements, adopted budgets) to answer owners' records requests.
