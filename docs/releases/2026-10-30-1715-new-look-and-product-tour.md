## Summary

The app and the website now look like one product. The app takes the website's violet colour and font, and the sign-in screens get a branded design. The homepage has a new "See it work" section with five realistic app screens, and the hero shows the product larger.

## What's new

### App
- New colours and font throughout the app: violet buttons, links and highlights, and the same font as the website. Light and dark mode both pass contrast checks.
- Sign-in, create account and forgot password now show a violet panel beside the form on desktop, and a violet header strip on phones.
- The logo is violet everywhere, including on the website.
- Text in app menus, badges and Copilot answers is at least 12 pixels.
- Property filters and sort links are easier to tap on phones.
- The dashboard opens with a welcome band showing your association's name, today's date and a "Review approvals" button. The number cards now have violet icon tiles and an arrow when they link somewhere. "Needs your attention" is a visible heading at the top. The same cards appear on about 20 other pages, so those change too.
- Wide tables in the financial packet and the annual report scroll inside their box on phones, instead of pushing the page sideways.
- The Fire Prevention Week reminder now follows the real week (Sunday to Saturday), not a fixed October 9.
- The favicon, the app icons, the link preview image and the daily digest email are violet too.

### Website
- A new "See it work" section on the homepage, with tabs for Approvals, Dues and credits, Violations, Announcements and Ask your documents. Each tab shows a large sample screen and three plain lines on what the board does there. All the data is sample data.
- The hero's product picture is larger and layered on desktop, and phones now see it too.
- The roadmap reads as cards on phones instead of a squeezed table.
- Small captions across the site are now 14 pixels.
- Three new Georgia guides on overpayments and credits, the SB 406 payment order, and a checklist for applying a credit. See the separate SEO note for links.
- Better for keyboard and screen-reader users: the Products menu works with the arrow keys, page landmarks are cleaner, and the demo form checks the email address as soon as you leave the field.

## Under the hood

- A new check stops colour classes that don't exist from slipping in. It found and fixed one on the compliance page.
- The release-note email uses the new violet.
- "Active boards" in the weekly numbers email no longer counts AI usage records, so it reflects boards that really used the app.

## Needs your action

- Sign in at about laptop width and on your phone, and check that the new sign-in screen looks right.
- Look at the dashboard home. The welcome band and new cards were checked by tests only, not by eye.
- Turn on dark mode and check the light lavender buttons and links aren't too pale.
- Tell Claude if the amber used for AI features should change to the website's mint.
