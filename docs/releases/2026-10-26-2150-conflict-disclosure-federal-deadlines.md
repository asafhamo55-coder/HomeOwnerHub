## Summary

Boards can now record each director's conflict-of-interest disclosure when awarding a bid or approving a vendor. For Georgia associations this becomes a required step from January 1, 2027, under SB 406. Every association also sees the two federal filing deadlines most HOAs face, the 1099-NEC and Form 1120-H, on Compliance and in the daily digest, linked to the year-end package for your CPA.

## What's new

### Conflict-of-interest disclosures
- On each bid and vendor page, every board member records "no conflict" or "conflict: recused" with a short note.
- **Georgia, from January 1, 2027:** the bid can't be awarded and the vendor can't be approved until every board member has disclosed. This is HomeownerHub's rule, built on SB 406's statement that owners can expect directors to disclose conflicts (the statute is quoted on the page). Before 2027 it's a recommended step; other states see it as good practice.
- Notes stay on the decision record and are never written to the activity log.

### Federal deadlines (every state)
- **1099-NEC:** for 2026 payments it is due **February 1, 2027** (January 31 is a Sunday). The 2026 reporting threshold is $2,000 (irs.gov).
- **Form 1120-H:** due the 15th day of the 4th month after the tax year ends (April 15 for a calendar year); Form 7004 extends it.
- Both rows can be marked done with evidence and link to the year-end package. They aren't tax advice; your CPA decides.
