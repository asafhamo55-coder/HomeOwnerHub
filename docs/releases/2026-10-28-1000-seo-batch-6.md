## Summary

The website has three new pages for self-managed boards, Georgia first. Each describes only features that exist today, quotes Georgia law only from the checked official text, carries a not-legal-advice note, and ends with "Book a demo".

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/give-hoa-cpa-attorney-limited-access`: how a board gives its CPA read-only books and its attorney only the matters it assigns, using Settings → Professionals. It quotes Georgia's attorney's-fee notice and records text word for word.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-certified-mail-collections-notices`: what Georgia's statutes say about certified mail and statutory overnight delivery, how the fee-notice and foreclosure waiting periods are worded (sent versus received), and what to record. It describes certified-mail tracking on a collections letter.
- `/templates/georgia-annual-meeting-report-checklist`: a free, printable checklist for the report to owners at the annual meeting (§ 44-3-230(a)), with each item tagged statute or best practice.

The Georgia hub's lists and related links now include them, and they are in the sitemap.
