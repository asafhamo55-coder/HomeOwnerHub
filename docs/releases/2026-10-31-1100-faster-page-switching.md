## Summary

Moving between pages used to show an empty grey outline for a moment while the page loaded. The menus now start loading a page as soon as you point at it, so most pages appear with their data on the click.

## What's new

### Navigation
- The board sidebar, the resident tab bar, the professional sidebar and the platform admin sidebar load the page you point at or touch, before you click.
- A page loaded this way is reused for up to 30 seconds. After that, the next visit loads it fresh, so you never see data older than that.
