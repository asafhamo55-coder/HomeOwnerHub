## Summary

The website has three more state guides: Florida and California records inspection, and the Texas management certificate. Each quotes the law only from the checked official text, carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo". Each page also says plainly what HomeownerHub does not do for that topic.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/florida-hoa-official-records-inspection`: Fla. Stat. § 720.303(5). The 10-business-day deadline, 7-year retention, inspection by screen or phone camera, the rule against asking an owner for a reason, the one 8-hour day a month floor, copy fees, the $50 a day minimum damages, and the records an owner cannot see.
- `/guides/california-hoa-records-inspection-rules`: Cal. Civ. Code §§ 5200, 5210 and 5220. What counts as an association record, the three-fiscal-year window, permanent minutes, the 10-business-day and 30-calendar-day response times, election materials, and the membership-list opt-out. It says which neighboring sections the checked text does not cover.
- `/guides/texas-hoa-management-certificate`: Tex. Prop. Code § 209.004. What the certificate holds, recording in each county, the 30-day amendment clock, the 7-day filing with the Texas Real Estate Commission, and what a lapse does to the association's lien and collection fees.

The State laws page now links each guide under its state, and the new pages are in the sitemap.
