## Summary

The website has three new Georgia guides. One covers how owners can take control of the association from the developer if it misses basic duties. One covers who owes unpaid dues when a lot is sold or foreclosed. One covers when an HOA can charge some lots more than others. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-owners-take-control-from-declarant-44-3-232-1`: the six duties a developer must meet, the written notice, the 30 days to cure, the court step and the attorney fees.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-unpaid-assessments-when-a-lot-is-sold-44-3-225`: what a buyer owes for the seller's unpaid dues, how a statement caps it, and what a lender that forecloses owes.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-charging-some-lots-not-all-44-3-225`: the three cases where costs can fall on some lots only, and the limit on routine maintenance.

All three are listed on the Guides page, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K), in the sitemap and on the Georgia page.
