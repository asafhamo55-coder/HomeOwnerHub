## Summary

The website has three new printable templates for Georgia boards. One is an architectural change request form for exterior changes. One is a welcome letter for new owners. One is a board resolution that adopts rules and regulations. Each has a checklist, a fill-in document, the Georgia text behind it, and a "Book a demo" button. Each says plainly to check your declaration and ask your attorney, and that HomeownerHub does not decide requests, adopt rules or give legal advice.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/templates/georgia-hoa-architectural-change-request-form`: what an owner describes and attaches, what the board records, and the Georgia text on approving exterior changes.
- `/templates/georgia-hoa-new-owner-welcome-letter`: dues, documents, rules, meetings and contacts in one letter.
- `/templates/georgia-hoa-rules-adoption-resolution`: authority, quorum, vote, effective date and delivery of a rule to owners.

All three are listed on the Templates page, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K) and in the sitemap. The architectural review, board powers and owner compliance guides now link to them.
