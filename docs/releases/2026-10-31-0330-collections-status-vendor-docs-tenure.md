## Summary

Three things that were half built now work. A board can close, write off or reopen a collection case, and the close date and reason are kept. Uploaded vendor documents can be opened, not just deleted. Many homes can be marked owner-occupied or leased in one step. Residents see which announcements are new, and the board can search past messages.

## What's new

### Collections
- On a property with an open case, "Change case status" offers Resolved (paid) and Written off. Each asks for a short reason. Closed cases show their close date and reason, and a closed case can be reopened.
- Writing off a case only changes its status. It does not change what the owner owes.

### Vendors
- Each uploaded document (insurance certificate, W-9, license) has a View button and shows its expiry date.

### Properties
- Tick homes on the properties list and choose "Set tenure" to mark them owner-occupied or leased together, up to 200 at a time. The "Tenure unknown" view makes this easy to start. Marking a home as leased clears its waiting-list entries, just as it does for one home.

### Resident announcements
- New announcements are marked "New". Category chips filter the list, and long messages fold up with a "Read more" link.

### Communications
- A search box finds past messages by subject. Counts at the top show messages sent in the last 30 days, drafts, and messages with a failed delivery.
