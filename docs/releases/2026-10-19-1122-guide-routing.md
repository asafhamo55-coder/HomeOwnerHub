title: Ask the guide sends how-to questions to the right place
---

## Summary

When you ask the guide how to do something in the app, it now answers from the user guide, even if your question uses words like "allowed", "how long can" or "declaration". Questions about your association's own rules and governing documents still go to Ask the Docs.

## What's new

- **Better routing:** how-to questions such as "how long can a resident's ARC request stay open?" or "is a board member allowed to reply from the inbox?" now get a guide answer, not a document search.
- **Better answers:** the guide now understands "company", "contractor", "plumber" and "landscaper" as vendor questions, and ranks whole articles as well as single sections.
- A few guide articles (text messages, inbox replies, waiting list, the ARC rules check, support tickets) have clearer wording, so they are easier to find.

## Under the hood

- 33 new test questions cover both directions. The guide's answer accuracy went from 93.8% to 99.5% on 252 test questions.
