---
title: Tell residents about decisions, and a smarter Ask the guide
---

## Summary

Boards can now have HomeownerHub email residents when the board decides their ARC application, asks them for more information, or replies to their support request. It's off until a board turns it on. Ask the guide also sends how-to questions to the right place more reliably.

## What's new

### Telling residents about decisions and replies
- A new switch in **Settings → Notifications → "Tell residents about decisions and replies"**. It is **off** for every association until a board member turns it on.
- When it's on, residents get an email when the board approves or denies their ARC application, asks for more information, or replies to their ticket. Residents who opted in to texts also get a short text.
- Internal notes are never sent. Residents' own email and text preferences are respected, and each decision or reply is sent only once.
- If sending fails, the board's decision is still saved.

### Ask the guide
- How-to questions that use words like "allowed", "how long can" or "declaration" now get an answer from the user guide. Questions about your association's own rules and documents still go to Ask the Docs.
- The guide now treats "company", "contractor", "plumber" and "landscaper" as vendor questions. Several articles were reworded so they're easier to find.

## Under the hood

- The guide scored 99.5% on 252 test questions, up from 93.8%.
- The screens for the next database update (electronic-notice consent, "notice posted" on meetings, "visible to owners" on documents) are built but stay hidden until that update is applied.
