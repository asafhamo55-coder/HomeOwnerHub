## Summary

The owner finished tonight's database updates, so every feature that was waiting on them is now switched on.

## What's new

### Records requests
- **For owners:** they can ask for association records from their portal (Requests → Request association records).
- **For the board:** each request shows under Tickets → Records requests with its legal deadline (Florida and California). The chip turns amber or red as the deadline nears, the morning email reminds you, and **Mark fulfilled** closes the request.

### Violations
- **"Fined" and "Dismissed"** can now be saved. Before tonight's update they couldn't.
- **Fine hearings:** an owner can ask for a hearing from their violation page. The board schedules it, records the panel and outcome, and drafts the hearing notice. The state-law check takes the hearing into account.

### Insurance
- **A new Insurance page** (under Money) keeps your policies and fidelity bond in one place, with renewal reminders at 90, 60 and 30 days, and a coverage check against Florida and California rules.

### Inbox
- **Snooze:** set a conversation aside until a date. It comes back when the resident writes again.
- **First reply:** each conversation shows how long the first reply took.
- **Filed attachments:** emails show "Filed to library" when an attachment was saved to Documents, and those documents link back to the email.
- **Unverified sender:** a red "Unverified sender" warning appears when an email fails its sender checks. Contact-detail changes from those emails are blocked.

### Money and compliance
- **Opening balances** you enter are never charged late fees.
- **State-law deadlines** marked done are saved properly.

## Good to know
- No one has checked these screens in a browser yet. Please open Insurance and the inbox once.
