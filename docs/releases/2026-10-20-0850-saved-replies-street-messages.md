## Summary

Answering residents is faster: the inbox now has saved replies and works well on a phone. Announcements can go to a single street, you can see exactly how a message will look on a phone before sending, and after sending you can see who got it and remind the ones who didn't open it.

## What's new

### Inbox
- **Saved replies:** 10 ready-made answers (paying dues, portal sign-in, ARC how-to, pool hours, gate fobs, trash, parking, noise, records requests, resale certificates). Your community's own details are left as blanks you must fill in before approving, so nothing wrong goes out.
- **Insert into reply:** after turning an email into a ticket, violation or ARC link, one click adds a short "here's what we did" line to your reply.
- **On a phone:** a summary strip shows the home, balance and open items at the top. A bar at the bottom has Approve, Edit and Close.
- **Safer contact-detail requests:** a request labelled as coming from the inbox is now trusted only when a board member filed it.

### Communications
- **Urgent or routine:** tick "This is urgent" to add a text message (only if texting is set up). Routine messages go by email and the portal.
- **Phone preview:** tabs show the email, text, portal and Spanish versions exactly as residents will see them. "Send a test to me" emails only you.
- **Streets:** pick "A street" to message just the homes on Elm St. When the AI draft mentions a street, it picks it for you.
- **Plain-language audiences:** All homes, All owners, Renters, Homes behind on dues, One home.
- **"Did people get it?":** the results page says how many homes received and opened the message. "Email again to the N who haven't opened" sets up a reminder for you to review; nothing goes out until you press Send.
- **Less clutter:** rarely used settings are now under "More options".

## Good to know
- Text messages still show as "sent", not "delivered". Delivery receipts need a later database update.
- No one has checked these screens in a browser yet. Please try the inbox on your phone and open New message once, and tell us if anything looks off.
