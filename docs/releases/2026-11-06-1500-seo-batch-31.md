## Summary

The website has three new guides for board members. Two cover Florida: the annual budget, optional reserves and the yearly financial report, and how members recall a director. One covers Texas: the right of an owner to bring a Chapter 209 claim against the association in justice court. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly that HomeownerHub does not handle these steps.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/florida-hoa-budget-reserves-and-financial-reports-720-303`: the budget, reserve accounts, waiving reserves and the financial report tiers.
- `/guides/florida-hoa-board-director-recall-720-303`: recall by written ballot or meeting and the board's 5 business day step.
- `/guides/texas-hoa-owner-suit-justice-court-209-017`: what Section 209.017 says and the definitions it relies on.

All three are listed on the Guides page, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K) and in the sitemap.
