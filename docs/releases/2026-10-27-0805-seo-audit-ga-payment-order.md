## Summary

The website got a full search-engine checkup. Every page now has a share image, titles and descriptions sized for Google, and lighter, faster pages. Georgia boards also get a "Record payment" button that splits an owner's payment in the order SB 406 requires from January 1, 2027.

## What's new

### Website (SEO checkup)
- Every page now shows a preview image when shared on social media or in messages.
- Page titles and descriptions now fit what Google displays, and each is unique.
- Two pages that nothing linked to (Docs and Blog) are now reachable from the footer.
- The heaviest pages load less code.
- An automatic check now keeps these rules from slipping.

### Recording payments (Georgia)
- On a home's dues, Georgia boards can use **Record payment** to enter one payment that covers several charges.
- From January 1, 2027 the app splits it in SB 406's order: regular dues first, then special assessments, then late fees and fines, then everything else. Interest, collection costs and payment-plan installments go last, because the law doesn't name them. The screen says to ask your attorney about those.
- You see the split before saving, and nothing already recorded is changed.
- Before 2027 (and outside Georgia), payments go oldest charge first, as today.
- For now, record the payment in one go. If a part fails, the screen says so, and making the whole payment save as one step is next (BL-177).
