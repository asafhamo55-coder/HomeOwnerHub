---
title: Safer handling of refunds and returned payments
---

## Summary

This release prepares online payments, which are still switched off. When a refund, returned payment or dispute arrives while the books are closed for the month, it now waits and tries again, so nothing is lost. If a refund fails after it was recorded, the board is told once.

## What's better

### Online payments (still switched off)
- A refund, returned bank payment or lost dispute that comes in while no accounting month is open now waits and retries for up to 3 days. The board gets one message asking them to open the month.
- If a refund fails after it was recorded, the amount goes back on the home's balance and the board gets one message to check it in Stripe and contact the resident.
- If one refund on a payment was already recorded and a second one had to wait, the second is now recorded in full. Before, it could be recorded short.

## Under the hood

- A test now fails if the database wording these retries depend on ever changes.
