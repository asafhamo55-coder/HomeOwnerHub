---
title: Correct closed-year reports, open next year, and fair-use limits on AI
---

## Summary

Reports for a closed fiscal year now show that year's real income and expenses. They had been showing them as nearly zero. Admins can also open next year's books ahead of time, so a drafted budget can be saved, and the app stays on the current year until the new one actually starts. AI features residents can use now have generous hourly and daily limits that keep costs predictable.

## What's new

### Open next fiscal year
- On **Accounting → Fiscal periods**, admins can choose **Open next fiscal year**. It follows the books' existing pattern (yearly, quarterly or monthly) and keeps the same start month.
- The current year stays current until the new year begins in the association's own time zone. Dues, bills and recurring entries keep posting to the right year.
- The budget draft page offers the same button when next year isn't open yet. After opening it, your draft is still there and **Save** turns on.

### Fair-use limits on AI
- The rules check on ARC requests allows 10 checks per person per hour. Ask the Docs, Ask the guide and "Improve with AI" allow 30 per person per hour. Each also has a daily limit per community.
- If someone reaches a limit, they see when they can try again. They can still submit their application.
- Board-only AI tools are not limited.

## Fixes

- **Closed-year reports:** once a year was closed, the income statement, budget vs. actual, the board packet and the month-end report showed its income and expenses as about zero. They now show the real activity. The balance sheet was already correct and still is.
- **The ARC rules-check button** no longer runs off the edge of a phone screen.
- **Guide screenshots:** 17 screenshots were refreshed to match the current screens.

## Under the hood

- Screenshots are now taken automatically in GitHub's checks on every change. They catch layout problems (two so far) and keep the guide's pictures up to date.
