## Summary

The website has two new pages that list every guide and every template in one place, grouped by state. Three new guides were added: a Georgia estoppel letter guide, a Texas resale certificate guide, and an annual budget worksheet. In the app, the standard agenda again cites the Georgia law that calls for reports to owners at the annual meeting.

## What's new

### Website
- /guides lists every guide, grouped by Georgia, Florida, California, Texas, and any state.
- /templates lists every template and checklist.
- Both pages are linked from the footer, and new guides show up on them automatically.
- New pages:
  - /guides/georgia-hoa-estoppel-letter-resale
  - /guides/texas-hoa-resale-certificate-chapter-207
  - /templates/hoa-annual-budget-worksheet

### Meetings
- For a Georgia annual meeting, "Insert standard agenda" again adds "Annual report to the owners (O.C.G.A. § 44-3-230(a))". The law's own words: "At the annual meeting, comprehensive reports of the affairs, finances, and budget projections of the association shall be made to the lot owners."

## Fixes

- Restored the Georgia citation on the agenda's owners' report line. It had been removed by mistake earlier today.
