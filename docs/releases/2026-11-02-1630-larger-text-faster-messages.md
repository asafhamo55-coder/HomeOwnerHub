title: Larger text, a faster message list, restyled state pages and three new Georgia guides
---

## Summary

Small text in the app is now larger and easier to read, and addresses are no longer cut off. The message list should open faster, and overdue architecture requests now appear in "Do this next". The website's state pages match the rest of the site, and three new Georgia guides are live.

## What's new

### App
- The smallest text in the app is now 14px instead of 13px.
- Addresses wrap onto a second line instead of being cut off.
- "Do this next" on the dashboard now flags architecture requests that are past their decide-by date.

### Website
- The states page and the Florida, California and Texas pages now match the rest of the site, with Georgia featured first.
- The Blog and Docs pages no longer overflow on phones.

## Fixes

- The message list no longer loads the full text of every incoming email just to show the newest one in each conversation. The improvement is not yet measured on the live site.
- Fewer links load their pages ahead of time.
- The automatic screenshot check passes again. It had failed on page overflow and on an out-of-date button name.

## Under the hood

- Nothing about payments, voting or other features in progress is included in this release.
