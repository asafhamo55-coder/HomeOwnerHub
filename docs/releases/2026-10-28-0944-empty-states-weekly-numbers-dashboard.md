title: Clearer next steps on new screens, a weekly numbers email, and a faster dashboard
---

## Summary

Settings → Professionals and the collections mail-tracking panel now tell you what to do next when they're empty. A weekly email with your usage numbers is ready, and turns on once you choose where it goes. The dashboard now reads your association list once per page view instead of eight times.

## What's new

### Clearer next steps
- **Settings → Professionals:**
  - When no one has been invited, the page explains what it's for and offers **Invite a CPA or attorney**.
  - While no Treasurer is set, a note explains that money approvals go to the Treasurer first once one is set. Admins get a link to set it.
- **Collections letters:** a mailed Georgia notice with nothing recorded explains why to record how and when it was mailed. The fee and foreclosure clocks count from it, and only certified mail or statutory overnight delivery starts a clock.

### Weekly numbers email (for the owner)
- **When:** every Monday morning.
- **What it shows:** a counts-only summary of new HOAs and associations, active boards, approvals created and approved, announcements sent and AI drafts, each against the week before.
- **Privacy:** no names, emails or addresses.
- **Off by default:** it sends nothing until an address is set (owner action OA-38).

### Faster dashboard
- **One read:** eight dashboard cards now share a single read of your association list per page view.
- **Isolation:** each request reads only its own HOA's data.

## Needs your action

- **OA-38 (whenever):** choose where the weekly numbers email goes.
- **Settings → Professionals:** set your Treasurer.
