## Website

- **New pricing page.**
  - Move the slider to your number of homes to see the monthly or yearly price for both plans.
  - It shows everything included, grouped by area, and marks which items are in Complete only.
  - A plain table shows what is free and what costs extra. Online card and bank payments are listed as coming soon.
- **A page for each main feature:**
  - /features/dues-and-accounting
  - /features/violations
  - /features/announcements
  - /features/architecture-requests
  - /features/ask-your-documents
- **Sample screens.**
  - They now show real example text, not grey placeholder lines.
  - The "Ask your documents" example now shows a second cited answer. Before, it showed the message you get when your documents don't cover a question.
- **Compare and About pages** end with the same free-trial buttons as the homepage.
