---
title: Property pages now say why a home has no photo
---

## Summary

When a home shows the house drawing instead of a street picture, the property page now says why, in a short sentence, and links to the fix when the board can make one.

## What's new

### Properties
- A home with no photo shows one of these lines under the drawing: "No photo: we need your community's city and ZIP to find this street" (with a link to "Where the community is" in Association settings), "Google has no street picture for this address", "Photos are turned off", or "Google could not be reached, try later".
