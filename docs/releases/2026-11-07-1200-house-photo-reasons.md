---
title: Property pages now say why a home has no photo
---

## Summary

When a home shows the house drawing instead of a street picture, the property page now says why, in a short sentence, and links to the fix when the board can make one.

## What's new

### Properties
- A home with no photo shows one of these lines under the drawing:
  - "No photo: we need your community's city and ZIP to find this street" (board members get a link to "Where the community is" in Association settings; residents are told the board needs to add it).
  - "No photo: this home has no street address yet".
  - "Google has no street picture for this address".
  - "Photos are turned off".
  - "Google could not be reached. Try again later."
- The picture is now loaded once, only when it scrolls near the screen, so the page makes no extra requests.
