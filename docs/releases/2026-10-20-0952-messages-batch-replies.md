## Summary

The inbox and announcements now live together under **Messages**. Boards can answer a group of residents who asked the same thing in one go, file email attachments into Documents, and never lose a half-written announcement. Contact-detail changes are now tied to the resident's own login, and several screens work better with screen readers and keyboards.

## What's new

### Messages
- **One place:** the sidebar now has a single **Messages** item with three tabs: Inbox, Sent to residents and Drafts. Old links still work.
- **New message:** one button asks whether you're writing to one resident or to many homes.
- **Replies to announcements:** a read-only view of residents' replies to your announcements, with the home's dues, violations and ARC applications alongside.

### Inbox
- **Answer similar questions together:** when several residents ask the same thing, a banner offers "Answer all". You pick or write one reply, confirm, and each resident gets their own separate email. You can undo for 30 seconds.
- **Save to Documents:** file an email attachment straight into your documents library. You're warned before filing it as a type every resident can see.
- **Smarter drafts:** when a verified resident sends a PDF or document, the AI draft can use short excerpts from it. These excerpts are never presented as your governing documents.

### Announcements
- **Drafts save automatically** while you type. Find them under the Drafts tab and pick up where you left off.
- **Results by home:** one row per home, showing which channels reached it.

### Vendors
- **Insurance expiry:** vendors whose insurance certificate is expiring or expired get a badge and appear on the dashboard. **Request updated certificate** drafts a polite email for you to review and send.
- **Umbrella coverage minimum:** a new field in vendor insurance settings, used in bid requests and compliance checks.
- **Change:** the app no longer emails vendors automatically about expiring insurance. The board decides when to send. (Owner action OA-19 asks whether to turn automatic emails back on.)

### Security and accessibility
- **Contact-detail changes** are now tied to the account that actually filed them. Typed-in names or emails can't redirect a change to someone else's record.
- **Accessibility:** better contrast on the phone preview tabs, larger buttons on phones, screen-reader announcements for saving and undo, and keyboard focus that no longer gets lost in dialogs.

### Behind the scenes (not visible yet)
- **New pricing plans** are built and switched off. Checkout still charges today's price.
- **"Unverified sender" warnings** for faked email addresses are built. They switch on after a database update (owner action OA-17).

## Good to know
- No one has checked these screens in a browser yet. Please open Messages once and tell us if anything looks off.
