title: Owner budget notice from your approved budget
---

## Summary

Once your annual budget is approved, the board can prepare the budget notice owners are owed, straight from the budget. Print it, or turn it into an announcement draft to review and send as usual.

## What's new

- On an approved budget, select **Prepare owner budget notice**.
- The notice lists each income and expense line, the totals, and the regular assessment per home. That figure comes from the dues you've billed, or from the budget when every home pays the same.
- **Florida (§ 720.303(6)):** includes the operating budget and the line saying owners can get a copy at no charge. Surplus or deficit, and amenity fees, are blanks you fill in.
- **California (§ 5300):** labelled **Partial**. It lists the items of the annual budget report that the app doesn't produce, such as the reserve study summary and the § 5570 form.
- Reserve figures are always blanks for you to fill in.
- Nothing can be printed or drafted until every blank is filled and you tick **I've checked these figures**.
- **Create announcement draft** opens a new message with the notice filled in. You review it and send it the normal way. Nothing is sent automatically.
