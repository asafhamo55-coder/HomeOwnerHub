## Summary

www.homeownerledger.online has a new color scheme that Asaf picked: Royal purple. Buttons, links, highlights and the logo are a rich purple, and the dark bands at the top and bottom of pages are a deep purple gradient. Words, pages and layout are the same as before. The HOA app keeps its current colors.

## What's new

### On www.homeownerledger.online
- Brand purple for buttons, links and highlights, with light lilac tints behind cards and boxes.
- The hero, "how it works", closing bands and the footer use a deep purple gradient, and their cards match it.
- The logo tile and the "Hub" in the wordmark are purple on the website, and the browser-tab icon matches.

## Under the hood

- Every text and button color was checked for readability. The lowest contrast is 4.8 to 1, above the 4.5 to 1 accessibility minimum.
- An independent review found two leftovers from the old colors, and both were fixed before release.
- No app change in this release. No screenshots: the automatic screenshots cover the app, not the website.
