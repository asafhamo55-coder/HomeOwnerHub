## Summary

Georgia associations now see a "Proposed rules (not in force)" card on the Compliance page. It covers the Secretary of State's draft rules for association registration, records, complaints and board conduct. The card says plainly that nothing in it is required yet, gives the planned effective date, and lists the comment deadline and hearing dates from the official notices.

## What's new

### Compliance
- A card for Georgia associations with a short summary of each of the seven parts, the rule numbers, the hearing times, and a few exact quotes from the draft text. The comment deadline line disappears once the deadline has passed.

## Under the hood

- The draft text is kept in the repository with the official PDFs, and a test checks every quote on the card word for word against it.

## Needs your action

- Open Compliance for a Georgia association and read the card. Comments are due Monday, November 2, 2026.
