## Summary

The website has three new pages that show how a self-managed board uses the workspace day to day. Each describes only features that exist today, says the board approves every draft, and ends with "Book a demo".

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/ai-hoa-violation-letters-board-approval`: how to use AI to draft a violation letter that cites the rule, with the board approving it before anything is sent. It quotes Georgia's rules-and-fines and injunction text word for word.
- `/guides/ai-hoa-meeting-minutes`: how to turn a meeting transcript into draft minutes, what the secretary must still check, and Georgia's permanent-records and board-quorum text.
- `/templates/hoa-announcement-templates`: a free, printable send checklist and a fill-in event notice for Halloween, holidays, pet rules and other community announcements. It works in any state.

The Georgia hub's related lists and the minutes and agenda templates now link to them.
