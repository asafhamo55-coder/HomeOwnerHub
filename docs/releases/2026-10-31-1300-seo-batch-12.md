## Summary

The website has one more Georgia guide and two more free templates. The guide explains what Georgia law lets a property owners' association board do. The templates list the fields for a proxy form and a written ballot, and for a payment plan agreement. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-board-powers-44-3-231`: O.C.G.A. § 44-3-231. The powers to hire, improve the common area, approve lot changes, enforce the documents, borrow and amend to match the law, the rule that the board acts through the officers, and the duty to keep minutes and financial records. It links to the amending covenants guide.
- `/templates/georgia-hoa-proxy-and-written-ballot-form`: the fields a proxy appointment form and a written ballot should carry, built from O.C.G.A. §§ 14-3-724 and 14-3-707, with a fill-in proxy form.
- `/templates/georgia-hoa-payment-plan-agreement`: the fields of a written payment plan agreement, with a fill-in sample. The page says the Georgia text we hold has no payment plan rule, quotes only late charge, interest and payment order text, and gives no advice on what a plan may contain.

The written ballots guide links the proxy and ballot form, the payment order guide links the payment plan agreement, and the new pages are listed on the Guides and Templates pages and in the sitemap.
