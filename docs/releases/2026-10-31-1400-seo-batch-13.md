## Summary

The website has one more California guide and two more free state templates. The guide explains the internal dispute resolution process California associations must offer. The templates list the fields for a Texas payment plan agreement and a Florida fine hearing notice. The Guides and Templates pages now have a search box, and the search palette on the site (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K) finds every guide and template. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/california-hoa-internal-dispute-resolution`: Civil Code §§ 5900 to 5920. Which disputes it covers, the minimum rules for the procedure, who can start it and who must take part, the meet and confer fallback, and the annual notice to members. The page says HomeownerHub does not run or track dispute resolution requests.
- `/templates/texas-hoa-payment-plan-agreement`: the fields of a written payment plan agreement, from Tex. Prop. Code §§ 209.0062 and 209.0063, with a fill-in sample.
- `/templates/florida-hoa-fine-hearing-notice`: a checklist and a fill-in notice of the right to a hearing before a fine or suspension, from Fla. Stat. § 720.305(2).

The new pages are listed on the Guides and Templates pages, on the Florida, California and Texas pages, and in the sitemap.

### Search on the website
- Guides and Templates have a "Search" box that narrows the cards by title and description as you type. It shows how many pages match, and a message with a way back when none do. Without JavaScript the full list still shows.
- The Cmd-K search palette now finds every guide and template, labelled Guide or Template.
