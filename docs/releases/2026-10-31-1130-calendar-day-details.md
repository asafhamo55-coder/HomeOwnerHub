## Summary

A green day on the dashboard calendar meant "on track" but did not say what was on track. Clicking a coloured day now shows its plan under the calendar.

## What's new

### Dashboard
- Coloured days on the compliance calendar can be clicked or tapped. The details appear under the calendar: for example "12 dues charges due, not late yet" or "1 violation with a cure deadline on this day, still in the cure period".
- The calendar opens on the next day that has something on it.
- Each line links to Dues or Violations.
