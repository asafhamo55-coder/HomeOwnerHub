## Summary

Boards can now email owners a receipt for a payment, either automatically (if you switch it on) or with one click. We reviewed every new screen from today's screenshots and fixed a dozen small problems. Several more security improvements are also included.

## What's new

### Payment receipts
- **Automatic receipts (off unless you turn them on):** Settings → Resident payments → "Email owners a receipt when we record their payment".
- **Send receipt:** a button on any Dues row with a payment sends that owner a receipt, even with automatic receipts off.
- **What's in it:** each owner gets their own email with the amount, date, method and remaining balance, plus a link to their ledger. Owners who turned off email aren't sent one, and each payment gets at most one automatic receipt.

### Screen fixes
- **Inbox on phones:** the Approve button is no longer cut off.
- **Inbox side panel:** it explains in words how an email was matched to a home.
- **Dashboard:**
  - Overdue homes show their address.
  - "1 units" now reads "1 unit".
  - Minutes for meetings that haven't happened yet are no longer listed as waiting for approval.
- **Messages:**
  - Clearer captions and urgent-message hints.
  - The remaining "Communications" labels now say "Messages".
  - The empty Drafts tab no longer offers "Clear filters".
- **Resident Ask:** the sample questions are separated properly.

### Security
- **Simultaneous billing updates** always settle on the newest one.
- **Platform invitations** go through the safety guard.
- **Law-loader downloads** are size-limited.

### Ready for the next database update (switched off until then)
- **When it switches on:** after the owner applies runbook section J.
- **What switches on:** late fees will skip opening balances, inbox snooze, first-reply timing, links between emails and filed documents, and saved "done" marks for state-law deadlines.

## Good to know
