## Summary

The website has three new Georgia guides. One explains the terms the Georgia Property Owners' Association Act defines, such as declarant, common area, common expenses and instrument. One covers incorporation, the words an association's name must include, who the members are and how owners get copies of the articles and bylaws. One covers what the Act says when an association's recorded paperwork has a defect. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-act-definitions-44-3-221`: the eight defined terms boards meet most often, quoted from the Act.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-incorporation-name-and-bylaws-44-3-227`: incorporation under Title 14, the five name words, membership, the first board and copies of the articles and bylaws.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-defects-substantial-compliance-44-3-233`: liberal reading, substantial compliance, and the two ways to cure a defect.

All three are listed on the Guides page, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K), in the sitemap and on the Georgia page.
