## Summary

The one plan now sells at your chosen prices, and the website's comparison page covers the whole market instead of one company. Existing subscriptions keep their current price until renewal, and boards get a notice first.

## What's new

### Prices
- 1 to 50 homes: $59 a month or $590 a year.
- 51 to 100 homes: $109 a month or $1,090 a year.
- 101 to 200 homes: $169 a month or $1,690 a year.
- 201 to 400 homes: $249 a month or $2,490 a year.
- Over 400 homes: $0.65 per home a month or $6.50 per home a year.
- The 30-day free trial with no card stays.

### Website comparison
- The compare page now lists HomeownerHub with 11 other products side by side, and PayHOA is one row among them.
- Each competitor's details link to their own website, with the date we checked. What we could not confirm says "Not confirmed".
- The page says plainly what HomeownerHub does not have yet, including online card and bank payments.
- The separate PayHOA page is gone, and its old address now opens the compare page.

## Under the hood

- New checkouts use the new Stripe prices. If a price were ever missing, checkout shows a clear error and never charges an old price.
