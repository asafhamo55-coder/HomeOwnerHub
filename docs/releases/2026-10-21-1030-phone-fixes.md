## Summary

Two small fixes for phones on screens switched on last night.

## What's new

### Fixes
- **Quorum kit sign-in sheet:** on a phone the table now scrolls sideways inside its own box, with a hint, instead of stretching the whole page. Printing is unchanged.
- **Insurance:** policy rows stay inside their card on narrow screens. Long carrier names wrap, and the expiry badge and buttons move to a new line.

## Good to know
- These were found by our automatic screenshot checks on the new screens.
