---
title: Estoppel certificates from the property page
---

## Summary

When a home is being sold and the title company asks for an estoppel certificate, the board can now prepare one from the property page. The app fills it in from the home's account and shows the legal deadline and fee limits.

## What's new

- On a property, open the **⋯** menu and select **Prepare estoppel certificate** (board and admins only).
- The certificate is filled in from the home's record:
  - the balance, and charges not yet due;
  - the regular assessment and the paid-through date;
  - special assessments;
  - open violations;
  - any payment plan;
  - the collections stage.
- **Florida (§ 720.30851):**
  - It's due within 10 business days of the request.
  - It's valid for 30 days, or 35 if mailed.
  - The fee is capped at $250, plus $150 if the account is delinquent and $100 for delivery within 3 business days. The page reminds you to check the state's current inflation-adjusted amounts.
- **California (§ 4525/§ 4530):** due within 10 days. The certificate covers the statement of amounts owed and the open violation notices, and lists the other documents the resale packet needs.
- Anything the app doesn't know shows as a blank to fill in. You can't print until every blank is filled and you tick **I've verified these figures**.
- Nothing is emailed. You print it or save it as a PDF. Each certificate prepared or printed is recorded in the activity log.
