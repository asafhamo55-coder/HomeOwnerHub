## Summary

The portal used by your CPA or attorney now shows a loading screen while pages load, and a friendly retry if a page fails. Tasks and RFPs show the key numbers at the top. Law updates can be corrected in place. Two new state templates are live, and the Georgia special assessments guide was improved.

## What's new

### Professionals portal
- Pages show a loading screen while they load. If a page fails, it says "This page didn't load" and offers Try again, instead of a bare error.

### Tasks
- Counts at the top: Overdue, Due in 7 days, Unassigned, Done in the last 30 days. Each count opens that list.

### RFPs
- Counts at the top: Draft, Open, Closing within 7 days, Awarded. Each count filters the list.
- A Bids column shows how many bids each RFP has received (submitted, awarded or declined).
- Open RFPs show "Due in N days".

### Law updates
- Platform admins can edit a law update to fix a typo or a date, instead of deleting it and starting over.

### Website
- New pages:
  - /templates/california-hoa-hearing-resolution-letter
  - /templates/florida-hoa-records-request-response-checklist
- Improved: /georgia/special-assessments now covers how Georgia law defines an assessment, and how special assessments work for condominiums.
