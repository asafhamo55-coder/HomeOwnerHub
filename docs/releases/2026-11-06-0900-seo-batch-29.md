## Summary

The website has three new Georgia guides for board members. One covers what Georgia law says about fines and suspensions in a condominium association. One covers the lien, the statement of amounts due and the five business days, and says plainly where the law is silent on a release. One covers how a condominium divides its common expenses. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/georgia-condo-fines-and-suspensions-44-3-76`: the power to fine and suspend, the limits, utility services and the notice the law names.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-lien-paid-off-payoff-statement-and-release-44-3-232`: who can ask for the statement, the deadline, the fee limit and how long the lien lasts.
- `/guides/georgia-condo-common-expense-assessments-44-3-80`: limited common elements, expenses for some units, the declaration formula and no exemption for nonuse.

All three are listed on the Guides page, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K), in the sitemap and on the Georgia page.
