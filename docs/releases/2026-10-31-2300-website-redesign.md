## Website

- **See each feature work.** Every feature page now plays its process step by step, in a screen that looks like the app. You can pause it, go back or jump to any step:
  - violations, from photo to approved letter;
  - announcements, through to the "Send to N homes?" check;
  - architecture requests, from the resident's pre-check to the decision letter;
  - dues, from generating the month to recording a payment;
  - asking your documents, with the cited section.
- **New menu.**
  - The Features menu groups everything by money, communication, rules and requests, and AI and law, with a short line under each item.
  - The header stays at the top as you scroll.
  - On phones, a full-screen menu has large rows and the trial button at the bottom.
- **New footer** in four tidy columns.
- **Homepage.**
  - A "Why boards switch" section shows five chores before and after.
  - Clearer "Up and running" steps.
  - A Georgia SB 406 panel.
- **Pricing.**
  - A plan comparison table you can scroll through.
  - The trial terms right under the prices.
  - A savings calculator that labels its estimates.
- **Georgia.** The Georgia page explains what changes on January 1, 2027 and what HomeownerHub does about it today.
- **Guides.**
  - Easier to read, with a table of contents, key points and styled law quotes.
  - The guide library can be filtered by state and topic.
- **Compare.** A new page compares HomeownerHub and PayHOA fairly, with prices as of Oct 31, 2026.
- **Security and Roadmap** now say only what is true today, including that two-factor sign-in is not available yet.
- **Book a demo** has a simpler form, with a free-trial option beside it.
