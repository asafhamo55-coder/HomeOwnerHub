## Summary

No change you'll see today. This release adds the rest of the CPA, attorney and treasurer tools, switched off until the database update for those roles is applied (OA-37). When they're switched on:

- the Georgia registration kit fills in each officer's title;
- the Treasurer sees CPA submissions in the daily digest and on the Approvals badge;
- the digest flags professional access that is about to end or has ended;
- a CPA can download the trial balance, general ledger and receivables aging as CSV, showing owner names only and only what their access allows.
