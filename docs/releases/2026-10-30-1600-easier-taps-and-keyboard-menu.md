title: Easier taps on tick boxes, a keyboard-friendly website menu
---

## Summary

On phones, tick boxes, radio buttons and switches in the app are easier to hit. Tapping anywhere on the row now works, not just the small box. On the website, the mobile menu now works properly with a keyboard and screen readers.

## What's new

### App
- Checkbox, radio and switch rows are 44 pixels tall on phones and tablets, and tapping anywhere on the row toggles them. Desktop looks the same as before.
- With "Reduce Motion" turned on in your phone or computer settings, loading spinners now turn slowly instead of freezing. Before, a frozen spinner made the app look stuck.

### Website
- Opening the mobile menu moves the keyboard into it. Tab stays inside the menu, and Escape closes it and returns you to the menu button.
- Screen readers now announce the footer's link columns as "Product links" and "Company links". The footer looks the same.

## Fixes

- A link inside a checkbox's description stays tappable on phones.

## Needs your action

- On your phone, tap the empty space next to a checkbox label in a bulk-select list. It should toggle.
- Open the website menu on your phone, tap a link, and check that the menu closes cleanly.
