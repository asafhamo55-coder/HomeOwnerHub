title: Easier sign-in, and a check before you send to everyone
---

## What's new

### Signing in
- **Expired links.** When an invite or sign-in link has expired or was already used, the page says so plainly. One button emails you a new link.
- **A different phone or computer.** Opening a link there now explains what happened and offers a new link, instead of a confusing error.
- **Choose a password.** If you joined from an email link, you are offered a password for next time. You can skip it and keep using email links.
- **Remembered sign-in method.** If you usually sign in by email link, the sign-in page now opens on that option.
- **Plain error messages.** They say what to try next.
- **Help.** Every sign-in screen shows support@homeownerledger.com.
- **Show password.** The reset password screen has a show-password button and asks for the password only once.
- **Larger text** on all sign-in screens.

### Fewer one-tap mistakes
- **Announcements.** Sending asks you to confirm first, with how many homes will get it and how (email or text).
- **Architecture requests.** Approving, denying or marking one in review asks you to confirm first, and says the homeowner will be told.

### Messages you can catch
- **Success messages** now stay for 8 seconds, and pause while your finger is on them.
- **Error messages** stay until you close them.

### Easier to find your way
- **Menu and Help.** The "Menu" and "Help" labels stay visible even with large text or zoom.
- **Home.** The logo at the top of the menu takes you back to the dashboard.

### Accessibility
- **Larger tap areas on phones** for:
  - checkboxes, including bulk approval;
  - the photo Remove button in the violation form;
  - the date and assignee fields in task lists.
- **Screen readers** now announce progress while a violation draft saves or a photo is analysed.
- **Errors after an action** (such as awarding a bid or revoking an invitation) are now read aloud.
- **The AI rewrite window** keeps keyboard focus inside while open and returns it when closed.
- **The inbox shortcut "c"** (close the conversation) now tells you if closing failed, and refreshes the list.
