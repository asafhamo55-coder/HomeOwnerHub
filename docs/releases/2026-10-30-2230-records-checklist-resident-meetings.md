title: A records checklist for owners, and easier resident and meeting screens
---

## Summary

The compliance page now shows which records your state expects owners to be able to see, and whether each one is in your document library. On the resident home, meetings and announcements are one tap away, and the requests page shows the latest activity. The board's meetings list shows what's coming up and which minutes still need approving.

## What's new

### Compliance
- A new card, "Records your owners can see". For Florida associations it lists the records named in Fla. Stat. § 720.303(4). Each one shows Posted (with a link), Missing (with a link to upload), or Kept off-app when the app has no place to store it.
- For Georgia, the card says plainly that the law gives owners a right to inspect records but sets no posting list. It shows which records Georgia law requires the association to keep.
- "Posted" only ever means a matching document is in your library. This is not legal advice.

### Resident portal
- The home page has Meetings and Announcements tiles. Announcements shows "N new" when there are new ones.
- The pay page explains that a payment shows on the ledger once the board records it.
- The requests page lists the five most recent requests (tickets, ARC requests and reports) with their status.

### Meetings
- The list shows the next meeting, how many meetings were held this year, and how many minutes are not approved yet.
- Filters: All, Upcoming, Past, and Needs minutes.

### Billing
- The Stripe product is now named HomeownerHub.

## Needs your action

- Open Compliance and check that the records card matches the documents you have uploaded.
