title: Two new setup steps, Text size on the sign-in screen, and clearer high-contrast mode
---

## Summary

Boards get two optional setup steps: inviting owners, and adding the community's city and ZIP. Anyone can make the text bigger before signing in. Focus outlines and button edges stay visible in Windows high-contrast mode. The help panel has new articles for Text size, the How to join notice and the welcome video. The website has three more Georgia guides.

## What's new

### Boards
- A new setup step, "Invite your owners", shows how many owners with an email have not been invited yet, and links to the printable How to join notice for owners without email. An invited owner still has to accept.
- A new setup step, "Add your community's city and ZIP", lets house photos show for homes whose address is only a street.
- Both steps are optional. You can finish and close the setup checklist without them.
- The Text size setting is explained in the help panel, along with the How to join notice and how to share the welcome video.

### Everyone
- The sign-in, join and first-time setup screens have a Text size choice above the form: Normal, Large or Largest.
- In Windows high-contrast mode, the box around the button or field you are on, and the edges of buttons and checkboxes, now stay visible.

### Website
- Three new Georgia guides: handing over when board members change, board meeting quorum and minutes, and what the secretary does.

## Fixes

- Two screens that looked like tabs (the quorum check-in view and the governing documents uploader) are now plain buttons, which screen readers describe correctly.
- Photo attachments are described as "Attachment 1", "Attachment 2" for screen readers, instead of a file name.

## Under the hood

- The HOA app is now checked by ESLint on every change, including accessibility rules.
