---
title: Bigger text when you want it, a cleaner logo in dark mode, and a nudge for open polls
---

## Summary

Anyone can now make the text in HomeownerHub larger. The email logo looks right in dark mode. Residents see when a poll is waiting for their answer.

## What's new

### Everyone
- A "Text size" setting with Normal, Large and Largest. Residents find it under Account, boards in the account menu. The choice is remembered on that device.

### Residents
- The Community tile shows how many polls are waiting for an answer, for example "2 to answer".
- When a poll closes within three days, the home screen shows "A poll closes on <day>" with the question, so it is not missed.

### Emails
- The HomeownerHub logo in emails is now a purple badge that looks clean in light and dark mode. Before, dark mode showed it on a white box.

## Fixes

- The notification list fits on very narrow phones.
