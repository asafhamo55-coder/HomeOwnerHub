## Summary

The "Law updates" feed on the State Law page is shared by every association. To keep it trustworthy, only the HomeownerHub team can now post, edit or archive updates. Boards still see every update. Nothing else changes for your board today. This release also contains the next two database updates (tenant isolation and CPA/attorney/treasurer roles), ready for the owner to apply. The new roles stay switched off until then.

## What's new

### State Law → Law updates
- Boards see the feed read-only. The "Post update" button is gone for boards, and the HomeownerHub team maintains the feed.

## Under the hood

- Database updates 0070 (security hardening between associations) and 0071 (CPA, attorney and treasurer roles) are included and fully tested. They take effect only when applied (OA-37).
