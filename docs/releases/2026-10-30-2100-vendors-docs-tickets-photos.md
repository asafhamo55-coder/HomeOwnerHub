## Summary

Boards can see at a glance which vendors have expired or missing insurance. They can search the document library, and flip through violation photos without leaving the page. Tickets, violations and ARC requests show urgent counts at the top. On the website, Florida, California and Texas each have their own page, the homepage has an FAQ, and there are three more state guides.

## What's new

### Vendors
- New counts at the top: Expired, Expiring within 30 days, Missing insurance, and All clear. Each one filters the list.
- You can sort by insurance expiry date. Nothing is emailed to vendors.

### Documents
- A search box and type filters (CC&Rs, Bylaws, Rules, Minutes, Other), with a count for each.

### Violations and ARC
- Violations show Open, In cure period, and Cure date passed at the top.
- ARC shows Waiting, Oldest wait, and Past decide-by date.
- Violation photos open in a viewer with Previous and Next buttons and arrow keys. Residents get the same viewer on their violation page.

### Tickets
- New counts at the top: Needs attention, Oldest open, and No one on it (open tickets with no follow-up task assigned).
- A search box that looks in the subject, the resident and the unit.
- An Age column.

### Accounting
- The overview has a Budget vs actual card that shows the three lines most over budget, with a link to the full report.

### Resident portal
- The ARC request form lists what to include for each kind of project, such as a survey for a fence or a color chip for paint.

### Website
- New state pages: /states/florida, /states/california and /states/texas. Each lists what the workspace does in that state and links to that state's guides.
- The homepage has an FAQ covering price, states, AI approval, data export, privacy, cancelling, and whether you need a manager.
- New guides:
  - /guides/florida-hoa-official-records-inspection
  - /guides/california-hoa-records-inspection-rules
  - /guides/texas-hoa-management-certificate

## Needs your action

- Tickets have no "assigned to" field today. Tell Claude if you want one (it needs a small database update).
- On /vendors, check the insurance counts match what you know about your vendors.
