## Summary

Boards can now approve minutes on a meeting that was scheduled first. Before this fix, those minutes could never be approved, so residents never saw them. Each home shows its full letter history, and a board member can email an owner their dues statement in a couple of clicks. Vendor pages and the accounting reports and bank pages show the key numbers at the top. Three more state guides are live.

## What's new

### Meetings
- "Approve minutes" appears on a past meeting once minutes are saved. It asks you to confirm first. Residents can read the minutes as soon as you approve them.

### Properties
- The Compliance tab lists every violation letter sent for the home, newest first, with links to each letter.
- On a home's statement, "Email to owner" sends the statement to each current owner with an email address on file. Before you confirm, you see their addresses partly hidden. Owners who turned off association email are skipped. The same statement can't be sent twice within 24 hours. Every send is recorded in the activity log, and nothing changes in the books.

### Vendors
- The top of each vendor page shows insurance status, days until the certificate expires, open bids and RFPs, and the last activity.

### Accounting
- The reports page shows income, expenses and net for the year so far, plus cash on hand and whether the books are open, with each figure linking to its report.
- The bank page shows total cash, the accounts that need reconciling, and unmatched transactions. Each account shows when it was last reconciled, with a "Reconcile now" link.

### Website
