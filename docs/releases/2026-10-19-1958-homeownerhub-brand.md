## Summary

The product is now called **HomeownerHub** everywhere: in the app, on the website and in these emails. It has a new logo, an H whose crossbar is a roof, and the whole platform has moved to a fresh blue and light-blue palette.

## What's new

- **New logo:** the H-roof mark, with the "HomeownerHub" wordmark where "Hub" is in blue. You'll see it in the sidebar, the sign-in screens, the browser tab icon, the home-screen icon on phones, and the website.
- **New colours:** a confident blue for buttons and links, light-blue tints for highlighted areas, and cool grey neutrals. Every text and button colour pair was checked to meet accessibility contrast standards (WCAG AA), in light and dark mode.
- **One name:** "HOA Hub" in the app and "Ledger" on the website both now read **HomeownerHub**. Accounting terms such as the general ledger keep their names, and the web address stays homeownerledger.com.

## Good to know

- Stripe checkout still shows the old product name until it's renamed in Stripe.
