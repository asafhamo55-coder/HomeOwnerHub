## Summary

The website's page for CPAs and attorneys (homeownerledger.online/professionals) said professional access was "coming soon". It went live in the app on October 27, and the page now describes how it works.

## What's new

- **/professionals** explains how access works:
  - The board invites a CPA or attorney with a signed, single-use link that expires in 7 days.
  - Access ends on a date the board sets, at most a year away.
  - A CPA sees the books read-only, with owner names only.
  - An attorney sees only the matters the board assigns.
- **Officer controls:** officer titles, Treasurer routing, and an optional two-board-member amount for money approvals.
- **Other pages:** the HOA page's "Also included" list and the Georgia page no longer call professional access "coming soon".

## Under the hood

- A website test now fails if the professionals page says "coming soon", "planned" or "rolling out" again.
