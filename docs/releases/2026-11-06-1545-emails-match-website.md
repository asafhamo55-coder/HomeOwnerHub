## Summary

Every email HomeownerHub sends now looks like the website: the HomeownerHub logo at the top, royal purple buttons and links, and the same dark text. This covers emails to residents (welcome, receipts, statements, dues reminders, polls, announcements) and emails to boards (daily summary, monthly report, vendor and bid requests).

## What's better

### Emails
- A HomeownerHub logo header on every email, on a white band with a purple stripe so it reads well in light and dark mode.
- Royal purple buttons (large and easy to tap) and links, matching the website.
- Poll results show purple bars.
- If images are turned off, the email still shows "HomeownerHub" in text.

### Website
- The website's logo files now use the same royal purple as the rest of the site.

## Under the hood

- One shared colour palette for all emails, checked by a test so old colours can't creep back in.
