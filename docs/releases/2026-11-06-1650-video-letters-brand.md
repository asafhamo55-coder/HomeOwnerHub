## Summary

The resident getting-started video and its cover pictures now use the website's royal purple and the HomeownerHub logo. Printed letters (violation and collection letters, the how-to-join notice, the budget notice and statements) carry the HomeownerHub logo at the top.

## What's new

### Residents
- The getting-started video at /welcome now opens and closes with the HomeownerHub logo, with large, easy-to-read captions.

### Boards
- Printed letters and notices show the HomeownerHub logo next to your association's name. The letter text stays black on white so it prints clearly.

### Website
- A public "Text message alerts" page explains how residents sign up for texts and how to stop them, as required to approve texting.

## Under the hood

- Every text message will end with "Reply STOP to opt out." Texting is still switched off until the texting account is approved.
