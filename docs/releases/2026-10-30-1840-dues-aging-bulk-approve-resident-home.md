## Summary

Treasurers can now see who owes by how late they are and remind one group in a couple of clicks. Board members can approve several violation letters at once. Residents see the newest announcement on their home page and can add meetings to their calendar. On the website, the product tour now appears on the HOA and pricing pages, and four new Georgia guides were added.

## What's new

### Dues
- The Who owes list has filter chips: Current, 1 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90 and Over 90 days. Each chip shows how many owners are in that group and what they owe in total.
- With a group selected, "Remind this group" opens the usual reminders window with those owners already ticked. Reminders follow the same rules as before.

### Approvals
- Violation letter drafts and contact updates can be ticked and approved together. A bar at the bottom shows "Approve N", and the app asks you to confirm before approving. If any item can't be approved, it stays ticked and the message says why.
- Payments, bills, collections letters, ARC decisions, join requests and vendor approvals still go one at a time, on purpose.

### Announcements
- The review step shows the email and the text message side by side, and says how many texts the message becomes.

### Resident portal
- The home page shows the latest announcement, with its title, date and first line. Spanish readers see the Spanish version.
- Upcoming meetings have an "Add to calendar" button.

### Website
- The product tour now appears on the HOA page. The pricing page shows three sample screens under the plans.
- Four new Georgia pages:
  - /guides/georgia-hoa-written-ballots-without-a-meeting
  - /guides/georgia-hoa-amend-covenants-two-thirds
  - /guides/georgia-hoa-assessment-lien-rules
  - /templates/hoa-board-conflict-of-interest-disclosure-checklist

## Under the hood

- Large lists load with one less database round trip.

## Needs your action

- On /dues, click a few aging chips and check that the groups match what you expect.
- On /approvals, try ticking two violation letters and approving them together.
