## Summary

Replying to residents takes far fewer clicks. The inbox drafts a reply as soon as you open an email and shows everything about the sender's home next to it. Scheduled messages now actually send, every association gets ready-made templates, and messages respect residents' opt-outs.

## What's new

### Inbox
- **Draft on open:** open a resident's email and a reply starts drafting straight away. The draft cites your documents, and when the sender is a resident of that home, it can use the home's balance and the status of their requests.
- **Context panel:** the side panel shows who wrote, the home's balance and last payment, open violations, ARC applications, tickets and earlier conversations, each one a link.
- **Approve and send:** this sends the reply, closes the conversation and opens the next one waiting for a reply. You can still undo for a few seconds.
- **Close, Waiting and Reopen:** these options keep the list tidy. A closed conversation reopens by itself when the resident writes back.
- **Waiting badges:** a badge shows how long each resident has waited (amber after 2 days, red after 5).
- **Assignment:** you can assign a conversation to a board member, and filter by **Mine** or **Unassigned**.
- **Morning email reminder:** the board's morning email says how many residents have waited more than 2 days.
- **Keyboard shortcuts:** j and k to move, a to approve, e to edit, c to close, ? for help.

### Communications
- **Scheduled messages now send** at the time you pick, in your own time zone. You can cancel one before it goes out. Before this update they were saved but never sent.
- **Built-in templates for every association:** dues reminder, meeting notice, water shutoff, pool closing, holiday office closure, welcome new owner and severe weather.
- **Opt-outs are respected:** residents who turned off email don't get emails, and texts go only to residents who opted in. The review step shows how many will be skipped.
- **Text messages report problems honestly:** when texting isn't set up, the app says so instead of showing "sent".

## Fixes

- **Payments:** if a subscription payment fails to save, the app now tells Stripe so it retries, instead of silently ignoring it.
