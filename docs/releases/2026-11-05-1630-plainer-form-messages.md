---
title: Plainer messages when a form needs fixing
---

## Summary

When something typed into a form isn't right, the app now says so in plain words, such as "Required" or "Must be at most 500 characters." This replaces technical wording that crept in with a software update. Print-and-mail letters are also switched on, in the safe print-it-yourself mode.

## What's better

### Forms
- Error messages read plainly: "Required", "Enter a number.", "Enter a valid email address.", "Choose at least 1 item." Vendors filling in a bid or an onboarding form see the same plain wording.

### Letters
- Collection and violation letters are ready for mail-by-the-app once a mailing service is connected. Until then, nothing changes for boards: you print the letter and mail it yourself, and there is no button that would fail.

## Under the hood

- The form-checking and testing libraries were moved to their current major versions, from the November 2 security audit.
- Two unused libraries were removed.
