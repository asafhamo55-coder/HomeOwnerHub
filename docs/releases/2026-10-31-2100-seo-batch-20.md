## Summary

The Documents page now opens with four count tiles, so a board can see at a glance how many documents are in the library and how many have no searchable text. The website has two more Texas pages for boards: a guide to the violation notice in Texas Property Code § 209.006 and a printable checklist. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### Documents
- A summary strip above the list shows Total documents, Not searchable, CC&Rs and Rules. The counts cover the whole library, not just the current search.
- Each tile opens the matching list. "Not searchable" opens the documents that have no text yet, so you can open each one and paste its text.

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/texas-hoa-violation-notice-and-hearing-209-006`: what § 209.006 says a Texas association must do before a fine, a suspension of common area use or a charge for damage. Certified mail, what the notice must say, the cure date, curable and uncurable violations, the right to ask for a hearing within 30 days of mailing, the military service line and the rule that a violation cured in time cannot be fined. It says the text we hold does not include § 209.007, so it does not describe the hearing, and it points out that the statute names both certified mail and verified mail without choosing between them. It says Texas violation notices are not built into HomeownerHub.
- `/templates/texas-hoa-violation-notice-checklist`: a printable checklist and fill-in worksheet for one notice, each item tagged statute or practice, with the Texas text behind each step.

The new pages are listed on the Guides and Templates pages, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K), in the sitemap and on the Texas state page. The Texas flag display, third-party collections and payment plan pages link to the guide.

## Under the hood

- Every law quote is checked word for word against the official text, a new Texas excerpt of chapter 209.
- The Documents strip uses the rows the page already loads and adds no queries.
