## Summary

The website has its first guides outside Georgia: two for Florida, one for Texas and one for California. Each describes only features that exist today, quotes the law only from the checked official text, carries a not-legal-advice note, and ends with "Book a demo".

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/florida-hoa-fine-hearing-committee`: Fla. Stat. § 720.305(2). The 14-day hearing notice, the committee of at least three members who are not officers, directors or employees, the 90-day window, the majority vote, the 7-day findings and the payment date at least 30 days out.
- `/guides/florida-hoa-estoppel-certificate-rules`: Fla. Stat. § 720.30851. The 10-business-day deadline, delivery, the 30 and 35 day validity, the fee limits and who may charge them, and the consequences of being late or wrong.
- `/guides/texas-hoa-payment-plans-and-payment-priority`: Tex. Prop. Code §§ 209.0062 and 209.0063. The duty to adopt payment-plan guidelines, the three-month minimum, the cases where a plan is not required, filing in the county records, and the order a payment is applied.
- `/guides/california-hoa-fine-hearing-and-penalty-rules`: Cal. Civ. Code §§ 5850, 5855 and 5865. The $100 cap and its health and safety exception, no late charge on a fine, the 10-day notice, the right to cure, and the 14-day written decision.

The State laws page now links each state's guide, and the new pages are in the sitemap.

## Under the hood

- Every new law quote is checked word for word against the official text. The check now also reads the Florida Senate's HTML chapter.
- Titles and descriptions pass the weekly SEO audit.
