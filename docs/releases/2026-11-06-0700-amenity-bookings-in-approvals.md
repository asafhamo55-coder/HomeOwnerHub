---
title: Amenity booking requests now wait in Things to approve
---

## Summary

When a resident asks to book the clubhouse, pool or another space that needs the board's OK, the request now shows up in Things to approve with everything else the board decides. A board member can approve it or turn it down right there.

## What's new

### Things to approve
- A new Amenity bookings tab lists every booking request still waiting, oldest first. Each shows the space, the day and time, which home asked, how long and how many people.
- Approve or Turn down from the list. Turning down asks for a short reason, which the resident reads in their email.
- Approve several at once with the bulk button. If two requests are for the same time, the first is approved and the second is refused with a plain reason.
- The number of waiting bookings is included in the sidebar count.
- The bell notice for a new booking request now opens Things to approve.

## Fixes

- A few dashes in the Things to approve page text were replaced with plain punctuation.
