## Summary

The website has three new Florida guides. One covers what an HOA's architectural review can and cannot restrict. One covers the mediation Florida requires before many HOA disputes reach court. One covers flags, flagpoles, access ramps and security signs. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/florida-hoa-architectural-review-720-3035`: the committee's authority comes from the declaration or published guidelines, standards apply equally, the limits on interiors, hidden air-conditioning systems and building permits, the written denial notice, and hurricane protection.
- `/guides/florida-hoa-presuit-mediation-720-311`: which disputes need mediation first, which do not, how the demand is served, the 20 and 90 day clocks, and what refusing costs.
- `/guides/florida-hoa-flags-ramps-and-signs-720-304`: the flags an owner may display, the flagpole, access ramps and security signs, and what the board can still require.

All three are listed on the Guides page, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K), in the sitemap and on the Florida page.
