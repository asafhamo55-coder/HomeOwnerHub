## Summary

When someone joins or leaves a Georgia association's board in HomeownerHub, the app now starts the 30-day clock to file an amended registration with the Secretary of State. It appears on Compliance and in the registration kit. The quorum kit also quotes Georgia's nonprofit proxy rule word for word.

## What's new

### Georgia registration
- A board change (resident to board or admin, or back) adds a "File an amended registration" item due 30 days later. Mark it done with the kit's existing buttons ("Record the amended filing" or "Officers didn't change").
- Before January 1, 2027 the item says to list the new officers in your first filing.

### Meetings
- The quorum kit quotes O.C.G.A. § 14-3-724 for associations incorporated as Georgia nonprofit corporations. The rule says a proxy is valid for 11 months, can be appointed electronically, and how it can be revoked. Unincorporated associations should check their bylaws.
