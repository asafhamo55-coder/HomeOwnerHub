title: Plainer website wording, bigger tap targets on phones
---

## Summary

The website's text was rewritten so it sounds like a person wrote it. The stock phrases and the long dashes that made it read as machine-written are gone. In the app, buttons and menus are easier to tap on phones and tablets, and screen readers now hear form errors as they appear.

## What's new

### Website
- Clearer wording on the demo page, pricing, security, honesty bars, the hub pages and the blog list. Prices, quality bars and Georgia facts are unchanged.
- The description shown in Google and link previews now says what the product does: "AI tools for self-managed HOA boards: letters, notices, minutes and covenant answers drafted from your own documents. The board approves everything."
- The demo form keeps what you typed when something is wrong and jumps straight to the field that needs fixing.
- Keyboard users can skip past the menu straight to the page.

### App
- On phones and tablets, buttons are 44 pixels tall, a comfortable size for a thumb. Desktop stays compact.
- The mobile menu, search and close buttons are larger, and the close button shows keyboard focus.
- Toast messages have larger Dismiss and Undo buttons.

## Fixes

- Some grey text in the HOA switcher and the notification bell was showing in the wrong colour. It now matches the rest of the app.
- Very small 10 to 11 pixel labels in those menus are now 12 pixels.

## Under the hood

- Screen readers now announce form errors, the current HOA in the switcher, and the attachment paperclip in the inbox.

## Needs your action

- On your phone, check that the larger buttons don't crowd busy rows, such as the inbox thread header and table actions.
- Read the homepage, pricing and demo pages once to confirm the new wording sounds like you.
