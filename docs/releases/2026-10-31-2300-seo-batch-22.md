## Summary

The website has two new Georgia guides. One answers whether Georgia's Property Owners' Association Act applies to your HOA. The other covers who can serve on the board when the documents say directors must be lot owners. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly that HomeownerHub does not decide these questions for a board.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-does-poa-act-apply-44-3-222`: the Act applies only if the declaration states an affirmative election (§ 44-3-222), how an older declaration opts in by amendment (§ 44-3-235), the condominium exclusion in § 44-3-235(b), and what § 44-3-234 says. It quotes the text in force now, and gives the text SB 406 puts in force on January 1, 2027 one sentence. It does not describe Code Section 44-5-60, which we do not hold.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-who-can-serve-on-the-board-44-3-229`: what § 44-3-229 says about who counts as a lot owner when the instrument requires a director or officer to be one, and what happens when the person leaves the owning entity.

Both pages are listed on the Guides page, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K), in the sitemap and on the Georgia page. The Georgia condo versus HOA guide links to the first.
