## Summary

The website has two more state guides and one more free template, all on collections notices. The guides explain what Texas and California require before an owner owes a collection agent's fees or a lien is recorded. The template lists the fields for a Florida 45-day notice of intent to record a claim of lien. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/texas-hoa-third-party-collections-notice`: Tex. Prop. Code § 209.0064. The certified-mail notice, the three things it must say, the 45-day cure period, how it meets the payment plan rules, and the fee rules on collection agent agreements.
- `/guides/california-hoa-pre-lien-notice-and-collections`: Civil Code §§ 5650 to 5665. When an assessment is delinquent, the late charge and interest caps, the 30-day certified-mail notice before a lien, what the notice must say, payment plans, and how payments are applied.
- `/templates/florida-hoa-notice-of-intent-to-lien`: a checklist and a fill-in notice from Fla. Stat. § 720.3085(4), with the 45-day period, the mailing rules and the notice before foreclosure.

Each page says HomeownerHub drafts the collections letter for the board to approve and print, and that it does not send certified mail or track the mailing for these states. The certified-mail record is for Georgia letters only.

The new pages are listed on the Guides and Templates pages, on the Florida, California and Texas pages, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K) and in the sitemap. They link to the existing payment plan, estoppel, resale certificate and dispute resolution pages.
