## Summary

The website has three new Georgia guides for board members. One covers what the Georgia Property Owners' Association Act says about reserves, and what it does not say about reserve studies. One covers the annual meeting: notice, delivery, reports and quorum. One covers the annual registration that a nonprofit HOA files with the Secretary of State. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-reserve-fund-and-reserve-study`: what the Act says about reserves, the 2027 definitions of regular and special assessments, and why a board might get a study anyway.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-annual-meeting-notice-and-reports-44-3-230`: 21 days of notice, how notice is delivered, the reports owners hear and the quorum default.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-annual-registration-secretary-of-state-14-3-1622`: what the yearly registration lists, when it is due and what a missed one can lead to.

All three are listed on the Guides page, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K), in the sitemap and on the Georgia page.
