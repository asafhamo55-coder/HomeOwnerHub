## Website

- **New homepage.** It opens with what HomeownerHub does: your board's letters, notices and answers are drafted from your own documents, and you approve each one before it goes out.
  - **"Start free trial"** now goes straight to sign-up. Before, it went to the demo page.
  - **The free trial in short:** next to the button, a short list says the trial is 30 days, needs no credit card, lets you import your owner list from a spreadsheet, and can be cancelled anytime.
- **Feature tour.** You can switch between dues and accounting, violations, announcements, documents and answers, and architecture requests. Each lists what you can do today.
- **Getting started.** A new "Up and running in days" section shows the three steps to start.
- **Price.** A slider shows the real monthly or yearly price for the number of homes in your community.
- **Easier to read.** The text is larger across the website.
- **Features menu.** It groups pages by money, communication, rules and requests, and AI and law.
