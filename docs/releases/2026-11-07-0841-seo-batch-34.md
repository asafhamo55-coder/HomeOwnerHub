## Summary

The website has three new printable templates for Georgia boards. One is a fine hearing notice, and it says plainly that the Georgia text we hold sets no hearing step, so the notice follows your own declaration and bylaws. One is a checklist and reply for a nonprofit association that gets a written request to inspect records. One is a checklist for answering an estoppel or payoff request during a sale. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page says to check your declaration and bylaws and ask your attorney, and each ends with "Book a demo".

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/templates/georgia-hoa-fine-hearing-notice`: what to write in a notice of hearing, with no statute claim about the hearing itself.
- `/templates/georgia-hoa-records-inspection-response-checklist`: the five business days, the two groups of records and the conditions, for an association that is a nonprofit corporation.
- `/templates/georgia-hoa-estoppel-statement-request-checklist`: who may ask, the five business days, the figures and the $10 fee limit.

All three are listed on the Templates page, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K) and in the sitemap. The Georgia fines and violation hearings, records inspection, owner compliance and estoppel guides now link to them.
