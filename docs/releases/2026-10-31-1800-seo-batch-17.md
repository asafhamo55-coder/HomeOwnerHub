## Summary

The website has two more Florida pages for boards. A guide covers the notice rules for board meetings: the 48-hour posting, agenda items in the notice, owners speaking and the vote rules for directors. A printable checklist covers each director's education certificate. In the resident portal, each insurance policy now shows how long it is in force. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### Resident portal
- On the Insurance page, each policy shows "In force through" with its end date, next to the limit and deductible. If a policy has no end date recorded, the line is left out.

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/florida-hoa-board-meeting-notice-rules`: what Fla. Stat. § 720.303(2) says about board meetings. It points to the meeting scheduler and its one-click standard agenda.
- `/templates/florida-hoa-director-certification-checklist`: a checklist and a fill-in reminder for the education certificate under Fla. Stat. § 720.3033: the 90 days, the 4-year renewal and keeping each certificate for 5 years. It links to the meeting guide and to the Florida official records guide.

The pages say HomeownerHub keeps the meeting agenda and tracks each Florida director's 90-day certificate deadline and 4-year renewal. It does not post notices, count the 48 hours, or track continuing-education hours.

The new pages are listed on the Guides and Templates pages, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K), in the sitemap and on the Florida state page.

## Under the hood

- Every new law quote is checked word for word against the official text.
- The Florida official records guide now links to the new checklist.
