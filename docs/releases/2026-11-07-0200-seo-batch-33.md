## Summary

The website has three new Georgia guides for volunteer board members. One is a checklist for when board members change: governing documents, minutes, financial records, the member list and the officer names on the state registration. One covers the board meeting quorum under 44-3-228 and the minutes the association must keep, and says plainly what the bylaws decide. One explains what a Georgia HOA secretary usually handles and which parts come from the statute. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-board-transition-checklist`: what changes hands when volunteers change, and which items are statute and which are practice.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-board-meeting-quorum-and-minutes-44-3-228`: the default board quorum, the duty to keep detailed minutes, and what the text we hold leaves to the bylaws.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-secretary-duties-notices-minutes-and-records`: owner notices, minutes, the member list, calls from owners and the state registration.

All three are listed on the Guides page, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K), in the sitemap and on the Georgia page.
