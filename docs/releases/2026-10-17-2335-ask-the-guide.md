---

## Summary

You can now ask the user guide a question in plain words, such as "how do I record a check payment?". The answer comes from the guide's own articles, with the steps and a button to the right screen. The guide won't make things up: if a question is about something HomeownerHub doesn't do, it says so. The setup checklist and empty pages now have "How?" links to the right article. This release also fixes several problems we found while writing the guide.

## What's new

### Ask the guide
- Ask from the Help centre, or from "Ask about this page" in the **?** panel.
- Answers show numbered steps, the buttons to look for, the articles they came from, and **Take me there**.
- Questions about your HOA's own rules are sent to **Ask the Docs**, which reads your governing documents.
- If the AI is busy, you get the matching articles instead.
- Your question is never stored or logged.

### Help where you need it
- Each setup checklist step has a **How?** link that opens the article for it.
- Empty pages (no violations yet, no meetings yet, and so on) have a **How this works** link.
- ⌘K finds help articles. If nothing matches, it offers to ask the guide.
- The version label in the sidebar shows a dot when there's something new on the What's new page.

## Fixes

- **Long text messages** no longer cut off the link to the full message. The text is shortened instead, so the link always arrives whole.
- **When the books aren't set up yet**, Generate dues, Add a due, Mark paid and paying a bill now show an **Open the books** button (or say who can do it). Before, they showed a technical message.
- **Ask the Docs** shows the document sections it used again.
- **The text-message hint** now says exactly what happens to long messages.
- **The invite form** shows the right description for each role.
- **The setup step for properties** no longer links to a page boards couldn't open.

## Needs your action

- Confirm that support@homeownerledger.com is a real inbox. Boards are now told to email it to have a spreadsheet imported.
