## Summary

The website has three new Georgia guides for board members. One covers architectural review under the covenants and what Georgia law leaves to the declaration. One covers short-term and Airbnb rentals, where the statute is silent and the declaration decides. One is a board election and nominations checklist, from notice and quorum to proxies, ballots and the state registration. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-architectural-review-covenants-44-3-231`: the association's power to approve or withhold exterior changes, what the declaration usually controls and where the statute is silent.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-short-term-rentals-and-airbnb-44-3-226`: what the Act does and does not say about short stays, the six-month sentence in 44-3-226 and how a rental rule is enforced or added.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-board-election-and-nominations-checklist`: annual meeting, 21 days' notice, quorum, joint owners, proxies, ballots, who may serve and what the bylaws control, such as nominations.

All three are listed on the Guides page, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K), in the sitemap and on the Georgia page.
