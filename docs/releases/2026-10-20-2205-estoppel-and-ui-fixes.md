## Summary

Estoppel certificates for Florida and California homes now follow the statute: they list only violations that were formally noticed to the owner. This update also fixes a mismatched label on the billing page, clarifies message audiences, and makes filters easier to use on phones.

## What's new

### Estoppel certificates
- **Florida and California:** the certificate's violation question asks only about violations noticed to the owner (Fla. Stat. § 720.30851; Cal. Civ. Code § 4525(a)(5)). It now lists only those.
- **Open violations without a recorded notice** appear on screen as "board to verify" and are never printed. If you sent a notice outside the app, record it on the violation first.
- **Texas and Georgia:** these work as before. Texas lists every violation the board knows about.

### Billing
- **Plan labels:** the plan label and "Current plan" now always agree.
- **Unavailable billing:** if billing is temporarily unavailable, you see a plain message instead of technical setup details.

### Messages
- **Clearer audience descriptions:** "Homes with an open violation" now explains who gets the message.
- **One home:** it shows a count only after you pick the home.

### Phones and inbox
- **Filters:** they wrap onto a second row instead of being cut off.
- **Meeting page:** the buttons are tidier.
- **Agenda:** the spacing between items is even.
- **Reply wait:** the inbox chip now says "Unanswered 2d" instead of "Waiting 2d", so it isn't confused with the Waiting filter.

## Good to know
- If you prepare Florida or California estoppel certificates, check that every violation you noticed has its notice date recorded.
