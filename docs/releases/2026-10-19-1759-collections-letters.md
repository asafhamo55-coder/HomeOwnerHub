## Summary

When an owner falls behind, the board can now draft the right collections letter from the property's page. It's built from the home's account and follows Florida, California or Texas law. Every letter is approved in Approvals and then printed; nothing is emailed.

## What's new

- On a property's **Collections** section, select **Draft collections letter**.
- **Florida:** the *Notice of Late Assessment* (§ 720.3085(3)(d)) and the 45-day *Notice of Intent to Record a Lien* (§ 720.3085(4)), using the statute's own wording.
  - The intent-to-lien letter can be drafted only once a late notice was recorded at least 30 days earlier.
- **California:** the pre-lien notice (§ 5660), including the required "IMPORTANT NOTICE" text. It can be drafted only once the assessment is delinquent, which is 15 days after it was due.
- **Texas:** the delinquency notice (§ 209.0064), with at least 45 days to cure and payment-plan options.
- **Other states:** a plain past-due letter.
- When a letter isn't allowed yet, the app tells you why and the first day you can draft it.
- Missing facts show as blanks, and you tick **I've verified these figures** before saving.
- Letters wait in **Approvals → Collections letters**. Once approved, you print them. Printing records the step on the collections case.
