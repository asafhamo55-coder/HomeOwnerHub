title: Payment plans that close themselves, editable recurring entries, and vendor insurance reminders
---

## Summary

Payment plans now close on their own once every installment is paid, and that home goes back to normal dues reminders. The board can also see which plans have fallen behind. Recurring journal entries can be edited, paused, resumed or stopped. Vendors get reminder emails before their certificate of insurance expires, and the board can set its own insurance minimums.

## What's new

### Payment plans
- A plan moves to **Completed** automatically when every installment is paid, waived or written off. Reminders then resume for that home.
- **Accounting → Payment plans** has a Progress column, for example "Behind on plan · 2 installments overdue · 3 of 6 paid", "On track · next due Nov 1" or "Completed Oct 18". A banner shows how many plans are behind.
- The property page's payment plan tile shows the same status.

### Recurring entries
- On **Accounting → Recurring**, each entry can be **edited** (description, frequency, next run, lines), **paused**, **resumed** or **stopped**.
- Lines must balance to the cent, accounts must be active, and the next run can't be in the past.
- Entries already posted to the ledger never change.

### Vendor insurance
- **Settings → Vendors:** set your minimum general liability (per occurrence and aggregate), auto liability, and whether workers' comp is required. Compliance checks and RFP invitations use these.
- **Reminders:** vendors with an expiring certificate get an email 30 days before, 7 days before and on the expiry day. Each board member gets an in-app note at each step. You can turn the emails off in the same settings screen.

## Fixes

- The AI that writes RFPs no longer receives unrelated association settings, only your insurance minimums.
