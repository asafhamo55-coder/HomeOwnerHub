## Summary

Boards can now print or download a year-end 1099 summary for each vendor, to hand to their CPA. This release also includes the groundwork for online payments, voting, polls, amenity booking, a community page, a message board and mailed letters. All of those are switched off and invisible until they are ready.

## What's new

### Year-end accounting
- A new 1099 vendor summary, under Accounting, then Year-end reports. It shows one sheet per vendor paid $600 or more, with the total paid, card payments taken out, and whether the vendor reaches the IRS filing minimum for that year.
- The minimum follows the year of payment: $600 for payments through 2025, and $2,000 from 2026.
- Tax ID numbers show only their last 4 digits.
- The page says plainly that the board or its CPA must check every figure and decide who gets a form. HomeownerHub does not file anything with the IRS.

## Under the hood

- Online payments, autopay, online voting, polls, amenity booking, a public community page, a message board and print-and-mail letters are built but switched off. They need database changes and, for payments and voting, a legal review before they can go live.
