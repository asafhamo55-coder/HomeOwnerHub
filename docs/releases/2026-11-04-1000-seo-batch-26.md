## Summary

The website has three new Georgia guides. One explains who casts the vote when a lot has more than one owner. One covers what owners can do when the board never holds the annual meeting. One covers an owner's duty to follow the rules and the remedies and limits the Georgia Property Owners' Association Act sets. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-joint-owners-voting-44-3-224`: one owner present, several owners present, and consent by silence.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-owners-call-annual-meeting-44-3-230`: when owners holding at least 5 percent of the votes, or a different amount the articles or bylaws set, up to 25 percent, can call the meeting, and what the call must contain.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-owner-compliance-and-remedies-44-3-223`: the duty to comply, the remedies, notice before an injunction, and the limits on fines and suspensions.

All three are listed on the Guides page, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K), in the sitemap and on the Georgia page.
