## Summary

Two small fixes to the step-by-step booking calendar that went live this morning.

## Fixes

- If a later month can't load, the calendar now stays where it was and shows "We couldn't load that month." with a Try again button, instead of showing every day crossed out.
- After cancelling a booking, the screen moves you back to "Your bookings" so you can see it is gone.
