## What's new

### Try it with sample data
- **A sample community for new boards.** A board with no homes yet can fill its account with a sample community: homes, dues, violations, tickets, architecture requests and a draft announcement. It lets you explore every screen on day one.
- **Nothing is sent.** Sample data never sends email, texts or alerts.
- **Your books are untouched.** No accounting entries are created.
- **Easy to remove.** One click removes the sample data, and anything you have changed stays.
- **Where to find it.** It's offered only until you add your own homes.

### Dashboard
- **"Do this next."** A new card at the top shows the one most important thing to do, with one button. Examples: a legal deadline that has passed, requests waiting for approval, emails from residents, violations past their fix-by date, or homes behind on dues.
- **"You're all caught up"** shows when nothing is waiting. If something couldn't be checked, the card says so.
- **Charts and community numbers** now sit under "Show more numbers". They start folded for new associations, and the app remembers your choice.
- **One welcome card for invited board members,** with the first three things to do. The setup checklist stays with the admin who set up the association.

### Phones
- **Fewer buttons in the top bar.** Admin-only tools moved out of the phone top bar, so "Menu" and "Help" always fit.
