title: Owner statements for the board, more exports, and keyboard shortcuts
---

## Summary

Boards can open and print any owner's dues statement from the property page, with the same numbers the owner sees. Tickets, ARC requests and insurance policies can now be exported as spreadsheets. The insurance page shows counts at the top, and pressing ? anywhere in the board app lists the keyboard shortcuts. The website gains three state guides and a search box on its guides and templates pages.

## What's new

### Properties
- A "Statement" button on each property opens a printable dues statement for a date range. It uses the same layout and math as the owner's own statement. Nothing is sent.

### Exports
- "Export CSV" on Tickets, ARC requests and Insurance. Each export keeps the filters you picked. Ticket exports include the resident's name but no email address.

### Insurance
- Counts at the top: Active, Expiring within 90 days, Expired, and Missing required coverage. Each count filters the list.

### Keyboard shortcuts
- Press ? anywhere in the board app to see the shortcuts. New ones: g then d goes to the dashboard, g then i to the inbox, and g then a to approvals. "Keyboard shortcuts" is also in the user menu.

### Website
- Search boxes on /guides and /templates. Every guide and template now appears in the Cmd-K search.
- New pages:
  - /templates/texas-hoa-payment-plan-agreement
  - /templates/florida-hoa-fine-hearing-notice
  - /guides/california-hoa-internal-dispute-resolution
