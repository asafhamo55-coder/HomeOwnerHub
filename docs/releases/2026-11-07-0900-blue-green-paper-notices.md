## Summary

HomeownerHub has a new look: blue and green on the website, in the app, in every email, on printed letters and in the welcome video. Boards can now print dues reminders, hearing notices and mailing labels for owners who get their notices on paper. The website has three more Georgia templates.

## What's new

### Everyone
- New colors everywhere: blue for buttons and links, green for things that are done or approved. The logo is now a blue badge, and it stays clean in dark mode.
- The welcome video for residents uses the new colors.

### Boards
- Print mailing labels (standard 30 to a sheet) for owners who get notices on paper. Owners with several homes get one label, and long names are shortened so the city and ZIP always fit.
- Print dues reminders for those same owners: one letter per household with the amount owed, how to pay and how to reach the board. The page tells you if any home that owes money could not get a letter, and why.
- Print a violation hearing notice to mail or hand out. It uses the same wording as the email, lists your board contacts, and will not print until the hearing date, time and place are saved. It warns you if the date is too close for the state's notice period.

### Website
- Three new Georgia templates: a fine hearing notice, a checklist for answering a records request, and a checklist for a request for a statement of amounts due.
