## Summary

The website has three more Florida pages for boards: a lien and foreclosure timeline, a fine cap checklist and an estoppel request checklist. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/florida-hoa-assessment-lien-and-foreclosure-timeline`: Fla. Stat. § 720.3085 in order. Interest, the late fee cap, the order payments are applied, the notice of late assessment before attorney fees, the 45-day demand before a lien and the second 45-day notice before foreclosure. It quotes both ways the statute counts the first 45 days and does not choose between them. It points to the Florida collections letters, and says HomeownerHub does not draft the foreclosure notice or track certified mail for Florida.
- `/templates/florida-hoa-fine-cap-and-daily-fine-checklist`: a printable checklist and fill-in worksheet for § 720.305(2): the fine limit per violation, the daily fine for a continuing violation, the $1,000 aggregate and the hearing steps. It says the quoted text does not explain how the per-violation figure and the daily fine fit together, and tells the board to ask its attorney. It points to the fine hearing record and the State law check.
- `/templates/florida-hoa-estoppel-certificate-request-checklist`: a printable checklist and request log for § 720.30851: the 10 business days, delivery, the 30 or 35 day validity, the fee limits and the late and amended certificate rules. It tells the board to confirm the current fee amounts. It points to the estoppel certificate on a property.

The new pages are listed on the Guides and Templates pages, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K), in the sitemap and on the Florida state page. The Florida notice of intent to lien template, the fine hearing guide and the estoppel guide link to them.

## Under the hood

- Every law quote is checked word for word against the official text.
- The State law check flags a Florida fine over $100 per violation. It does not check the $1,000 aggregate, and the new page does not say it does.
