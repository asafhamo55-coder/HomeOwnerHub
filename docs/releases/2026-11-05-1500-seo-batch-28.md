## Summary

The website has three new Georgia guides for board members. One covers the records a nonprofit HOA must keep and which of them members can inspect. One covers what comes ahead of the assessment lien: property taxes and two kinds of mortgage. One covers what Georgia law says about amending covenants to restrict rentals. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-records-the-association-must-keep-14-3-1601`: permanent records, accounting records, the member list and what members can ask to see.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-lien-priority-taxes-and-mortgages-44-3-232`: the three exceptions to the lien's priority and how foreclosure treats superior liens.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-amendment-rental-restrictions-44-3-226`: the vote, the protection for existing rentals and when a lot must conform.

All three are listed on the Guides page, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K), in the sitemap and on the Georgia page.
