title: Spanish announcements, and California rules checked against the law
---

## Summary

Boards can now add a Spanish version to any announcement in one click. The board reads it, and can edit it, before anything is sent. California fine and hearing rules have been checked word for word against the official state law, and violation letters for California associations are more complete.

## What's new

### Spanish version of announcements
- In **Communications → New**, the Message step has **Add Spanish version**. AI drafts the translation.
- You can edit the Spanish, ask for a new translation, or remove it.
- You must tick **"I've read the Spanish version"** before sending. If you change the English afterwards, you'll be asked to confirm again.
- Residents get the English first, then the Spanish under an **Español** heading.
- A text message includes both languages only when both fit in one message. Otherwise it goes out in English, and the Review step tells you so.

### California violation rules
- Every California fine and hearing rule the app uses was checked against the official Civil Code text, the 2025 version that includes AB 130.
- Hearing notices now list everything the law requires: time, place, what the violation is, the owner's right to speak to the board, and a closed session on request.
- Letters now mention the 14-day written decision and the owner's right to ask for dispute resolution.
- An owner who gives a financial commitment to cure is now protected from a fine, as the law requires.
- California letters no longer tell the board to "verify against the statute".

## Under the hood

- The official California text comes from the state's own bulk download, because its website blocks automated access. That same source will feed the monthly state-law sync.
