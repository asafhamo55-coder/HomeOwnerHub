## Summary

The Events page now opens with four count tiles, so a board can see what is coming and what slipped. The website has a new Texas guide to the ballot rules in Texas Property Code § 209.0058. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, the page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and it says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### Events
- A summary strip above the list shows Next 30 days, Reminders due today, Paused and Past, never reminded. Days are counted in each association's own time zone.
- "Past, never reminded" counts active events whose date has passed and whose reminder was never sent, so a missed reminder is easy to spot.
- If the events cannot be loaded, the tiles say "Not available" instead of 0.

### New page on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/texas-hoa-written-ballots-209-0058`: what § 209.0058 says about written and signed ballots, ballots on other matters, electronic votes, uncontested races and secret ballots with counting observers. The official text prints subsections (a) and (d) twice, once for each of two 2015 amending acts. The page quotes the S.B. 1168 form of each and says it does not decide how the two fit together. It does not cover § 209.00592 (electronic votes), which we do not hold. It says Texas ballots are not built into HomeownerHub.

The new page is listed on the Guides page, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K), in the sitemap and on the Texas state page. The Texas violation notice guide links to it.

## Under the hood

- Every law quote is checked word for word against the official Texas excerpt of chapter 209.
- The Events strip uses the rows the page already loads and adds no queries.
