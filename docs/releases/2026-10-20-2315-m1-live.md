## Summary

The owner applied a database update tonight, so several features that were waiting on it are now switched on. The most important: when an HOA pays for a plan, the plan now applies straight away. Before this update, payments were recorded at Stripe but the app could stay on the free plan.

## What's new

### Billing
- **Paid plans apply correctly:** subscription changes from Stripe (new plans, renewals, upgrades) now save properly. Stripe automatically retries the messages that failed over the last few days, so affected HOAs catch up on their own.

### Documents
- **Show to owners:** besides your CC&Rs, bylaws and rules (which owners always see), you can now share any other document with owners in their portal.

### Florida notices
- **Notice-posting dates:** meeting notices record when they were posted, so the Florida notice checks use real dates.

### Email consent
- **Recorded consent:** owners' consent to receive official notices by email is now recorded. California official notices go by email only to owners who consented.

### Alerts
- **Event alerts that fail to send** are now tracked, so they stop retrying forever.

### Security
- **Revoking an invitation** now only removes that person from your association. It never deletes their account.
- **Inviting owners who already have an account elsewhere** no longer attaches them automatically. They stay on the "without an account" list for now.
- **Billing ignores fake records:** the billing system only trusts records Stripe itself confirms.
- **Opening balances** can only be proposed once per association, so they can never be posted twice.
- **Small fixes:** stricter checks on approvals, hearing requests and "marked done by" names.

## Good to know
- If an HOA paid in the last few days and still shows the free plan tomorrow, tell us. Stripe's retries should fix it within a day.
