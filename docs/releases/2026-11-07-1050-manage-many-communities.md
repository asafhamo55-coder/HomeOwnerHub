---
title: One login for many communities, a "Managed by" line, easier amenity setup and printed meeting notices
---

## Summary

People who serve on several community boards can now reach all of them from one login, with a search box when the list is long. Boards can add a "Managed by" line to their emails and letters, set up amenities on a clear full page, and print a meeting notice to post at the clubhouse.

## What's new

### Boards
- If you belong to more than one community, the community switcher now shows for every role, not only admins. With more than 8 communities it becomes a search box.
- A "Managed by" line, set under Settings, Association, appears at the bottom of your emails, announcements, dues reminders and printed letters. Leave it empty if you manage yourselves.
- Adding or changing an amenity now opens a full page with clear sections instead of a crowded window. Less-used rules sit under "Show more rules".
- Print a meeting notice to post or hand out. You type the start time and place, the page asks you to confirm them, and it warns you if the agenda is missing or the date is too close for your state's notice period.

### Everyone
- Hints and error messages under form fields are a little larger and easier to read.

## Fixes

- A board member of two communities could act on the other community's pending cash receipt while the first one was selected. That is now refused, along with several other places where one community's records could show inside another.
- A voter now always sees their own home on a ballot.

## Under the hood

- The website now tells Bing which pages changed after each update, so new guides are found sooner.
- Scripted emails to the owner check that the logo loads before sending.
