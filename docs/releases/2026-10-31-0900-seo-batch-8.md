## Summary

The website has four new Georgia governance pages for self-managed boards. Each describes only features that exist today, quotes Georgia law only from the checked official text, carries a not-legal-advice note, and ends with "Book a demo".

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-written-ballots-without-a-meeting`: how a Georgia nonprofit HOA can vote by written or electronic ballot instead of a meeting (O.C.G.A. § 14-3-707, numbered § 14-3-708 before 2023). It quotes what the ballot and the mailing must say and when a ballot vote counts. It says plainly that the newer § 14-3-708 on written consent is not in the checked text.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-amend-covenants-two-thirds`: amending the declaration under § 44-3-226: the two-thirds default, the 80 percent cap, rental protection, proof of the vote, recording and the one-year presumption.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-assessment-lien-rules`: the assessment lien under § 44-3-232: what it covers, no separate claim of lien, how long it lasts, and the five-business-day statement of amounts due. It links to the attorney's-fee notice checklist.
- `/templates/hoa-board-conflict-of-interest-disclosure-checklist`: a free, printable checklist for recording each director's conflict disclosure before a bid or vendor decision, with the SB 406 text quoted and each item tagged statute or best practice.

The Georgia hub's lists and related links now include them, and they are in the sitemap. The attorney's-fee notice checklist gains three new inbound links.

## Under the hood

- Every new law quote is checked word for word against the official text, and titles and descriptions pass the weekly SEO audit.
