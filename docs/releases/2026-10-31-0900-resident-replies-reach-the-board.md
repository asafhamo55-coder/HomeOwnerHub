---
title: Boards now hear about resident replies, and residents get clearer messages
---

## What's new

### Tickets
- When a resident replies on a ticket, the board gets a notification in the app's bell. Nothing is sent by email or text.
- The ticket list shows "Resident replied" or "Waiting on resident" on open tickets. A new filter shows only tickets where the resident wrote last.

### Architecture requests and concerns
- When a resident files an architecture request or reports a concern, the board gets a bell notification with a link to it.

### For residents
- If something goes wrong while sending a form, residents now see "Couldn't send. Try again, or message the board." instead of a technical error.
- The documents page links straight to meeting minutes.
- The new ticket form says photos and files can be added on the next screen.

## Fixes

- "Report a concern" no longer cites "Policy §12.03". That number was not in any association's documents. It now says the reporter's identity is kept confidential where possible and the board can see who reported.
