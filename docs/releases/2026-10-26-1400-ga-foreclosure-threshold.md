## Summary

Georgia boards now see, on each open collections case, whether the amount owed is above the foreclosure threshold. The panel shows the rule in force today and the stricter rule that starts on January 1, 2027. It also explains, in plain words, the order in which the new law says payments must be applied. The panel is read-only: it changes nothing about how payments are recorded.

## What's new

### Collections (Georgia)
- A "Foreclosure threshold" panel on the home's collections case, for board members and admins. It shows the balance that counts (regular and special assessments, less unapplied credit), the threshold, and whether the case is above or below it.
- Today's rule is a lien of at least $2,000. From January 1, 2027 the threshold becomes the lesser of $4,000 or 12 months of regular dues, never below $2,000. The 12-month figure comes from your dues-rate schedule.
- A "How payments should be applied" card that quotes SB 406's payment order word for word. HomeownerHub doesn't yet apply payments in this order.
