## Summary

Five tools for running the board well: one list of every task from your meetings, a checklist for when board members change, a snapshot of your reserve fund, a year-end package for your accountant, and a kit for running the annual meeting and reaching quorum.

## What's new

### Board tasks
- **Tasks** (under Community) gathers every action item from your meetings and tickets: Overdue, This week, Later and Done. Assign each to a board member, set a due date and mark it done.
- **"My tasks"** on the dashboard, and each board member's morning email lists their own overdue and upcoming tasks.

### Board handover
- **Settings → Board handover** is a checklist for when board members change: who has access, connections set up by one person (the HOA mailbox, bank connections, Zelle, billing) with links to move them, each person's open work, and a printable summary with no passwords or secrets.

### Reserve snapshot
- **On the dashboard and in the monthly board report:** your reserve balance, contributions this year compared with the budget, interest, and withdrawals. A warning appears if contributions fall more than 10% behind budget.
- **No reserve fund yet?** You'll see "Set up a reserve fund" instead of zeros.

### Year-end package for your accountant
- **Accounting → Reports → Year-end:** pick a fiscal year and download one zip with the statements, the ledger, amounts owed at year end, bank reconciliation status, vendor payments and budget vs actual.
- **Tax helpers:** a Form 1120-H worksheet (anything uncertain is left blank for your accountant) and a list of vendors who may need a 1099. No tax ID numbers are ever included. This is not tax advice, and the app doesn't file the return.

### Annual meeting kit
- **For annual meetings** (and special meetings where owners vote): a live quorum counter, a check-in list, proxy tracking, a list of homes with no proxy yet, a printable sign-in sheet, and printable proxy forms with Florida and California wording where the law is on file.
- **Nothing is sent to residents.** The board follows up itself.
