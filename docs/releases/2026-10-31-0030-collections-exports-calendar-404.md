## Summary

The dues page lists every open collection case, with the ones that have waited longest at the top. A treasurer can download the who-owes list as a spreadsheet for the CPA or attorney. The compliance page can add every upcoming deadline and insurance renewal to your own calendar. Missing pages in the app and on the website now show a friendly page with useful links.

## What's new

### Dues and collections
- "Open collection cases" on /dues shows the unit, stage, days open, days since the last step, the firm and the mail clock. Each row links to the property. Nothing here sends anything or changes a balance.
- "Export CSV" next to the aging chips downloads the who-owes list. It follows the group you picked and shows the same numbers as the screen. Email addresses are not included.

### Compliance
- "Add to my calendar (.ics)" downloads the next 12 months of state and federal deadlines and your insurance renewal dates as all-day events. Importing it again updates the events instead of adding duplicates. Policy numbers are not included.

### People and residents
- The People page shows counts at the top: board members, owners with an account, owners not invited yet, and pending invites.
- A resident's account page starts with their name, home and three chips that show whether email notices, texts and Spanish are turned on.

### Website
- A branded "page not found" page on the website and in the app.
- New pages:
