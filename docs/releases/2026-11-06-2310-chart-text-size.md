## Summary

If you picked Large or Largest text, the numbers and labels on your charts now grow too. The website also has three new guides on Florida budgets and director recall and a Texas owner right.

## What's new

### Boards
- Chart labels on the dashboard and the analytics page follow the Text size setting. Long names are shortened with "..." so the bars stay visible on a phone, and the smallest chart text is now 12px instead of 11px.

### Website
- Three new guides: the Florida budget and reserves rules, recalling a Florida board member, and a Texas owner's justice court claim under Section 209.017. Every quote is checked word for word against the official text.

## Under the hood

- The status ring on the dashboard grows with the text so the total stays inside it.
