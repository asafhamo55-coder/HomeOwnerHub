## Summary

The website has two more templates for boards and a fuller Georgia guide. A California template covers the written decision after a fine hearing: the cure options, the agreement path, dispute resolution and the 14-day notice. A Florida checklist walks a board through a written records request, from the day it arrives to the reply. The Georgia special assessments guide now quotes the statute's definition of an assessment and the lead-in to the condominium limit. Law quotes come only from the checked official text, each page carries a not-legal-advice note and ends with "Book a demo", and each says plainly what HomeownerHub does and does not do.

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/templates/california-hoa-hearing-resolution-letter`: a checklist and a fill-in notice of the board's decision, built on Civ. Code §§ 5850 and 5855. It links to the California fines guide, and the guide links back.
- `/templates/florida-hoa-records-request-response-checklist`: a working checklist and a fill-in reply for a written records request under Fla. Stat. § 720.303(5). It links to the Florida official records guide, and the guide links back.

### Improved page
- `/georgia/special-assessments`: adds the definition of "assessment" (SB 406) and the first line of the condominium limit in § 44-3-80(g).

The pages say HomeownerHub gates the Fined step for California violations on the hearing record. It does not write the decision letter. For Florida records, the app's request tracking uses Georgia's five-business-day rule, so the page tells boards to keep their own 10-business-day calendar.

The new templates are listed on the Templates page, in the search palette (Cmd-K or Ctrl-K) and in the sitemap, and on the Florida and California cards.
