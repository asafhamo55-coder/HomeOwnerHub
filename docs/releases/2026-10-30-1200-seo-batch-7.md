## Summary

The website has three new Georgia pages for self-managed boards. Each describes only features that exist today, quotes Georgia law only from the checked official text, carries a not-legal-advice note, and ends with "Book a demo".

## What's new

### New pages on www.homeownerledger.online
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-overpayments-and-credits`: how a board tracks an owner's overpayment as a credit on account. It says plainly that the Georgia text we hold has no overpayment rule, and quotes the rule that an association may not refuse a payment of any amount. It describes the Credit badge on the Dues list and the Apply credit button.
- `/guides/georgia-hoa-payment-order-sb-406`: the SB 406 payment order (dues, special, specific, then other fees and fines) quoted word for word, with a worked example.
- `/templates/apply-hoa-credit-to-owner-balance-checklist`: a free, printable checklist for applying a credit to a charge, with each item tagged statute or best practice.

The Georgia hub's lists and related links now include them, and they are in the sitemap.
