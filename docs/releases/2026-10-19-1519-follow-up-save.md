---
title: Follow-up letters wait in Approvals only once you save them
---

## Summary

A follow-up violation letter now goes to Approvals only when you select **Save to approvals**. If you close the dialog without saving, the draft isn't kept and won't clutter your queue.

## What's new

- **Save to approvals** is now the only way a follow-up letter reaches Approvals. The dialog says so.
- To keep AI use reasonable, a board member can draft up to 30 follow-up letters an hour, and an association up to 300 a day. Reporting a new violation and inspection mode have no limit.

## Coming soon

- **Meetings and notices in the resident portal:** owners will see upcoming meeting agendas and approved minutes, never drafts. The page is built, but it stays off until a database update is applied. Until then it tells residents that meeting notices aren't in the portal yet.

## Website

- The marketing site now lists Spanish announcements, AI ARC review, inspection mode, follow-up letters and payment plans as available features.
- Figures we can't back up have been removed, including hours saved, conversion rates, competitor prices and uptime claims.
