## Summary

Boards can set a deadline for deciding ARC applications and see at a glance which ones are running late. Two new records help with accountability: an activity log of what board members did, and a motion log that pulls every motion from your meeting minutes into one searchable list.

## What's new

### ARC decision deadline
- **Set your deadline:** under ARC → Decision deadline, choose how many days the board has to decide (every day or business days only). The default is 30 days.
- **Deadline chips:** each application shows how long is left. The chip turns amber within 7 days and red once it's overdue. The "Needs decision" list shows the oldest first.
- **Overdue warning:** an overdue application explains that some declarations treat it as approved if the board doesn't decide in time. Check your declaration.
- **Daily email:** the board's morning email now counts applications that are overdue or due within 3 days.

### Board activity log
- **Settings → Activity log** shows who on the board did what, and when, with filters by person, area and date.
- **What it hides:** residents appear only as "A resident", and free-text details are never shown.
- **Export:** you can download it as a spreadsheet, and each export is itself recorded.

### Motion log
- **Meetings → Motion log** lists every motion from your minutes: what was moved, who moved and seconded it, and the result, with a link to the minutes. You can search it and download it as a spreadsheet.
- **Search:** motions now also appear in ⌘K search.

### Behind the scenes
- **One ordered database checklist** for the owner (owner action OA-1), plus the next database update (0065), tested automatically. It prepares the new pricing and the text-consent guard.
