---
title: Payment receipts for residents, a one-click agenda, and a setup checklist
---

## Summary

Residents can open and print a receipt for any payment, and they can see where their exterior-change request stands. Boards can drop a standard agenda into a meeting with one click, and Settings now shows what still needs setting up. Approvals and dues get clearer at-a-glance numbers.

## What's new

### Resident portal
- Every payment in the ledger has a "Receipt" link. The receipt shows the date, amount, method, the charge it paid and any credit left, and it prints cleanly. Residents only ever see their own home's payments.
- An exterior-change (ARC) request shows a simple timeline: Submitted, In review, then the decision or Withdrawn.

### Meetings
- "Insert standard agenda" on the schedule form fills in call to order, quorum, minutes, reports, old and new business, and adjourn. It can list open action items under old business. Georgia annual meetings get a line for the annual report to the owners.
- The meeting page has two columns. The agenda or minutes come first, with a "Meeting kit" column beside them: notice, quorum, election and annual report.

### Settings
- Settings General shows "Set up your association". There is a card for each area (payments, late fees, reminders, receipts, mailbox, professionals), each saying Set up, Not set up or Needs attention, with a count of how many are done. It also has a Contact support link.

### Approvals and dues
- Approvals shows three numbers at the top: how many are waiting, the oldest wait in days, and how many the AI drafted.
- Each dues row has a clear status: Paid, Due, Late (with days) or Credit. There is a new Owed column, and the header stays in place as you scroll. On phones, the amount owed is shown large.

## Fixes

- Approving several violation letters at once now works in the built app. A packaging problem had kept the update from building.

## Needs your action

- Open a resident's ledger and print a receipt.
- On Settings, check the setup cards match what you have actually configured.
