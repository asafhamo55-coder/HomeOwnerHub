## Summary

The Insurance page now shows how each renewal compares with last year and whether your fidelity bond covers the money the association holds. Owners can also see a plain summary of the association's insurance.

## What's new

### Insurance
- **Renewal comparison:** when a policy renews with the same insurer, the page shows the change in premium, limit and deductible, and highlights anything that got worse.
- **Fidelity bond check:** the bond limit is compared with your reserve and operating cash. Florida associations see the § 720.3033(5) requirement and a warning if the bond falls short.
- **For owners:** a new "Association insurance" page in the portal (from Documents) lists each policy's type, insurer, limit and deductible. Premiums and policy numbers are never shown.

### Georgia
- **Georgia law in our demo screenshots:** our automatic screenshots now include a Georgia association showing the Georgia law pages, so Georgia is tested the way Georgia boards will see it.

## Good to know
- The renewal comparison only matches renewals with the same insurer and a start date entered.
