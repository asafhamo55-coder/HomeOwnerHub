---
title: California boards can record a commitment to cure
---

## Summary

On a California violation, the board can now record that the owner has made a financial commitment to fix it. While the commitment is on file, the app won't let the violation be fined, as California law requires.

## What's new

- **Violation page (California associations only):** a new **Commitment to cure** card. Use **Record commitment** to enter the date and an optional note. Use **Withdraw commitment** to remove it.
- While a commitment is on file, moving the violation to **Fined** is blocked and the app explains why (Civil Code § 5855(c)(2)). Letter drafts for that violation also refuse a fine or suspension.
- Associations in other states don't see this card.

## Under the hood

- Official notices can now be emailed only to members who agreed to electronic delivery, with a list of who needs a paper copy. This stays switched off until the next database update is applied.
