# RLS sweep (migration 0063)

Status: in code on `claude/rls-sweep`, **not yet applied to production**.
Apply with `docs/specs/2026-10-16-rls-sweep-apply.sql`. 0061 must already be
applied, because 0063 uses its `auth_resident_unit_ids()` helper.

Closes BL-007 (`ownerships`/`units`). Lays the RLS groundwork BL-020 needs:
a resident can write their own `resident_communication_preferences` row.
It does not implement BL-020's consent columns or UI.

## Problem (proven)

After 0058 and 0061, 33 public tables still had one of two kinds of policy that
let a plain resident (anon key plus their own session) do more than the app allows:

1. **Blanket org policy**: `FOR ALL USING (org = ANY(auth_org_ids()))` with no
   WITH CHECK. Membership alone gated SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE.
2. **Resident "own row" FOR ALL** on the submission tables. The submitter
   could rewrite the board's side of the record: approve their own ARC
   request, close their own ticket, post as `author_role = 'board'`, or edit
   and delete the board's replies on their thread.

Proven by `tests/integration/security/rls-sweep.test.ts` against the replay
of 0000–0062: **100 failed / 43 passed (143)**. All 43 passes are positive
controls or checks that already held, such as `property_residents` writes,
which 0041 had gated. After 0063: **143 / 143 pass**.

Concretely, a resident could:

- re-point the Zelle recipient in `associations.compliance_settings`. Only the
  app's HMAC signature stood in the way, and that signature stays as
  defence in depth;
- **BL-007**: forge an `ownerships` row, or set `units.legacy_hoa_property_id`
  on a neighbour's unit to a property they are email-linked to. Either one
  widens `auth_resident_unit_ids()` to the neighbour's unit, and through
  0061's resident read path that exposes the unit's dues;
- insert `governing_document_chunks` (and, through
  `/api/governing-docs/upload`, which had no role check, a whole governing
  document). That poisons every neighbour's Ask-the-Docs answers;
- read every neighbour's ownership, tenancy, contact details
  (`property_residents`), violations, collection letters (`communications`,
  `communication_recipients`), push-subscription keys and notifications;
- write or delete `audit_log`, rename the org, change its plan or suspension, or
  delete it (`orgs` had a FOR ALL member policy);
- edit minutes, documents, properties, events, violations, communications and the
  daily digest.

Separately, the `property_bridge_gaps` view (0028) is a plain view owned by
postgres. It bypassed RLS and was granted to `anon`, so the addresses of every
org's unbridged units and properties were readable **without a login**.

## Method

1. `pnpm db:local:reset`, then `pg_policies` for all 95 public tables. All 95
   have RLS enabled.
2. For every swept table: every `.from('<table>')` insert, update, upsert or
   delete under `apps/` and `packages/` was found by script, including the
   `as never` call sites. Each was classified as service role or user
   session, and each user-session writer's role gate was checked.
3. Every resident-portal read (`lib/resident*.ts`, `app/resident/**`,
   `pay-settings.ts`, `/api/ai/ask-docs`) was traced to its exact query
   shape. Each tightened SELECT is tested with that shape.

**RP** = `auth_resident_property_ids()` (new in 0063, C-19). **own** = the caller's own row.

### Deliberately not swept (parked)

- **pm_properties, pm_rent_ledger, eviction_cases**: still org-only FOR ALL.
  PM and eviction onboarding insert `org_members.role = 'owner'`, which
  `auth_is_board_or_admin` does not recognise (and the 0012 CHECK rejects).
  Gating on board/admin could lock real PM or eviction users out of their own
  data. Same reasoning as 0061's `pm_rent_ledger` parking. Revisit when the
  PM/eviction role model is fixed.
- **state_law_updates**: a global table, not per org. INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE are
  allowed for a board/admin of *any* org, and the `state-law.ts` actions have
  no app gate. A resident cannot reach it, but one board can edit what every
  HOA sees. That needs a product decision (platform-admin-only, or per-org
  rows), not a policy tweak.

## Decisions

- **Reads tightened** where the resident portal only ever reads its own rows and
  the org-wide read exposed neighbour PII: ownerships, tenancies,
  property_residents, hoa_violations, communications,
  communication_recipients, notifications, push_subscriptions, audit_log,
  plus six board-internal tables. Every tightened resident read has a
  positive control that uses the app's exact query shape (getOwnedUnits with
  its units join, the email fallback with `ilike`, the dashboard violations
  query, the announcements page's `buildRecipientIdentityFilters` plus the
  communications fetch).
- **Reads left org-wide** (5 SELECT policies): associations (the resident
  Pay screen needs `compliance_settings`), hoa_recurring_events,
  communication_templates, hoa_document_versions (through its document,
  which is type-filtered) and orgs. Everything else is board, own row, or
  resident-visible type (see C-19 below).
- **Email matching uses `auth.jwt() ->> 'email'`**, lower-cased on both
  sides. That is what the app filters on (`actor.email`), and it avoids
  reading `auth.users` from a non-definer policy.
- **associations INSERT/DELETE are admin-only**, UPDATE is board/admin. No
  user-session path creates or deletes an association; board saves the Zelle
  settings and lease cap.
- **orgs**: no user INSERT or DELETE. The old `auth_can_insert_orgs` let any
  signed-in user create orgs, and nothing in the app uses it. Only admins may
  UPDATE.
- **The resident's ARC withdraw** is a second UPDATE policy: USING pending own
  row, CHECK `status = 'withdrawn'` with the board fields still null. The
  resident can still change their own free text in that same UPDATE. That
  is accepted, because it is their content.
- **Resident ticket and ARC inserts require `unit_id` NULL or in RU.** That
  matches the app's own `getResidentUnits()` check (same link rules as 0061)
  and the units FK. Violation reports are *about* other units, so they are
  not constrained.
- **audit_log is append-only for users** and must be written as yourself.

## C-19 amendment: owner contact list and board notes

Finding C-19 (auditor-confirmed): the first cut of 0063 kept `hoa_properties`
and `units` SELECT open to every org member. `hoa_properties` holds
`owner_name`, `owner_email`, `owner_phone` and the board's `notes`; `units`
holds board `notes`. One resident login plus the anon key returned the
whole HOA's owner contact list over PostgREST. 0063 was not yet applied, so
it is amended in place (no 0064).

**Resident read paths traced** (every one still works, each has a
positive control in the test file using the exact query shape):

| Path | Reads | Under the amendment |
|---|---|---|
| `lib/resident.ts` getOwnedUnits, ownership path | `ownerships` → `unit:units(unit_number, address_line1, association_id)` | own unit: in RU (`valid_to IS NULL` on both sides) |
| getOwnedUnits email fallback | `property_residents` → `property:hoa_properties(id, address, unit_number, org_id)`, then `units` by `legacy_hoa_property_id` | own property: in RP (same `moved_out_at IS NULL` + email rule); unit: in RU |
| `lib/resident-dashboard.ts` getOpenViolations | `units.legacy_hoa_property_id` for the caller's unit ids | own units only; `hoa_violations_select` unchanged |
| `/resident/documents` (`lib/resident-documents/query.ts`) | hoa_documents / governing_documents / versions by allowlisted type | RLS now enforces the same allowlist the module applies |
| Pay, ledger, statement | associations, assessments, payments | unchanged (0061 / associations_select) |
| tickets, ARC, violation-report forms | unit picker from getResidentUnits; violation report takes a **free-text** `about_address` | no read of other properties exists, so no "other properties" surface is exposed |
| Ask the Docs (`/api/ai/ask-docs`) | W1 via the service role | unaffected by RLS |
| Ask the Community (`/api/ai/ask-community`, reads owner contacts) | board/admin only (403 for residents) | unaffected |

**Design.** No resident page needs any column of another unit or property,
so there is no SECURITY DEFINER view or function exposing other rows.
The only new function is `auth_resident_property_ids()` (SECURITY DEFINER,
`search_path = public`, EXECUTE revoked from PUBLIC/anon, granted to
authenticated and service_role). Like 0061's `auth_resident_unit_ids()` it
returns only the caller's own ids: `property_residents.property_id` on
their JWT email (not moved out) plus `units.legacy_hoa_property_id` for
units in RU. It keeps the hoa_properties policy independent of
property_residents' and units' own RLS. Policies AND it with org
membership.

**Same treatment elsewhere.** Every other table 0063 left member-readable
was checked for owner PII or board-only material:

- `hoa_meeting_minutes` → **board-only**. Drafts, `raw_transcript`, `attendees`.
  No resident page reads minutes. A future owner-facing minutes page should
  go through a narrow function exposing approved minutes only.
- `hoa_documents` → board, or `type` in ccr/bylaws/rules. "other" is where
  collection letters and legal correspondence are filed (owner PII);
  "minutes" may be drafts. Mirrors `RESIDENT_VISIBLE_TYPES`.
- `governing_documents` → board, or the governing allowlist, current, not
  AI-generated. `governing_document_chunks` follow their document (a chunk
  is the document's parsed text).
- Left member-readable, checked: associations (Zelle block is needed by Pay;
  no PII), hoa_recurring_events, communication_templates, orgs
  (`stripe_customer_id` / `stripe_sub_id` are billing ids, not PII; noted).

**Residual, deliberately not closed here.**

- A resident still reads **their own** property and unit row in full,
  `notes` and `owner_*` included. For a resident linked as a tenant through
  `property_residents`, that includes the landlord's contact and the
  board's notes on that property. The getOwnedUnits email fallback in
  `lib/resident.ts` embeds `hoa_properties`, so the row must stay readable
  until that read moves to a narrow function returning
  `(id, address, unit_number, org_id)`. Then the own-row term can drop.
- Storage: 0055/0056 still let any org member read (and insert) every
  object in `hoa-documents`, `hoa-photos` and `governing-docs`, so a
  resident who learns or lists an object path can still fetch an "other"
  document's file. Needs its own storage-policy change.
- `/api/ai/analyze-violation` has no role gate. Under the amendment a
  resident gets `property_not_found` for anyone else's property, so it no
  longer leaks `owner_name`, but it should be board-gated.
- W1 (Ask the Docs) retrieves governing chunks via the service role with no
  type filter, so minutes or AI drafts in `governing_documents` can surface
  in a resident's answer. That is an app-level filter, not RLS.

## App-layer gates (step 4)

`requireBoardOrAdminForAction(denied?)` in `lib/accounting/guard.ts` now
takes an optional message. The new `BOARD_ONLY_DENIED` reads "Only board members
and admins can make this change." It runs before any DB access and returns an
error; it never redirects. Added to:

- violations.ts: createApprovedViolation, updateViolation, deleteViolation, updateViolationStatus
- meeting-action-items.ts: createActionItem, setActionItemStatus, updateActionItem, deleteActionItem
- meetings.ts: approveMeetingMinutes, scheduleMeeting
- documents.ts: uploadDocument, replaceDocumentFile, restoreDocumentVersion,
  updateParsedText, deleteDocument. The last two keep their existing
  throw-on-error contract, so the gate throws the same message.
- properties/index.ts: createProperty, deleteProperty (deleteProperty throws, same reason)
- property-events.ts: logPropertyEvent. It is an exported `'use server'` function, so it is reachable directly.
- communications: sendCommunication, deleteCommunication
- routes returning 403: `/api/ai/parse-document`, `/api/ai/daily-digest`,
  `/api/governing-docs/upload`

These were already gated and are unchanged: leases, events, property-residents,
tickets.ts, board-review.ts, notifications, push-subscriptions,
pay-settings, vendors' compliance override, resendFailedRecipients, and the inbox
resident actions.

The Zelle settings HMAC signing (`lib/pay-by-zelle.ts`) is unchanged. It stays as
defence in depth now that RLS also refuses resident writes to `associations`.

## Risks

- **A resident read path not found by the inventory** would go empty rather
  than error (RLS filters rows). The residents' pages were traced in full, but
  there is no browser verification (Safari only, no automation). After the
  apply, sign in as a real resident and check `/resident`, dues, the
  announcements list, tickets, ARC and the violation report screens.
- **The JWT email claim** is what scopes `property_residents` and recipient
  reads. If a user changes their email, the claim follows on the next token
  refresh. That matches what the app itself filters on.
- **PM/eviction tables are still open** to any member (parked above).
- `auth_owner_unit_ids()` (0012, unused by any policy) is also derived from
  ownerships. It is no longer forgeable now that ownership writes are board-only.
