## 5. Onboarding a brand-new HOA: where a volunteer gets stuck

**The flow:**
1. `/signup`.
2. `/onboarding`: name and door count. `onboarding/actions.ts` creates `orgs` and `org_members` but **no `associations` row**.
3. `/onboarding/setup`: 4 steps.
4. The dashboard "Get your HOA set up" checklist, 6 steps (`lib/dashboard/setup-checklist.ts`), plus the FirstWeekCard (`lib/guide/first-week.ts`).

**1. Homes added before the association exists are never billed (correctness bug; verified in code).**
- With no association, `chooseAssociation` returns `associationId: null` (`apps/hoa/src/lib/properties/lot.ts:102`).
- `/properties/new` and `/properties/import` then write `units.association_id = NULL`. The column is nullable (`database.types.ts`, `units.Row`).
- The association is created later from /dues by `setUpAssociationCore` (`lib/association-setup.ts`), which never touches `units`.
- Dues generation filters `.eq('association_id', assoc.id)` (`lib/assessments.ts:290, 296, 407`), so those homes are silently skipped.
- Both checklists lead users into exactly this order: properties before dues.
- The guide promises the opposite. `setup-properties-residents.md` says "The home is ready to bill straight away", and `start-board-admin.md` says "Each home you add can be billed straight away." → **BL-125.**

**2. Opening balances can't be entered.**
- The import screen says "enter opening balances in Accounting" (`properties/import/RosterImportSteps.tsx:480`), and so does `setup-import-roster.md`.
- But accounting has no manual or opening journal-entry screen. The ledger is view, reverse and promote-to-recurring only.
- "Open the books" (`lib/accounting/books-not-open.ts`) only creates funds, the chart of accounts and the period.
- Owner arrears can only be entered as a one-off charge per home. Cash and reserve opening balances can't be entered at all. → **BL-126.**

**3. The guide contradicts the UI.**
- `start-board-admin.md` says "Have a spreadsheet of homes? Contact support … and we'll import it", but a self-serve `/properties/import` exists.
- It also says the step adds "homes one at a time".
- The guide describes "The six steps", but `/onboarding/setup` shows 4. → **BL-127.**

**4. "Invite the board" is ticked by inviting a resident.** Both checklists count every `org_members` row, so 2 rows of any role count as done. → **BL-127.**

**5. Real setup steps appear in neither checklist:**
- create the association and open the books;
- set up Zelle (`/settings/payments`; until then residents see "Online payments aren't set up yet");
- connect the bank;
- set the budget;
- set dues reminders;
- collect electronic-notice consent.

→ **BL-127.**

**6. No bulk invites.**
- The import records owners but invites nobody. Invites go out one at a time.
- `members-invite-board.md` admits: "There is no list of pending invitations, and invitations can't be resent." → **BL-128.**

**7. Dead ends with workarounds that need keys or a migration (below the line):**
- **Bank:** Plaid only. `bank_accounts.plaid_*` are nullable, so a manual account is possible, but there is no statement CSV import.
- **Mailbox:** Google only. Outlook and Microsoft 365 boards can't finish that checklist step, and the card can't be dismissed. This needs an Azure app (keys).
- **Dues:** generation uses one flat amount for every home. `units` has no share or rate column, so this needs a migration.

#### #6 · BL-130 Statute-drift guard on fines and collection letters
- **Pain:** N1 and N7. Fines and collection fees collapse because a newer statute overrides the board's rules or notice.
- **Build:**
  - A pure per-state check that runs before a fine or collection letter is approved. It returns blocking or warning findings with the citation:
