## 3. First 30 days and budget season: what the statutes in our fixtures require

**Florida (`flsenate-ch720-2026.html`)**
- § 720.303(6)(a) **(fixture):** "The association shall prepare an annual budget… The association shall provide each member with a copy of the annual budget or a written notice that a copy of the budget is available upon request at no charge." The budget notice is already built.
- § 720.303(2)(c)2 **(fixture):** "An assessment may not be levied at a board meeting unless the notice of the meeting includes a statement that assessments will be considered and the nature of the assessments. Written notice of any meeting at which special assessments will be considered … must be mailed, delivered, or electronically transmitted … and posted conspicuously on the property … not less than 14 days before the meeting." Not checked by `notice-law.ts` today.
- § 720.306(9)(a) **(fixture):** "An election is not required unless more candidates are nominated than vacancies exist… Any challenge to the election process must be commenced within 60 days after the election results are announced."
- § 720.306(9)(b) **(fixture):** "A person who is delinquent in the payment of any fee, fine, or other monetary obligation … on the day that he or she could last nominate himself or herself … may not seek election to the board". A director "more than 90 days delinquent … shall be deemed to have abandoned his or her seat". We have the ledger, so the product can check both.
- § 720.3033(1)(a) **(fixture):** "Within 90 days after being elected or appointed to the board, each director must submit a certificate of having satisfactorily completed the educational curriculum…" The certificate "is valid for up to 4 years". Fewer than 2,500 parcels: "at least 4 hours of continuing education annually".

**California (`ca-civ-davis-stirling-2026.txt`)**
- § 5300(a) **(fixture):** "an association shall distribute an annual budget report 30 to 90 days before the end of its fiscal year." For a calendar-year association that window is **2026-10-02 to 2026-12-01, and it is open now.**
- § 5100(a)(2) **(fixture):** an election for a board seat "at the expiration of the corresponding director's term and at least once every four years".
- § 5115 **(fixture):**
  - (a) general notice of the nomination procedure and deadline "at least 30 days before any deadline for submitting a nomination";
  - (b) general notice of the return deadline and address, the counting meeting and the candidate list "at least 30 days before the ballots are distributed";
  - (c) ballots and two preaddressed envelopes "not less than 30 days prior to the deadline for voting";
  - (d)(2)–(3) a reconvened meeting at least 20 days later with a 20% quorum, noticed at least 15 days ahead.
- § 5120 **(fixture):** votes are counted in public by the inspector. (b) "Within 15 days of the election, the board shall give general notice … of the tabulated results".
- § 5125 **(fixture):** the inspector keeps custody of ballots until the § 5145 challenge period ends.
- **Not in fixtures:** §§ 5605, 5610 and 5615 (assessment increase caps and notice), § 5806 (fidelity), and § 5551. Load them from the same `pubinfo_2025.zip` range reads (BL-069 adapter) **before** BL-137 or BL-142 quote them.

**Texas:** only §§ 207.*, 209.004, 209.0062–.0064 are in fixtures. Load §§ 209.0051 (open meetings and notice) and 209.00592–.00594 (elections) before quoting. Until then the product says "check your bylaws".

**Georgia:** § 44-3-230 (meeting frequency and notice) is in the fixture, so check it before quoting. BL-124 (refresh the snapshot before 2027-01-01) still needs scheduling for mid-December.
